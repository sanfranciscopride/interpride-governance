Introduction

Thank you for your interest in InterPride’s Solidarity Fund grant program. Our grant-making is competitive because we are unable to fund all of the applications that we receive. Submitting an application does not guarantee that your project will be funded. Before you apply, please review all the guidelines. It is important to learn about changes and requirements for the application process. The grant application is located and completed online at Solidarity Fund Application Form. For more information see the Frequently Asked Questions (“FAQs”) section.

InterPride Global Grant Fund Programs (IGGFP) InterPride offers three (3) grant funding programs focused on two categories - organizational support funds and individual support funds. Organizations may apply for the Solidarity Fund and/or the Humanitarian Fund. Individuals may apply for the Scholarship Fund. (See the FAQs for an explanation of these Funds). All funds are allocated on the basis of need.

About InterPride Vision To achieve legal, social, and cultural equity for the SOGIESC (diverse sexual orientations, gender identities and expressions, and sex characteristics) community through the Pride movement.

Mission
  - To contribute to the visibility and inclusion of SOGIESC communities.
  - To connect, grow, and support Pride Platforms and networks globally.
  - To assist the Pride movement to design and conduct human rights events that deconstruct myths, lies, and prejudices surrounding SOGIESC realities.
  - To maximize networking and communication across the global Pride movement, offer resources, grow and leverage partnerships.

Values Trust, Respect, Accountability, Diversity, Multiculturalism, Kindness, Bravery

Role InterPride is the international organization that advances the Pride movement by coordinating with global partners that share our values. We are uniquely suited to carry out our work because InterPride:
  - Works with and supports the visibility and development of Pride networks around the world, thereby contributing to the presence and influence of Pride efforts at regional and local levels.
  - Supports the production of Pride events for the global SOGIESC community.
  - Manages, supports, and awards WorldPride™, global grant programs, and related initiatives. Founded in 1982, InterPride is a not-for-profit membership-based organization with more than 300 members from over 60 countries. Sources of income include membership dues, licensing fees, sponsorships, donations, and merchandise sales. We partner with other international, regional, and national LGBTQIA+ (Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, Transgender, Queer, Intersex, Asexual+) associations and human rights organizations to facilitate social change for LGBTQIA+ people around the world. Each year, InterPride, its partners, and its members engage millions of people through in-person and virtual events, mainstream media, and social media.

About Pride
  - InterPride defines Pride as any public or semi-public gathering led by and for people from SOGIESC communities that promotes visibility and inclusion.
  - Such gatherings take many forms including but not limited to a parade, march, rally, festival, as well as events reflecting varying cultural practices such as pageants, traditional dance, ceremonies, and story-telling.
  - The nature of Pride gatherings is influenced by political, social, and cultural contexts including constraints imposed by at-risk environments.
  - A Pride organization is one that produces a Pride event. An organization may be described as Pride-like where it does not produce a Pride event as described above but aspires to do so. The inability to produce a Pride event may be due to factors such as the prohibition against public assembly, politically hostile environments, lack of funding, or organizational capacity.

Solidarity Fund Overview InterPride is committed to supporting the evolving needs of our LGBTQIA+ community. The Solidarity Fund provides grants to LGBTQIA+ Pride or Pride-like organizations around the world for community-based Pride events, with a focus on Global South and Eastern Europe, where public LGBTQIA+ activities often face opposition or hostility. Grants are not awarded to individuals.

Grant Amount Under the Solidarity Fund, InterPride awards grants up to a maximum award of USD $2,000. Note that the maximum grant is rarely awarded to enable the total amount of funding available to be distributed as widely as possible.

Grant Categories InterPride awards Solidarity Fund grants for purposes such as:
  - educational programing
  - events for social or human rights purposes
  - film festivals
  - arts, cultural or history projects
  - community development projects
  - conferences and meetings Eligible uses associated with these purposes include:
  - registration fees and permits
  - venue hire
  - speaker fees
  - fees for performers and entertainers

  - printing
  - transportation
  - catering
  - branding and promotion
  - film screening fees
  - audio visual hire Solidarity Funds are not available for annual operating costs such as salaries, office rent, office equipment, and furniture.

Target Populations InterPride awards grant funding to LGBTQIA+ Pride and Pride-like organizations in developing and/or hostile locations that have a focus on the following (but not necessarily limited to) LGBTQIA+ communities:
  - Ethnic minorities
  - Indigenous or 2Spirit
  - People with disabilities
  - Black and people of color
  - Refugees and asylum seekers
  - Trans, non-binary and gender diverse
  - Female identified
  - Sex workers
  - Other demographic groups subject to oppression and/or discrimination based on their sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, or sex characteristics

Eligibility Criteria
  - The applicant is a Pride or Pride-like organization led by and directly involving LGBTQIA+ individuals, verified by the relevant Global Advisory Council (GAC) representative from the region the application is submitted from and at least one (1) other Grants Committee member who is not from the applicant’s region. Verification may involve research into the applicant’s history and operations and/or confirmation of submitted information from independent third parties.
  - The application is for a purpose and use consistent with the Grant Categories.
  - The application is for one or more of the Target Populations.
  - The applicant has not received funding from InterPride in the past two years.
  - Funding sought is not more than USD$2000. Note: If an organization received grant funding from InterPride in the past two years, they are considered ineligible for funding under this program. The organization may reapply for funding from two years after the last grant was awarded. This policy aims to ensure that there are sufficient funds available to support equitable distribution of grants globally.

Exclusions Grants are not awarded to:
  - Individuals
  - Paying debts
  - For-profit companies
  - Government organizations
  - Endowments or capital campaigns
  - Organizations that are less than one (1) year old unless the application is for their first Pride event.
  - Organizations that do not align with our mission, vision, values, or ethics policy
  - Organizations that discriminate based on race, ethnicity, creed, religious affiliation, sexual orientation, gender identity, or national origin
  - Activities, projects, or programs that will be completed before funding becomes available (i.e., no retrospective funding)

Application Submission Applications may be submitted to the Grants Committee (Committee) at any time throughout the year using the form by clicking here. If you are unable to access the form, please contact grants@interpride.org for assistance. The steps from application submission are set out in Appendix A. In addition to other specified information, the application form requires the applicant to provide:
  - Organization name and address
  - Website, and/or social media links. InterPride recognizes that a website or social media presence may be restricted or prohibited in some locations.
  - Primary contact person (Name, Pronouns, Email, Phone)
  - Alternate contact person (Name, Pronouns, Email, Phone)
  - Year the organization was established
  - Description of organization purpose
  - Description of organization activities
  - Number of organization members
  - Location of the project for which funding is being sought
  - An itemized budget up to a maximum request of USD$2000. Budget requests above the maximum amount will not be considered. The submitted budget can be in an excel spreadsheet or a table format.
  - A description of how funds will be used
  - A description of who will benefit from the project
  - Banking or financial institution for deposit information, should the application be successful. InterPride undertakes to store this information securely.

  - Agreement to supply photographs, images, or video (Appendix B) where such material does not pose a safety or security risk to individuals, should the application be successful.
  - Agreement to the Brand Recognition requirements (Appendix C), should the application be successful. InterPride recognizes that an online presence may be restricted or prohibited in some locations.
  - Agreement to submit the required post-event Impact Report, should the application be successful, no more than thirty (30) days after the project has been completed. Grantees can submit the impact report here.
  - The applicant has confirmed that grant funds will be used within thirty (30) days of receipt. Applications must be fully completed to be considered. Submission of applications will be acknowledged within 48 hours of receipt.

Application Assessment While the Committee will make every effort to expedite the assessment of applications, a period of sixty (60) days from submission should be allowed for notification of the outcome of the application. Each review panel consists of volunteers from several InterPride regions who score each application based on the assessment criteria.

Assessment Criteria Factors that are taken into consideration in application assessment include:
  - Does the proposal align with InterPride’s mission and values?
  - Is the project well-described?
  - Are the desired outcomes clear?
  - Is there a vital need for the project?
  - What benefits will the project deliver to the local community?
  - Will the project enable capacity building and/or support community-driven change?
  - Does the applicant demonstrate the capacity to successfully deliver the project based on the quality of the proposal and past impact/success?
  - Does the applicant demonstrate sound financial management practices?
  - Has the applicant demonstrated the ability to report on success in achieving the project goals

Notification of Decision All applicants will be notified in writing of the outcome of their application within 48 hours of the decision being made.   - The number of individuals that benefited from the project.
  - What outcomes the project delivered.

Management and Approval The Grants Committee will manage the application and assessment process, including:
  - Ensuring the availability and eligibility for funding are promoted through InterPride and other platforms
  - Assessing applications for funding
  - Making recommendations on approval of grants to monthly meetings of the InterPride Board
  - Maintaining a database of applications
  - Providing reports on the allocation of grants for inclusion in InterPride’s annual report and social media.
  - Communicating with grant applicants, including notifying successful applicants
  - Ensuring grant recipients provide a post-event Impact Report on the project funded - a link to the Impact Report submission form will be included in notification emails.

Scholarship Fund Overview InterPride is committed to supporting the evolving needs of our LGBTQIA+ community. The Scholarship Fund provides grants to representatives of InterPride Full Member organizations to enable them to attend the InterPride Annual General Meeting & World Conference (GM&WC). Under the Scholarship Fund, the total number and value of grants approved is subject to funding availability. Funds for the Scholarship Fund are donated by our member organizations, partners, and sponsors.

Grant Amount The Scholarship Fund provides reimbursement of up to seventy percent (70%) of the costs incurred to attend the GM&WC. In some cases, such as where an applicant organization is located in a country eligible for Official Development Assistance (as defined by the OECD), up to one-hundred percent (100%) reimbursement of costs may be made. The proportion of costs reimbursed to the applicant organization is at the discretion of the Scholarship Committee.

Grant Purpose InterPride awards Scholarship Fund grants for the following costs:
  - Conference registration
  - Accommodations
  - Transportation
  - Meals (that are not included in the Conference registration fee)

Additional funding requests are considered on a case-by-case basis.

Target Populations InterPride awards grant funding to up to two nominated delegate(s) of Full Member Pride organizations.

Eligibility Criteria
  - The applicant is a Full Member Pride organization. The name of the applicant organization must match the name of the member organization.
  - The applicant has paid its membership fee for the current year.
  - The applicant has demonstrated compliance with InterPride’s values, code of ethics and conduct, bylaws and standing rules.
  - The application is for not more than two representatives.
  - The applicant organization has not been a recipient of the Scholarship Fund more than twice previously.
  - The application is limited to the costs of attendance at the GM&WC.

Exclusions Grants are not awarded to:
  - Non-member Pride delegates
  - Paying debts
  - For-profit companies
  - Government organizations
  - Endowments or capital acquisitions
  - Grants are not generally awarded to the same delegate for more than two consecutive years
  - Organizations that do not align with our mission, vision, values, or ethics policy
  - Organizations that discriminate based on race, ethnicity, creed, religious affiliation, sexual orientation, gender identity, or national origin

Scholarship Conditions
  - **1.** The Scholarship Fund provides reimbursement of costs expended by the applicant organization for attendance of their representative(s) at the GM&WC including conference registration, accommodation, transportation and meals (that are not included in the conference fee).

  - **2.** Funds to meet the costs of attendance at the GM&WC are not generally paid in advance. In some cases however, such as where an applicant organization is located in a country eligible for Official Development Assistance (as defined by the OECD), up to fifty percent (50%) of costs may be paid in advance. Payment of funds in advance is at the discretion of the Scholarship Committee.
  - **3.** Where funds are paid in advance of attendance and the applicant organization’s delegate(s) fail to attend the GM&WC, the applicant organization must repay InterPride the total amount of funds paid in advance within fourteen (14) days. Failure to repay funds in these circumstances will render the applicant organization ineligible for any future funding from the Scholarship Fund or any other grants programs.
  - **4.** All expenses submitted for reimbursement must include receipts. Introduction

Thank you for your interest in InterPride’s Humanitarian Aid Fund grant program. Our grant-making is competitive because we are unable to fund all of the applications that we receive. InterPride Global Grant Fund Programs (IGGFP) InterPride offers three (3) grant funding programs focused on two categories - organizational support funds and individual support funds. Organizations may apply for the Solidarity Fund and/or the Humanitarian Fund. Individuals may apply for the Scholarship Fund. (See the FAQs for an explanation of these Funds). All funds are allocated on the basis of need.

About InterPride Vision To achieve legal, social, and cultural equity for the SOGIESC (diverse sexual orientations, gender identities and expressions, and sex characteristics) community through the Pride movement.

Mission
  - To contribute to the visibility and inclusion of SOGIESC communities.
  - To connect, grow, and support Pride Platforms and networks globally.
  - To assist the Pride movement to design and conduct human rights events that deconstruct myths, lies, and prejudices surrounding SOGIESC realities.
  - To maximize networking and communication across the global Pride movement, offer resources, grow and leverage partnerships.

Values Trust, Respect, Accountability, Diversity, Multiculturalism, Kindness, Bravery

Role InterPride is the international organization that advances the Pride movement by coordinating with global partners that share our values. We are uniquely suited to carry out our work because InterPride:
  - Works with and supports the visibility and development of Pride networks around the world, thereby contributing to the presence and influence of Pride efforts at regional and local levels.
  - Supports the production of Pride events for the global SOGIESC community.
  - Manages, supports, and awards WorldPride™, global grant programs, and related initiatives. Founded in 1982, InterPride is a not-for-profit membership-based organization with more than 300 members from over 60 countries. Sources of income include membership dues, licensing fees, sponsorships, donations, and merchandise sales. We partner with other international, regional, and national LGBTQIA+ (Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, Transgender, Queer, Intersex, Asexual+) associations and human rights organizations to facilitate social change for LGBTQIA+ people around the world. Each year, InterPride, its partners, and its members engage millions of people through in-person and virtual events, mainstream media, and social media.

About Pride
  - InterPride defines Pride as any public or semi-public gathering led by and for people from SOGIESC communities that promotes visibility and inclusion.
  - Such gatherings take many forms including but not limited to a parade, march, rally, festival, as well as events reflecting varying cultural practices such as pageants, traditional dance, ceremonies, and story-telling.
  - The nature of Pride gatherings is influenced by political, social, and cultural contexts including constraints imposed by at-risk environments.
  - A Pride organization is one that produces a Pride event. An organization may be described as Pride-like where it does not produce a Pride event as described above but aspires to do so. The inability to produce a Pride event may be due to factors such as the prohibition against public assembly, politically hostile environments, lack of funding, or organizational capacity.

Humanitarian Aid Fund Overview InterPride is committed to supporting the evolving needs of our LGBTQIA+ community. The Humanitarian Aid Fund provides grants for essential needs, combating racism and facility development to LGBTQIA+ Pride or Pride-like organizations around the world. Grants are not awarded to individuals.

Grant Amount Under the Humanitarian Aid Fund, InterPride awards grants between USD $100 and a maximum award of USD $1,000.

Grant Categories InterPride awards Humanitarian Aid Fund grants for:
  - **1.** Essential needs, or
  - **2.** Combating racism, or
  - **3.** Facility development Applicants should identify the closest category to the grant application being made.

Essential Needs Supporting LGBTQIA+ organizations with funds to cover essential needs such as purchasing life-saving medical equipment, short-term rent or shelter for those facing eviction or who are homeless, blankets, food, water, hygiene needs, electricity, medications, internet, evacuation, or funeral expenses.

Combating Racism Supporting activities that act to combat the effects of racism on LGBTQI+ black and people of color populations including increasing access to essential services, providing safe and inclusive spaces, building skills and capacity, and strengthening resilience.

Facility Development Supporting LGBTQIA+ organizations with constructing a new facility where a previous one was impacted by damage from a natural disaster or civil unrest; or there is a need to repair or improve an existing space to accommodate more LGBTQIA+ members in an emergency situation.

Target Populations InterPride awards grant funding to LGBTQIA+ Pride and Pride-like organizations in developing and/or hostile locations that have a focus on the following (but not necessarily limited to) LGBTQIA+ communities:
  - Ethnic minorities
  - Indigenous or 2Spirit
  - People with disabilities
  - Black and people of color
  - Refugees and asylum seekers
  - Trans, non-binary and gender diverse
  - Female identified
  - Sex workers
  - Other demographic groups subject to oppression and/or discrimination based on their sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, or sex characteristics

Eligibility Criteria
  - The applicant is a Pride or Pride-like organization led by and directly involving LGBTQIA+ individuals, verified by the relevant Global Advisory Council (GAC) representative from the region the application is submitted from and at least one (1) other Grants Committee member who is not from the applicant’s region. Verification may involve research into the applicant’s history and operations and/or confirmation of submitted information from independent third parties.
  - The application is for a purpose and use consistent with the Grant Categories.
  - The application is for one or more of the Target Population.

  - The applicant has not received funding from InterPride in the past two years.
  - Funding sought is not more than USD$1000. Note: If an organization received grant funding from InterPride in the past two years, they are considered ineligible for funding under this program. The organization may reapply for funding from two years after the last grant was awarded. This policy aims to ensure that there are sufficient funds available to support equitable distribution of grants globally.

Exclusions Grants are not awarded to:
  - Individuals
  - Paying debts
  - For-profit companies
  - Government organizations
  - Political causes or candidates
  - Endowments or capital campaigns
  - Organizations less than one (1) year old unless the organization has an established structure such as an elected Board.
  - Conferences, symposia, or related travel
  - Advertising or promotion
  - Events, protests, education, community development, arts, cultural, or history projects
  - Grants are not usually awarded to the same applicant more than once.
  - Organizations that do not align with our mission, vision, values, or ethics policy
  - Organizations that discriminate based on race, ethnicity, creed, religious affiliation, sexual orientation, gender identity, or national origin
  - Activities, projects, or programs that will be completed before funding becomes available (i.e., no retrospective funding) Note that conferences, events, educational programs and arts, culture, or history projects may be eligible for funding under the Solidarity Fund Policy .

About InterPride Vision To achieve legal, social, and cultural equity for the SOGIESC (diverse sexual orientations, gender identities and expressions, and sex characteristics) community through the Pride movement.

Mission
  - To contribute to the visibility and inclusion of SOGIESC communities.
  - To connect, grow, and support Pride Platforms and networks globally.
  - To assist the Pride movement to design and conduct human rights events that deconstruct myths, lies, and prejudices surrounding SOGIESC realities.
  - To maximize networking and communication across the global Pride movement, offer resources, grow and leverage partnerships.

Values Trust, Respect, Accountability, Diversity, Multiculturalism, Kindness, Bravery

Role InterPride is the international organization that advances the Pride movement by coordinating with global partners that share our values. We are uniquely suited to carry out our work because InterPride:
  - Works with and supports the visibility and development of Pride networks around the world, thereby contributing to the presence and influence of Pride efforts at regional and local levels.
  - Supports the production of Pride events for the global SOGIESC community.
  - Manages, supports, and awards WorldPride™, global grant programs, and related initiatives. Founded in 1982, InterPride is a not-for-profit membership-based organization with more than 400 members from over 60 countries. Sources of income include membership dues, licensing fees, sponsorships, donations, and merchandise sales. We partner with other international, regional, and national LGBTQIA+ (Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, Transgender, Queer, Intersex, Asexual+) associations and human rights organizations to facilitate social change for LGBTQIA+ people around the world. Each year, InterPride, its partners, and its members engage millions of people through in-person and virtual events, mainstream media, and social media.

About Pride
  - InterPride defines Pride as any public or semi-public gathering led by and for people from SOGIESC communities that promotes visibility and inclusion.

  - Such gatherings take many forms including but not limited to a parade, march, rally, festival, as well as events reflecting varying cultural practices such as pageants, traditional dance, ceremonies, and story-telling.
  - The nature of Pride gatherings is influenced by political, social, and cultural contexts including constraints imposed by at-risk environments.
  - A Pride organization is one that produces a Pride event. An organization may be described as Pride-like where it does not produce a Pride event as described above but aspires to do so. The inability to produce a Pride event may be due to factors such as the prohibition against public assembly, politically hostile environments, lack of funding, or organizational capacity.

Opportunities And Benefits InterPride MBPP participants are afforded a number of benefits to help elevate their brand. Some of these benefits include:
  - Potential for market penetration and business growth.
  - Marketing and communication access to a captive base of target customers of Pride organizations around the world. There are an estimated 1,500 to 2,000 Pride organizations around the globe, and collectively they mobilize millions of individuals annually in communities across the globe. This can be achieved by listing the partner on our marketing materials, communication channels, live stream sessions, and newsletters.
  - Endorsement from InterPride, an association with a strong reputation and established team of Pride organizers.
  - Credibility to the products/services offered and increased trust in the supplier via InterPride's endorsement.
  - Increased visibility through InterPride's promotions to members, prospective members, and the larger business community.
  - Logo on our websites (Main, Member, Solo) that reach members throughout the year.

Eligibility Criteria
  - Organizations that publicly states support for LGBTQIA+ rights with a statement or policy on their respective website.
  - Opportunities for increased visibility of InterPride and member organizations.
  - Prior experience with the LGBTQIA+ community and Pride organizers highly preferred.
  - Meets the criteria under our Partnership & Sponsorship Ethics Policy ( Appendix C) and agrees to the requirements.

MBPP Requirements Any potential MBP requires a tangible, relevant, and eye-catching value. Proposed benefits cannot simply be benefits that members are able to access elseware.

Otherwise, they're neither meaningful nor effective as a member benefit for InterPride. InterPride has developed the following criteria for potential MBPP’s:

Relevance The products, services, or promotions must be highly relevant to festival and event production and non-profit organization management. InterPride will only consider products/services from vendors that demonstrate a proven value with unique benefits in terms of price, product, or service that members would not easily obtain elsewhere in the marketplace.

Membership Value The partnership should be focused on 'exclusive' benefits that are considered highly valuable by InterPride members and available only through membership with us.

Revenue Potential Sponsorship, advertising, and revenue-sharing opportunities are welcome and are expected in any proposal. Revenue-sharing opportunities created through carefully chosen partnerships can enhance InterPride's ability to generate additional revenue while providing value for members.

Term of Agreement MBP terms of agreements range from two (2) to five (5) years. InterPride assess the MBP’s every twelve (12) months.

MBP Marketing To ensure the growth and success of the program, InterPride and the partner will commit to a proposed marketing plan to create awareness of the offering and partnership. Ongoing marketing of the program/service/product will be established for the duration of the agreement. The plan will vary with each partner, but the channels and opportunities available include:
  - Stand-alone email blasts
  - Mentions in member newsletters
  - Online logo placement
  - Social media posts
  - Opportunities to share educational content via presentation or in writing
  - Sponsorship of conferences
  - Virtual events
  - Networking events

Reporting The partner must have the capability to monitor program participation by InterPride members and to report regularly to InterPride on program activity and growth.

Supplier Exclusivity There may be instances where InterPride will provide an MBP with supplier exclusivity, meaning that InterPride will not engage others in the same field (parameters to be determined in the contract) in similar MBP’s. InterPride will not, however, agree to exclusivity in terms of general advertising sales, membership, or involvement with competitors for purposes beyond an MBP.

Appendix C. Partnership & Sponsorship Ethics Policy InterPride is the international organization that ties Pride together globally. Members of our organization are dedicated volunteers who organize and work to put on Pride events worldwide. We value the active participation of partners and sponsors who provide essential support, resources, and finances to enable us to deliver our events and services.

InterPride exists to promote LGBTQIA+ Pride on an international level, increase networking and communication among Pride Organizations, and encourage diverse communities to hold and attend Pride events and act as a source of education. InterPride is a voice for the LGBTQIA+ community around the world. We stand up for inequality and fight injustice everywhere. Partners and sponsors do not influence the policy or operation of InterPride. Given our commitment to transparency, all partners and sponsors are listed in full on our website. InterPride will not enter into partnership or sponsorship arrangements with companies who:
  - Manufacture or promote tobacco products.
  - Manufacture or sell weapons or arms.
  - Promote alcohol or drugs (including marijuana) in a way that does not meet standards set by local government in relation to consumption, advertising, marketing, and age of sale.
  - Manufacture products in countries where laws criminalize LGBTQIA+ individuals unless the company has a stated policy and provides support to LGBTQIA+ advocacy organizations in that country and/or internationally.
  - Financially, verbally or in any other way, support regimes or organizations that violate human rights.
  - Directly or indirectly violate international treaties on Human Rights, including the Universal Declaration of Human Rights.
  - Directly or indirectly violate recognized international treaties or standards on the protection of the environment.
  - Engage in unethical profiteering schemes or lending practices that target those with a low socioeconomic status.
  - This is not an exhaustive list, and an assessment will be made on each potential partner or sponsor, including: ○ Listed on various LGBTQIA+ Equality Indexes such as that by the Human Rights Campaign (HRC), Stonewall, RainbowTick, or others ○ Signatory of any national or international LGBTQIA+ business practice protocols (i.e., UN STANDARDS OF CONDUCT FOR BUSINESS: Tackling Discrimination against Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, Transgender, & Intersex People) ○ LGBTQIA+ led or have an LGBTQIA+ Employee Resource Group (ERG)

○ Media analysis of human rights, LGBTQIA+, environmental and other coverage (positive, negative, neutral) ○ Review of any LGBTQIA+ policies ○ Review of any environmental policies (carbon footprint declaration, offsetting promises, monitoring, etc.) ○ Overall alignment with the mission of InterPride - a good fit with InterPride’s image ○ Does the partnership or sponsorship conflict with InterPride’s values? ○ Does the partnership or sponsorship suit our overall strategy? ○ Will this partnership or sponsorship help us realize our short and long-term objectives? This policy will be reviewed annually. For further information on becoming a partner or sponsor of InterPride, please email info@interpride.org. By submitting this application, the applicant acknowledges that they have read and fully understood the Partnership & Sponsorship Ethics Policy and agree to be bound by them.
