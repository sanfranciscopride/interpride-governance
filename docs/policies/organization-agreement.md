## Governance Overview

Under [`SR 01.11 Section 2.B.1`](../standing-rules/sr-01-11.html), the Governance Committee is responsible for administering and tracking compliance with the **Board Member, GAC, and Committee Co-Chairs Agreement**. In 2026, this agreement is executed digitally via DocuSign by all Board Directors, GAC Representatives, Committee/Subcommittee/Task Force Chairs, and Governance Committee members.

{: .important }
> **Key Enforceable Commitments in the 2026 Agreement:**
> - **Active Signal Account Required:** Signatories must confirm an active Signal account tied to their mobile number.
> - **Conflict of Interest Disclosure:** Any actual or perceived conflict must be disclosed immediately to the **Governance Chairs and Co-Presidents** (`SR 08.10`). Failure to disclose triggers a Governance review and potential removal.
> - **Strict Voting & Contributor Confidentiality:** All information regarding votes on motions, decisions, or elections—and names of non-public financial contributors—is strictly confidential. Breaches must be self-reported immediately to Governance and the Co-Presidents.
> - **Data Protection Survival Clause:** Signatories are legally bound under **GDPR (Europe), PIPEDA (Canada), CCPA (California), Texas privacy statutes**, and laws across 137 UN States, and must return/destroy confidential records upon concluding service.
> - **Communication & Attendance SLAs:**
>   1. **Three (3) consecutive missed meetings without notice** triggers a review and potential removal (`SR 01.01 Sec 5.G`).
>   2. **72-hour Email Response SLA:** Signatories must check and respond to InterPride emails within **72 hours** (local time zone).
>   3. **Minimum Time Commitments:** Co-Presidents (`>= 5 hrs/week`), Board Members (`3–5 hrs/week`), GAC Representatives (`>= 10 hrs/month`).
> - **Legal & Record-Keeping Compliance:**
>   - Fiduciary duties of **care, loyalty, and obedience** under **Texas Business Organizations Code §22.221**.
>   - **7-Year Record Retention** on InterPride Google **Shared Drives** only. Saving organizational files in personal `"My Drive"` is prohibited (`"My Drive"` is cleared at least once a year by IT Management).

## Full Text of the 2026 Organization Agreement

### Pledge & Role Identification
I, `[Name]`, confirm and pledge to devote my time and effort to promote InterPride's mission to increase the capacity of LGBTQI+ Pride organizations around the world. I will seek equal rights for all LGBTQI+ individuals and families and, to the best of my abilities within the scope of my role, support and promote the same for colleagues around the world.

- **My role at InterPride is:** `[Role]`
- **My endorsing member organization is:** `[Organization]`
- **My personal email address is:** `[Email]`
- **My mobile telephone number is:** `[Phone]`
- **I confirm that I have an active Signal account using the number above:** `[Yes/No]`

### Conflict of Interest
I affirm that I will avoid conflicts of interest, both perceived and actual, and will abide by the InterPride Bylaws, Standing Rules, Policies, and Procedures. If my activities or conduct are, or could be, conceived or perceived as conflicting with InterPride's purposes or best practices, I will disclose the conflict to the **Governance Chairs and the Co-Presidents**, who will, in turn, inform the Board.

Failure to disclose conflicts of interest will lead to a review by the Governance Chairs and may result in removal from the Board, committees, working groups, task forces, or other roles within the organization.

I agree not to use my role with InterPride or confidential knowledge gained as a Board Member, GAC Representative, or other InterPride representative for personal or professional benefit.

I understand and agree that InterPride's interests will be my priority and will always be the foundation of my actions within my role at InterPride.

---

### Diversity, Inclusion and Respect
I commit to fostering an environment that is inclusive and respectful of cultural contexts and individual differences. Specifically, I will:
- Recognize that Pride manifests differently across cultures and regions;
- Make reasonable accommodations to ensure activities and communications are accessible;
- Use inclusive language and respect individuals' names and pronouns;
- Support InterPride's commitment to diversity & inclusion by helping create welcoming spaces for all members, contractors, consultants and/or employees;
- Recognize, acknowledge, and respect the unique cultural and safety considerations of LGBTQI+ individuals in different regions.

I understand these commitments extend to all interactions with members, contractors, consultants, employees, and the communities InterPride serves.

### Accessibility
I recognize that accessibility needs must be respected, and I am committed to minimizing barriers and reducing access friction to ensure equitable access for all.

I understand these Diversity, Inclusion, Accessibility, and Respect commitments extend to all interactions with members, contractors, consultants, employees, the communities we serve, and all others we interact with.

---

### Confidentiality
As a Board member, Global Advisory Council member, Committee Chair, or other role, I pledge to:
1. Maintain confidentiality for sensitive organizational matters, including personnel issues, intellectual property, and internal deliberations.
2. Safeguard personal data in compliance with applicable laws, including GDPR, CCPA, and Texas privacy statutes/laws.
3. Not disclose confidential information without proper authorization, including but not limited to, information about board discussions and/or decisions, votes, committee decisions, financial contributors, or member organizations.
4. Notify the **Governance Chairs and Co-Presidents** immediately if I unintentionally share confidential information.

I fully accept and understand that any failure on my part to adhere to these Confidentiality provisions may result in a written warning, disciplinary action, notification to the Board, and/or termination from the Board, Committees, Workgroups, Task Forces, and/or membership.

I will not share, by any means, the names of financial contributors or contributing members of InterPride unless explicitly authorized by the Board or if the information is already publicly available.

I pledge to use discretion when discussing InterPride and its activities, always prioritizing the organization's best interests. As this information is proprietary to InterPride, I understand that sharing it outside the organization would be inappropriate and could cause harm to InterPride's operations and reputation.

I agree to maintain the confidentiality of all information related to votes on decisions, motions, or elections involving member organizations, Board members, Global Advisory Council members, Committee Chairs, and committee members as outlined in InterPride's bylaws, standing rules, policies, and procedures. I acknowledge that voting-related information is strictly confidential and will not disclose it under any circumstances without proper authorization.

I agree to follow the confidentiality matrix, which means I will not discuss with anyone who falls outside of a confidentiality level on that matrix. If I do share any confidential information, I agree to report this to the Governance and the Co-Presidents immediately.

I agree to maintain the confidentiality of InterPride's information even after my involvement has ended. Upon concluding my participation with InterPride, I will promptly return all physical or digital confidential information and property belonging to the organization. Additionally, I will comply with instructions to securely destroy any electronic materials, including but not limited to grievance documents, contracts, and designs.

---

### Confidentiality Obligation Regarding Personal Data
As a Board/Committee member, I understand that I am authorized to have access to official and personal data only to the extent necessary to perform the responsibilities of my role. Personal data includes but is not limited to name, address, phone number, email, and financial information.

I am legally bound to safeguard personal information and will not share such data with others who are not authorized or have not signed a Data Confidentiality Agreement. I will uphold all confidentiality and data protection requirements to comply with legislation, including but not limited to **GDPR (Europe), PIPEDA (Canada), and CCPA (California, USA)**. This obligation of confidentiality shall survive after my term in office and/or service to the organization ends.

I also acknowledge that as of January 2025, 137 of 194 UN States have data and privacy laws that InterPride may be required to uphold.

### Communication
I will maintain active communication with the Co-Presidents, Board, and relevant committees. I understand that:
1. Missing **three consecutive meetings** without notice constitutes a review and potential removal.
2. Only the Co-Presidents or their designees may officially speak on behalf of InterPride.
3. Media inquiries must be referred to the Co-Presidents or designated representatives.
4. I must regularly check and respond to emails within **72 hours** (based on my local time zone), ensuring accountability and timely participation.

---

### General Expectations
1. **Commitment:**
   - **Co-Presidents:** Minimum 5 hours per week.
   - **Board Members:** Minimum 3–5 hours per week.
   - **GAC Representatives:** Minimum 10 hours per month.
2. **Responsibilities:**
   - Recruit Directors, members, and volunteers.
   - Be accessible via telephone and/or web conference as needed.
   - Adhere to the Code of Ethics, Conflict of Interest policy, Value Statement, and Anti-Racism Statement.
   - Take direction and provide leadership within assigned responsibilities.
3. **Conferences and Events:**
   - **GAC Representatives** should attend their region's conference.
   - **Board Members** should attend their region's conference and one additional conference.
   - **Co-Presidents** should attend their region's conference, two additional conferences, and other events as required.
4. **Expense Reimbursement:**
   - All expenses must be pre-approved and submitted following InterPride's financial procedures (`SR 02.02`).
5. **Legal and Financial Compliance:**
   - **A. Fiduciary Duties:** As a Board Member, I understand and will fulfill my fiduciary duties of **care, loyalty, and obedience** as outlined under **Texas Business Organizations Code §22.221**.
   - **B. Indemnification:** Board members will be indemnified for lawful actions taken in their official capacity, except in cases of gross negligence or willful misconduct.
   - **C. Record-Keeping:** All organizational records will be maintained for at least **seven years** in compliance with Texas law. All files must be stored on InterPride's **Shared Drives** following the Organization's Contact and Storage Directory structure. No organizational files can be saved in your InterPride Google Workspace account's `"My Drive"` section. At least once a year, your `"My Drive"` will be cleared by the person responsible for IT and technology management.

## Matrix of Confidentiality

1. All information at InterPride is confidential to the organization unless it is explicitly stated to be able to be shared with partners, members, or the public.
2. The matrix describes the levels of confidentiality and the risks of data breaches if confidentiality is broken. A breach of confidentiality is damaging to the organization regardless of the level of restriction.
3. If you are not sure if you can share information, please contact the **Governance Committee Chairs** (`governance.chairs@interpride.org`) and they will connect you with the appropriate person to confirm if information can be released or not.

| Confidentiality Tier | Reporting & Disclosure Rule | Bodies & Workflows in This Tier |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: Strictly Confidential to That Body Only** | Confidential to that body only. Summaries are reported to the Membership or Board *only* on decisions made and actions taken. | **Co-Presidents**, **Legal Counsel**, **Technology**, **Grievance**, **Board Recruitment**, **Hiring**, **Member Vetting Team**, **Raw Data (PrideRadar)**, **Embargoed Communications** |
| **Tier 2: Confidential to Body / Full Reports to Board as Necessary** | Confidential information for Body only at times. Full reports released to the Board as necessary; summary reports and minutes to the Board. | **Board**, **Consultants**, **Other Projects**, **Finance**, **Fund Development**, **Individual Giving**, **Grant Writing**, **Governance**, **Leadership Development**, **Grants**, **Solidarity**, **Humanitarian**, **WorldPride**, **WorldPride Working Groups**, **Advisory Group** |
| **Tier 3: Confidential to Organization and/or Partner** | Summary reports and minutes released to members. | **FCP**, **EPOA**, **USAP**, **Other Networks & Partners**, **CSIC**, **CSIC Working Groups**, **Scholarship Committee**, **Human Rights**, **ROAP**, **UN Advocacy Working Group**, **Advocacy Partners**, **Member Services**, **Alumni**, **PrideRadar Working Group** |
| **Tier 4: Public Information for Members** | Public information for Members (unless identified as confidential through the utilization of *"In Camera"* processes). | **InterPride Members**, **Communications**, **Newsletter**, **Podcast** |
