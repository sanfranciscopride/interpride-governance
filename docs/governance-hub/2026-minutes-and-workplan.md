## 2. Chronological Summary of 2026 Governance Committee Meetings

### Meeting of August 20, 2026
- **Attendance & Leadership:** Co-Chaired by **Chris Robert**, **Artur Maciejewski**, and **Chris Grafton**; Mentor **Marsha H. Levine**; Secretary **Cristal Robinson**.
- **Key Actions:**
  1. **Record Date for 2026 Elections/Voting:** Confirmed the Record Date as **30 days prior to the opening of voting (August 30, 2026)** so Member Services and Finance can lock the certified voter roll.
  2. **Standing Rules Comprehensive Review:** Launched a full committee review of the Standing Rules (`04.06.2026` edition), assigning committee members to submit redlines into the shared folder/Google Form by **September 30, 2026** (submission window now closed as of October 2026; ready for committee synthesis and Board adoption).
  3. **Candidate & GAC Election Verification:** Coordinated ongoing verification of Board and GAC Regional Representative candidates with `vpms@interpride.org`.

### Meeting of May 14, 2026
- **Key Actions:**
  1. **Resolution 24-02 Restoration Confirmed:** Following review of the Medellín Plenary 3 audio recording, confirmed the exact floor-amended text of `Resolution 24-02` and transmitted the updated `03.20.2026` Resolutions document to the Board for formal Membership affirmation.
  2. **USAP Pride Platform Transition:** Reviewed the governance implications of the USAP contract expiration, including communication to USAP member organizations confirming their InterPride membership remains active through December 31, 2026, and initiated drafting of Standing Rule amendments governing Pride Platform wind-down and regional seat transitions.
  3. **GAC Election Process Updates:** Finalized language for the 8-step Regional Representative election procedure subsequently published in `IP_GAC_Rules and Regulations_English_07.17.26.pdf`.

### Meeting of April 23, 2026
- **Key Actions:**
  1. **Code of Ethics (`SR 08.04`) Implementation:** Followed up on the April 6, 2026 adoption of the 4-step progressive discipline process in `SR 08.04`.
  2. **Medellín Plenary Audio Audit (`Resolution 24-02`):** Assigned verification of the Medellín Plenary 3 audio recording to confirm the omitted floor amendments to `Resolution 24-02`.
  3. **USAP Contract Expiration Review:** Addressed initial referral from the Co-Presidents regarding the expiration/termination of the USAP Pride Platform agreement and its impact on U.S. members and Board representation.
