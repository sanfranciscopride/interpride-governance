## Constitutional Status & The 7-Year Sunset Rule (`SR 03.06`)

Resolutions express the formal policy mandates, human rights positions, operational directives, and commemorative declarations of InterPride's Membership and Board of Directors. Under [`Standing Rule 03.06 (Sunsetting of Resolutions)`](../standing-rules/sr-03-06.html):
- Every Resolution is subject to mandatory review by the **Governance Committee** **seven (7) years** after its adoption (and every 7 years thereafter) to determine whether it should be reaffirmed, codified into a Standing Rule, or sunsetted/archived.

## Key Resolutions Adopted or Restored in `2023–2026`

- **[`Resolution 23-01` (Communications Committee Structure)](operational-policy.html):** Restructured the Communications Committee and regional communications coordination.
- **[`Resolution 23-02` (GM&WC Gala Dinner Ticket Price Cap)](gmwc-and-worldpride.html):** Capped GM&WC Gala Dinner ticket costs and separated optional Gala tickets from base conference registration for lower-income and scholarship delegates *(Note: mislabeled as a second `23-01` on page 27 of the `2025` and `2026` PDFs; preserved and flagged here)*.
- **[`Resolution 24-01` (Commemorating Stonewall 55 & Compton's Cafeteria)](commemorations-and-themes.html):** Commemorated historic trans and queer resistance milestones.
- **[`Resolution 24-02` (Pre-Conference Caucus Day & Scholarship Support — Restored March 20, 2026)](operational-policy.html):** Mandated a dedicated pre-conference day and Scholarship Fund support at all GM&WCs for underrepresented caucuses (**BIPOC, Trans, Non-Binary and Gender Non-Conforming, Intersex, Asexual, Bi/Pan Sexual, and Disability**). Restored in the `03.20.2026` edition after the Governance Committee audited the Medellín Plenary 3 audio recording.
- **[`Resolution 25-01` (Global Pride Theme & Solidarity)](commemorations-and-themes.html):** Adopted at the 2025 GM&WC.
- **[`Resolution 25-02` (Governance & Operational Modernization)](operational-policy.html):** Adopted at the 2025 GM&WC.
- **[`Resolution 25-03` (GM&WC & WorldPride Host Accountability)](gmwc-and-worldpride.html):** Adopted at the 2025 GM&WC *(Note: mislabeled as `25-02` in the Table of Contents of `03.20.2026`, p. 3, but properly titled `25-03` on p. 28)*.
- **[`Resolution 25-04` (HIV & Sexual Health Solidarity Fund Priority & Global Advocacy)](hiv-aids-and-human-rights.html):** Mandated that at least one Solidarity Fund grant per cycle support HIV & Sexual Health initiatives.
