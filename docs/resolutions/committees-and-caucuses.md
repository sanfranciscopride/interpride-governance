## RESOLUTION 23-01
**Compendium History:** `2023-12-03`, `2025-12-12`, `2026-03-20`

> IN ORDER TO ENSURE that the communication of InterPride, the International Association of Pride Organizations focuses on, prioritizes, reflects, and honors, first and foremost, the realities, needs, and expectations of its current 350+ Pride member organizations from 72+ countries;

> Whereas, repetitive communications issues, as well as recurring and incomprehensible actions, including suppression of membership queries, blocking of Pride delegates on the social media outlets of InterPride, posting incorrect membership information despite corrections, dismissing proposals and contributions from member organizations, lack of social media representation of member organizations based outside of the US, all of which have further polarized dynamics among Pride organizations, undermined their friendships and the support they offer each other (in particular Resolutions 86- 12 and 91-06), as well as created unnecessary controversy and drama, and undermined and hindered the trust of the membership in InterPride;

> Whereas, in order to prevent these issues from happening again, plus to improve a respectable and respectful international organization worthy of being elected by Pride Organizations across the world as the international space for us to meet, network, share knowledge, and grow our impact; an organization that licenses the massive event “WorldPride,” and that has been recently, in June 2023, granted Consultative Status to the United Nations Economic and Social Council (ECOSOC);

> And in order to provide the Communications Committee with the necessary operational independence through streamlining the design, development, and distribution of communications materials that build and leverage strategic, focused, sensible, and realistic communication, and in particular effective and transparent communication shared with the membership, and focusing on the membership and on our needs, and elevating our voices;

> Be it resolved, that InterPride officially acknowledges these issues, and that the membership asserts its participation in supporting the efforts for a healthy and empowering communication, as outlined in past resolutions, and beyond.

Implementation: This resolution, once approved, constitutes the communications guidelines of InterPride and the guidelines of the communications committee. Passing the resolution requires the support of InterPride’s membership in its immediate implementation, as outlined in the annexed document.

WITHIN THREE MONTHS of the closure of the 2023 World Conference, the Board will produce and send to all members a detailed plan of how the financial losses from the Gala held in April 2023 will be recouped. This plan should be specific and have a definitive timeline. The Board should report on progress at each World Conference until all funds have been recouped.

## RESOLUTION 24-01
**Compendium History:** `2025-12-12`, `2026-03-20`

The People Living with HIV Caucus request that InterPride implements the Undetectable Equals Untransmittable (U=U) Global Campaign. InterPride is a current signatory.

The Caucus requests that InterPride create materials that member organizations can use in local, regional and global campaigns to promote awareness of People Living with HIV and the U=U Global Campaign.

Implementation Plan: The Communications Committee and Board must create text, graphics, and other materials for the InterPride supported campaign that all member organizations can use license free.

## RESOLUTION 25-02
**Compendium History:** `2025-12-12`, `2026-03-20`

> WHEREAS, Kink & Fetish have always been a part of the pride movement since the very beginning, and today is no different, and; As a community we embrace the freedom of self expression. Within the Pride movement we have recently, due to the political climates seen attacks on Prides who encourage the freedom of expression including members of the kink and fetish community, and; This resolution seeks to create a safe space within InterPride for all of those who identify or include themselves within the umbrella of kink and fetish, to be able to talk openly and freely and to encourage communication between members as well as providing support for our members; THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED THAT, This resolution instructs the Board of InterPride to create the new caucus ready for 2026, and to approach members within our organisation to help take the lead of this caucus. It also instructs that a meeting space is provided at each GM&WC going forward as other caucuses are given, and the caucus can report back into the GM&WC.

## RESOLUTION 25-05
**Compendium History:** `2025-12-12`, `2026-03-20`

> WHEREAS, InterPride, the International Association of LGBTQIA+ Pride Organizers, recognizes that women; including cisgender, transgender, intersex, culturally specific identities; and all who identify as a woman have been foundational to the history, leadership, and continued progress of the global Pride movement, and;


> WHEREAS, women continue to experience systemic inequities and under-representation in leadership, decision-making, and visibility within the international Pride network, and;


> WHEREAS, the inclusion, empowerment, and safety of women within Pride organizing spaces are essential to the fulfillment of InterPride’s mission of diversity, equality, and human rights, and;


> WHEREAS, InterPride has established various caucuses and committees to ensure equitable participation of historically marginalized groups, including but not limited to BIPOC, Trans, Non-Binary, Gender Diverse, Intersex, and People Living with HIV caucuses, and;


> WHEREAS, the establishment of a Women’s Equity Task Force will further strengthen InterPride’s commitment to gender equity and to the inclusion of women’s perspectives across all committees, programs, and events;

THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that InterPride shall formally establish a Women's Equity Task Force, dedicated to supporting, advancing, and amplifying the participation and leadership of women within InterPride and the global Pride movement, and;


> BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Women's Equity Task Force shall:
  - Serve as an advisory and advocacy body to the Board of Directors, committees, and membership;
  - Identify and address barriers to women’s full participation and safety in Pride events and leadership roles;
  - Develop recommendations for policies, programming, and communications that advance gender equity within InterPride;
  - Collaborate with other committees and caucuses, particularly those representing intersecting identities such as seniors, disabled, BIPOC, and Trans communities, and;
  - Provide space for connection, mentorship, and solidarity among women-identified members. Implementation: This resolution, once approved, shall take immediate effect. The InterPride Board shall open nominations for co-chairs of the Women’s Equity Task Force (WETF) within sixty (60) days of adoption and ensure equitable representation from diverse global regions. The committee shall report annually to the membership at the GM&WC, to the Board of Directors at the Mid-Year Meeting, and provide recommendations for continued advancement of gender equity within InterPride.
