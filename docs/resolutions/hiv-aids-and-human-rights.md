## RESOLUTION 89-14

## RESOLUTION 25-04
**Compendium History:** `2025-12-12`, `2026-03-20`

> WHEREAS, InterPride affirms the inherent dignity and equality of all people and is committed to advancing the health, safety, and well-being of 2SLGBTQI+ communities and all people affected by stigma and discrimination, including people living with HIV, and;

> Whereas, HIV remains a significant global public health and human rights issue, with persistent stigma, discrimination, and barriers to prevention, testing, treatment, and care impacting people living with HIV and their communities on a global scale, and;

> Whereas, pride organizations and InterPride member organizations are often on the front lines of HIV-related work, including education, testing, treatment navigation, anti-stigma campaigns, and community support, frequently with limited resources and capacity, and;

> Whereas, members of the People Living with HIV Caucus from Colombia, Argentina, Thailand, and other countries have shared that: Access to HIV treatment, prevention, and sexual health services is increasingly precarious in many contexts. Smaller and emerging organizations often struggle to access funding, technical support, and capacity-building, and; There is a pressing need for strengthened global collaboration, shared campaigns, and mutual support within the InterPride network to address the HIV epidemic effectively, and;

> Whereas, InterPride’s Solidarity Fund (or equivalent grants program) plays a crucial role in supporting member organizations’ projects and initiatives, including but not limited to human rights, health, and community organizing, and even modest micro-grants can have a significant impact at local and regional levels, and;

> Whereas compelling and culturally relevant public communications campaigns, including messages such as “U=U / Undetectable = Untransmittable,” anti-stigma education, and broader sexual health information, remain essential tools in reducing HIV transmission, combating misinformation, and supporting the dignity and rights of people living with HIV, and;

> Whereas, InterPride operates as a nonprofit corporation incorporated under the laws of the State of Texas, and as an organization recognized as tax-exempt under section 501(c)(3) of the United States Internal Revenue Code, and must therefore ensure that all of its programs, grants, partnerships, and communications are consistent with applicable Texas law, U.S. federal law, and 501(c)(3) requirements (including prohibitions on partisan political activity and restrictions on lobbying); NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT, that the following Operative Clauses be enacted:
  - **1.** Explicit Inclusion of HIV & Sexual Health Projects in the Solidarity Fund Resolved, that InterPride shall direct the Board of Directors, or the body responsible for the Solidarity Fund (or any successor grants program), to explicitly prioritize at least one grant per funding cycle, where financially and legally feasible, that supports projects related to HIV and/or broader sexual health, including but not limited to:
  - public education and communication campaigns,
  - anti-stigma and U=U messaging,
  - community-led monitoring or access-to-care initiatives, and
  - initiatives centering on people living with HIV;
  - And that such prioritization shall be implemented in a manner consistent with InterPride’s 501(c)(3) status, its Texas nonprofit obligations, and its bylaws, values, and Code of Ethics & Conduct.

  - **2.** Support for Local and Multi-Pride Micro-Projects Resolved, that InterPride encourages the use of its Solidarity Fund and other appropriate mechanisms to support both:
  - Local and national micro-projects led by member organizations, and
  - Regional or multi-Pride collaborations addressing HIV and sexual health, particularly where those projects are designed to be replicable and adaptable in diverse cultural and linguistic contexts.

  - **3.** Technical and Capacity-Building Support for HIV-Related Work Resolved, that InterPride shall explore and, where feasible, implement mechanisms to provide technical and capacity-building support to member organizations — especially smaller, emerging, or under-resourced groups — which may include:
  - guidance on project design and implementation for HIV/sexual-health initiatives;
  - support in preparing grant applications to the Solidarity Fund and other funders; and
  - training or shared resources on communications, community education, and community-led monitoring;
  - Provided that any such support is delivered within the financial means of InterPride and in conformity with applicable law and its tax-exempt purposes.

  - **4.** Annual Global Space for HIV & Sexual Health Collaboration Resolved, that InterPride shall, subject to available resources, convene at least one recurring virtual space per year (for example, a dedicated online meeting or working session) for members and caucuses working on HIV and sexual health to:
  - share experiences and good practices;
  - co-design or coordinate communications and advocacy campaigns within InterPride’s mission and legal limits;
  - build mutual support networks;
  - And that InterPride shall endeavor to provide multilingual access tools, such as AI-based interpretation services, to maximize participation by speakers of diverse languages.

  - **5.** Global HIV & Sexual Health Communications Campaign Resolved, that InterPride shall, in collaboration with interested member organizations, caucuses, and relevant committees, develop or coordinate a global communications initiative on HIV and sexual health that:
  - incorporates evidence-based messages, including “U=U / Undetectable = Untransmittable”;
  - centers the voices and leadership of people living with HIV;
  - is adaptable for use by member organizations in local and national contexts; and
  - aims to reach not only 2SLGBTQI+ communities but the broader public, recognizing HIV as a societal issue;
  - And that this initiative shall be designed and implemented consistent with InterPride’s tax-exempt educational and charitable purposes and without engaging in prohibited political campaign activity.

  - **6.** Strategic Partnerships with Health and Funding Organizations Resolved, that InterPride shall explore, identify, and, where appropriate, enter into partnerships with:
  - Regional and global health organizations (for example, United Nations Rights Mechanisms or other recognized health and human rights bodies), and;
  - Responsible private-sector partners whose values and practices align with InterPride’s Code of Ethics & Conduct; For the purpose of expanding resources, reach, and expertise for HIV- and sexual-health-related programming by member organizations, and that any such partnerships shall:
  - be vetted for alignment with InterPride’s mission, values, and ethical standards;
  - comply with applicable Texas law and U.S. federal law, including 501(c)(3) restrictions, and;
  - avoid undue commercial influence, conflicts of interest, or risk to InterPride’s independence and reputation.

  - **7.** Recognition of HIV as a Shared Responsibility Across InterPride Resolved, that InterPride affirms that HIV, including its social, economic, and structural determinants, is a matter of shared responsibility across the organization, and that combating HIV-related stigma, discrimination, and barriers to care is integral to InterPride’s mission; and Resolved further, that InterPride encourages its Board, committees, caucuses, and member organizations to integrate HIV and sexual-health awareness, anti-stigma work, and the leadership of people living with HIV into relevant programs, communications, and strategic planning, within the bounds of its legal and financial capacities.

Implementation And Reporting: Resolved, that the InterPride Board of Directors, or its designees, shall:
  - Implementation of this resolution should begin no later than 1 January 2026
  - Funding for the above activities should be procured through the InterPride Grants and Scholarship Programs, where feasible;
  - People Living with HIV should be supported to lead the implementation of the actions within this resolution;
  - When possible and ethically sourced, InterPride should attempt to procure grants and sponsorships for these activities dedicated to supporting People Living with HIV within the global Pride movement;
  - Take reasonable steps to implement this resolution, prioritizing actions that are financially sustainable and consistent with InterPride’s governing documents and legal obligations,and;
  - Provide a written report to the membership at or before the 2026 General Meeting summarizing:
  - steps taken to implement this resolution;
  - any HIV/sexual-health-related grants awarded under the Solidarity Fund;
  - progress on capacity-building efforts, global campaigns, partnerships, and annual collaboration spaces, and;
  - recommendations for future work in this area.

## RESOLUTION 25-01
**Compendium History:** `2025-12-12`, `2026-03-20`

> WHEREAS, InterPride is committed to advancing global 2SLGBTQIA+ human rights and to ensuring that all Pride events and gatherings under its umbrella are inclusive, safe, and accessible for every member of our community, and; Trans, nonbinary, gender nonconforming, and intersex individuals continue to face disproportionate levels of violence, discrimination, and state-sanctioned hostility in many parts of the world, particularly when traveling across borders, and; InterPride’s events, including WorldPride and InterPride General Meeting & World Conference (GM&WC), draw trans, nonbinary, and gender nonconforming participants, leaders, and community members from around the world, many of whom may face increased risks due to gender presentation, documentation, or local laws, and; Ensuring informed participation requires that all attendees, particularly trans, nonbinary, gender nonconforming, and intersex participants, have access to accurate travel advisories and comprehensive safety information before committing to travel, and; InterPride has a responsibility to model best practices in harm reduction, accessibility, and community care when approving host bids for and planning international gatherings; THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED THAT, All future WorldPride bids must include a Travel Advisory and Safety Plan specifically addressing the safety and inclusion of trans, nonbinary, gender nonconforming, and intersex participants, as a mandatory component of the bid submission. The Conference Committee, in collaboration with the host organization, must create a Travel Advisory and Safety Plan specifically addressing the safety and inclusion of trans, nonbinary, gender nonconforming, and intersex attendees.

Implementation: The Travel Advisory and Safety Plan must include, at minimum:
  - A summary of relevant local laws and border policies affecting trans, nonbinary, gender nonconforming, and intersex people, including during travel;
  - An assessment of healthcare access, including availability of gender-affirming care and emergency medical support;
  - A plan for gender-inclusive facilities and accommodations, including bathrooms and housing;
  - Identification of local 2SLGBTQIA+ organizations and resources available for support; A protocol for responding to safety concerns, incidents, or harassment involving trans, nonbinary, and gender nonconforming attendees. The InterPride Global Trans Task Force (IGTTF): in consultation with WorldPride committee leads, shall develop a template and review criteria for this requirement, to be incorporated into the official Host Bidding Guidelines by the next WorldPride bid cycle. The IGTTF will work with the Local Host and Conference Committee to complete a Travel Advisory and Safety Plan, to be implemented by the 2027 GM&WC. InterPride will provide support and mentorship to WorldPride bidding organizations to ensure they can meaningfully fulfill this requirement, particularly for bids from regions with developing or emergent Pride movements, and any currently selected host locations. For WorldPride: This requirement shall take effect beginning with the next call for WorldPride bids, and shall be reviewed for effectiveness after its first implementation cycle. For the InterPride GM&WC: This requirement shall take effect beginning with the opening of registration for the 2027 conference, and shall be reviewed for effectiveness after its first implementation cycle.

Rationale: This resolution strengthens InterPride’s global commitment to trans, nonbinary, gender nonconforming, and intersex inclusion, ensuring that Pride spaces remain aligned with principles of safety, transparency, and harm reduction. By making safety planning a bid requirement, InterPride demonstrates leadership in protecting community members whose safety may be most at risk when traveling internationally.
