## How to Use This Primer

This primer is designed to accompany the official governing documents of **InterPride (the International Association of Pride Organizers)** as an enduring operational and constitutional desk manual for a Co-President across a full three-year term.

It serves four practical functions:
1. **Legal & Constitutional Map:** Explains how InterPride's legal obligations, governing instruments, and parliamentary bodies fit together—and which document controls when texts conflict.
2. **Co-Presidential Command Guide:** Details the exact powers, limits, fiduciary duties, financial sign-off obligations, and inter-meeting authorities vested in the Co-Presidents.
3. **Complete Reference Across All Governing Texts:** Summarizes every Article of the Bylaws (`Articles I–XIII`), every Title of the Standing Rules (`SR 01.01–SR 12.01`), the Global Advisory Council framework, Active Resolutions (`1983–2025`), Grant/Fund Policies, Strategic Plan priorities, and Leadership Agreements.
4. **PDF Errata & Discrepancies Guide:** Documents known typographical bugs, copy-paste layout errors, and cross-document contradictions present in the current official PDF editions so you are never blindsided during a plenary, committee review, or Board vote.

## Part 1: Legal Foundation, Hierarchy of Authority & Fiduciary Duty

### 1.1 Corporate & Tax-Exempt Identity
* **Corporate Domicile & Statute:** InterPride is incorporated as a nonprofit corporation in the **State of Texas**, governed by the **Texas Business Organizations Code (TBOC), Chapter 22**, and subject to Texas state courts of competent jurisdiction (*Bylaws, Art. I & Art. XIII, §1*).
* **Tax-Exempt Status:** Organized exclusively for charitable and educational purposes under **U.S. Internal Revenue Code Section 501(c)(3)** (*Bylaws, Art. III & Art. XI*).
  * **Irrevocable Dedication & No Private Inurement (*Art. XI, §§1–3*):** Net earnings and assets are irrevocably dedicated to charitable/educational purposes. No asset may inure to the private benefit of any Officer, Director, Member, or individual. Upon dissolution, assets pass to a Texas-franchise-tax-exempt 501(c)(3) organization with similar purposes.
  * **Political Campaign Prohibition:** Because of 501(c)(3) status, InterPride cannot intervene in or endorse candidates for public political office.
  * **Secondary Non-U.S. Incorporation Mandate (*Resolution 25-03*):** The Membership has empowered the Board to research and establish a secondary incorporation or registration outside the United States to protect organizational operations and global funds.
* **Statutory Compliance Footprint (*SR 08.11 & Organization Agreement*):** Despite U.S. incorporation, global operations require compliance with:
  * **Data & Privacy Law:** GDPR (Europe), PIPEDA (Canada), CCPA (California), Texas privacy statutes, and applicable national privacy laws across member jurisdictions (137 UN States noted in the 2026 Organization Agreement).
  * **Whistleblower & Governance Mandates:** Sarbanes-Oxley Act (2002) anti-retaliation standards for accounting/audit reporting, TBOC §22.353 ethics/COI recordkeeping standards, Texas Labor Code §§554.002–554.003, and Texas Civil Practice & Remedies Code §27.001 (Anti-SLAPP).

### 1.2 Hierarchy of Authority (Order of Precedence)
When interpreting rules—or resolving a conflict between documents—higher-ranking instruments strictly override lower-ranking ones:

1. **Federal & State Law + Articles of Incorporation** (*Bylaws, Art. XIII, §2* — Bylaws are expressly subordinate to the Articles of Incorporation).
2. **InterPride Bylaws** (*Dec. 12, 2025 ed.*) — The organization's supreme internal constitution. May only be amended through the **two-stage Membership process** in *Article XII*.
3. **Standing Rules** (*Apr. 6, 2026 ed.*) — Binding operational policies enacted and amended by the **Board of Directors** (or by the Membership at a GM&WC). Cannot contradict the Bylaws (*SR 03.06*).
4. **GAC Rules & Regulations** (*Jul. 17, 2026 ed.*) — Operational governance of the Global Advisory Council and Regions. Amended by a **majority of a 50%+1 GAC quorum**, subject to Governance Committee conflict review and **Board approval** (*GAC Rules, §3.A.18*). Cannot supersede any InterPride policy (*GAC Rules, §1*).
5. **Resolutions** (*Mar. 20, 2026 ed.*) — Policy mandates, directives, advocacy stances, and task-specific charters adopted by the Membership or Board. Subject to **seven-year sunsetting review** under *SR 03.06*.
6. **Operational Policies, Agreements & Handbooks** — Fund Policies, the *2026 Organization Agreement & Confidentiality Matrix*, *Values Statement*, and Committee charters.
7. **Parliamentary Authority:** Standard nonprofit parliamentary procedure (e.g., *Robert's Rules of Order Newly Revised* where not displaced by InterPride's written rules).

### 1.3 Fiduciary Duties & Personal Liability Shield
* **Three Fiduciary Duties (*TBOC §22.221 & 2026 Organization Agreement*):**
  1. **Duty of Care:** Act in good faith with ordinary prudence—read financial/board packets, monitor the Risk Register, question irregularities, and attend meetings consistently.
  2. **Duty of Loyalty:** Put InterPride's interests ahead of personal, professional, home-Pride, or regional interests; strictly disclose and recuse on conflicts (*SR 08.04 §2.C & SR 08.10*).
  3. **Duty of Obedience:** Enforce the Articles of Incorporation, Bylaws, Standing Rules, 501(c)(3) restrictions, and duly enacted Membership Resolutions.
* **Liability Protection & Financial Prohibitions (*Bylaws, Art. X & Agreement*):**
  * **No Personal Liability (*Art. X, §1*):** Board members and agents are not personally liable for organizational debts, provided they act lawfully within official capacity (indemnification excludes gross negligence or willful misconduct).
  * **No Unauthorized Credit (*Art. X, §2*):** No Officer or Director may endorse debt, pledge organizational assets as collateral, or bind credit without an express **Board motion**.
  * **Loans Strictly Prohibited (*Art. X, §4*):** InterPride shall never lend financial assets to any Director or agent.

## Part 2: Co-Presidential Powers, Duties & Leadership Mechanics

InterPride employs a **shared three-seat Co-Presidency** (*Bylaws, Art. V, §1*) rather than a single executive president:
* **Three Seats:** One (1) held by a **woman**, one (1) held by a **man**, and one (1) held by a **non-binary or gender diverse person** (*Bylaws, Art. V, §1*; gender self-determination terms defined in *Art. II, §§Y–CC*).
* **Minimum Fill Mandate:** **At least two (2) of the three (3) Co-President seats must be filled at all times** (*Art. V, §1*). Should a vacancy leave only one Co-President in office, the Board and GAC must jointly elect a replacement (*Art. V, §14*, noting the PDF corruption detailed in Part 8).

### 2.1 Core Constitutional & Standing Rule Mandate (*SR 12.01 & Bylaws*)
Under *Standing Rule 12.01 (Board Duties & Responsibilities)* and the Bylaws, the Co-Presidents jointly hold the following authorities and obligations:

1. **Presiding Officer (*SR 12.01.A*):** Preside over all meetings of the Board of Directors and the Membership (plenary sessions at the GM&WC).
2. **Strategic Vision & Mandate Execution (*SR 12.01.B–C*):** Provide vision and direction; oversee implementation of Membership resolutions, annual goals, and organizational activities.
3. **Universal Ex-Officio Committee Seat (*SR 12.01.D; SR 01.01, §5.C*):** Serve as **ex-officio voting members** of all committees, subcommittees, and working groups—**except** where restricted by standing rule, where a body is tasked with nominating Officers to the Board, or where a conflict of interest exists (*SR 01.01, §5.C*). Co-Presidents also appoint Committee **mentors** (*SR 01.01, §5.B*) and serve as default Board focal points for major committees.
4. **Regional Conference Attendance Mandate (*SR 12.01.E; Agreement*):** Must attend the regional conference in their own region of residence (if held) **plus at least two (2) regional conferences outside their region of residence** (more extensive than other Officers, who must attend their own plus one outside conference). *Note under SR 02.02 §3: active Officers are reimbursed up to 50% of transportation, registration, and housing for required out-of-region conferences, and 0% for their own regional conference.*
5. **Financial Signatory & Treasury Gatekeeping (*SR 12.01.F; Bylaws Art. X, §3; SR 02.05; SR 02.02*):**
   * Each Co-President serves as one of the **three authorized signatories** on InterPride's U.S.-chartered bank accounts alongside the Treasurer. Accounts must remain accessible to both Co-Presidents and the Treasurer (*SR 02.05*).
   * Co-Presidents receive and must respond to reimbursement requests forwarded by the Treasurer within **five (5) days of receipt** (*SR 02.02, §2.E*).
   * **Self-Signatory Bar:** No Officer may sign a bank draft payable to themselves (*SR 02.02, §2.G*).
   * **Active-Status Determination (*SR 02.02, §3.B*):** The Co-Presidents, jointly with the Governance Co-Chairs, determine which Board members meet the "active board member" threshold (≥75% vote participation, timely reports, committee participation) required for up to 50% Mid-Year/GM&WC expense reimbursement. When a **Co-President** requests reimbursement, the *other* Co-President(s) and the Treasurer (with Governance Co-Chairs) verify their active status.
6. **Executive & Inter-Meeting Decision Authority (*SR 12.01.G–H*):** The Co-Presidents are authorized to make binding decisions on behalf of InterPride—including:
   * Financial decisions **within approved annual budgets**;
   * Actions designed to **increase organizational revenue**;
   * **Immediate decisions** required between Board meetings;
   * General **operational decisions**; and
   * **Negotiating and signing all contracts** on behalf of InterPride.
   * **Mandatory Accountability Check (*SR 12.01.H*):** All decisions made under *Section G* **must be reported to the Board of Directors at the very next Board meeting**.
7. **Agenda Setting & Meeting Calling (*Bylaws Art. V, §9.B & §9.E; SR 04.02; SR 08.06*):**
   * Call regular/urgent Board meetings (**48 hours' notice** for urgent decisions/meetings per *Art. V, §9.E*; regular electronic meetings require 10 days; in-person require 30 days).
   * Through the Officers, define agendas for GM&WC plenaries, Board meetings, and Officer meetings (*SR 04.02, §1.A.2*).
   * Release business for electronic vote outside meetings (`"ACTION REQUIRED – VOTE"`) and facilitate or delegate facilitation of email discussions under *SR 08.06*.
8. **Official Organizational Voice & Media Control (*Agreement; SR 10.01*):**
   * **Only the Co-Presidents or their explicit designees** may speak officially on behalf of InterPride or authorize mass member/press blasts (`everyone@interpride.org` / `all@interpride.org` are restricted to Co-Presidents and Tech; bulk distributions under *SR 10.01 §1.F* require Co-President/Officer/Board approval).
   * Co-Presidents nominate the **Media and Public Relations Assistant(s)** for Board majority appointment (*SR 10.01, §2.A*).
9. **Crisis, Harassment, Grievance & Ethics Recusal Protocol:**
   * Co-Presidents respond immediately to sexual harassment complaints (*SR 03.05, §4*), receive Ombudsperson post-conference reports within 7 days (*SR 04.04, §4.G*), serve on the WorldPride 7-month license negotiation team (*SR 06.01, §10.B*), and receive urgent risk/ethics reports.
   * **Built-In Recusal Firewall:** Whenever a grievance, whistleblower report, urgent risk escalation, or progressive discipline report concerns a **Co-President**, that Co-President must be omitted/recused from intake and review, and escalation routes directly to the **Governance Committee Co-Chairs** (*SR 03.04 §2.C; SR 08.04 §3.A.2; SR 08.09.G; SR 08.11 §2*).
10. **Operational Time & Tool Commitments (*2026 Organization Agreement*):**
    * **Minimum Time Commitment:** **≥ 5 hours per week** (compared to 3–5 hrs/wk for Board members and 10 hrs/mo for GAC Reps).
    * **72-Hour Email SLA:** Check and respond to InterPride emails within **72 hours** (local time zone).
    * **Active Signal Account:** Mandatory active Signal account tied to registered mobile phone.
    * **Three-Absence Rule:** Missing three (3) consecutive meetings without notice triggers Governance review and potential removal (*Agreement; SR 01.01, §5.G*).

## Part 3: Deep Dive into the InterPride Bylaws (Dec. 12, 2025 Edition)

### Article I & Article II — Name & Definitions
* **Official Name (*Art. I*):** InterPride.
* **Key Constitutional Definitions (*Art. II*) You Will Apply in Every Meeting:**
  * **Abstention (*II.A*):** Present, choosing not to vote. Does *not* meet "present and voting" (*II.M*) and is *not* included in vote totals—meaning abstentions effectively shrink the denominator for simple majority (`51%+` under *II.K*) and `2/3` calculations unless a specific Standing Rule overrides this (note the WorldPride `SR 06.01 §8.B` conflict in Part 8).
  * **Majority Vote (Simple) (*II.K*):** **51% or more** voting in favor.
  * **Present and Voting (*II.M*):** Present in person or permitted electronic medium and casting either `"yes"` or `"no"`.
  * **Quorum (*II.R*):** **50% or more** of those present and voting (Board: voting Officers/Directors; Membership: credentialed Full Members present; Committees: committee members). *(See Part 8, Item #2 regarding the PDF copy-paste error in `Art. II.R`.)*
  * **Pride Organization (*II.P*) vs. Pride Event (*II.N*) vs. Pride Network (*II.O*) vs. Pride Platform (*II.Q*):** A Pride Organization produces a Pride Event as a major purpose, operates like an NGO (registered or unregistered locally), and dedicates any profits to LGBTQI+ communities. A **Pride Network** is an umbrella whose membership is principally Pride organizations. A **Pride Platform** is a country-registered regional membership organization that has a signed joint membership agreement with InterPride.
  * **Global Gender Self-Determination Definitions (*II.Y–CC*, added Dec. 2025):** Explicitly defines **Woman**, **Man**, **Trans**, **Non-Binary People**, and **Gender Diverse**, affirming self-determination and naming global/Indigenous gender identities (*fakaleiti, leiti, muxes, chibados, two-spirit, yan daudu, hijra, khawaja sara, waria, māhū, bakla*) for Co-Presidential eligibility under *Art. V, §1*.

### Article III — Purpose
Organizes InterPride strictly for 501(c)(3) charitable and educational purposes: primary purposes are networking, empowering Pride organizations to produce Pride events, and organizing World Conferences; secondary purposes (as resources allow) are educational programming for LGBTQI+ people, public education, and advancing human rights on SOGIESC issues.

### Article IV — Membership Tiers, Term & Discipline
* **Four Membership Classes (*Art. IV, §§1–5*):**
  1. **Full Member:** Pride organizations producing a Pride event within the **15-month period** prior to a World Conference and paying dues. **Only Full Members hold voting rights** (`1 Full Member = 1 Vote`, *Art. VIII, §3.A*).
  2. **Associate Member:** Produced a Pride event within an **18-month period** prior to a WC, but does not meet the NGO/structure requirements of *Art. II.P*. Non-voting; limited benefits.
  3. **Affiliate Member:** Pride Networks (*Art. II.O*). Non-voting; limited benefits (note: under *SR 01.01 §5.F*, Affiliate Member orgs *can* endorse Committee Co-Chairs).
  4. **Provisional Member:** Has not produced a Pride event within an **18-month period** prior to a WC. Receives full membership benefits except voting rights.
* **Term & Lapse (*Art. IV, §6; SR 02.07*):** Calendar year (**January 1 – December 31**). All unpaid memberships lapse on Dec. 31.
* **Denial or Removal (*Art. IV, §7*):** No compliant Pride org/network may be denied. Organizations failing to adhere to InterPride policies/purposes may be denied or removed by a **two-thirds (2/3) vote of the Board present and voting** (which the Board may delegate to the GAC by Standing Rule).
* **Emergency Waiver (*Art. IV, §9*):** By **2/3 majority vote**, the Board may waive any section of *Article IV* during pandemics, disasters, social unrest, travel advisories, or extraordinary circumstances beyond member control.

### Article V — The Board: Elections, Meetings, Vacancies & Removal
* **Board Composition (*Art. V, §1*):**
  * **3 Co-Presidents** (Woman, Man, Non-Binary/Gender Diverse; ≥2 filled at all times)
  * **Up to 1 VP of Diversity and Inclusion** and **Up to 1 VP of Accessibility** (split in 2023/2024; optional ceilings `"Up to One"`)
  * **1 VP of Membership Services (VPMS)**
  * **4 VPs of Global Outreach (VPGO)**
  * **1 Secretary** and **1 Treasurer**
  * **1 Emeritus Council Representative** (**non-voting** voice added Jan. 2024)
  * **1 Representative for each partnered Pride Platform** (voting Director with signed partnership agreement)
  * **3 GAC Board Representatives** (elected from non-Pride-Platform regions by the GAC)
* **One Seat Per Person & Per Organization (*Art. V, §8 & §13.H*):** No individual may hold multiple Board seats simultaneously (*§8*), and **at no time may more than one (1) person from any given Full Member organization serve on the Board** (*§13.H*).
* **Board Meeting Cadence & Notice (*Art. V, §§9, 10, 17*):**
  * Mandatory **3 meetings at every GM&WC**: pre-Plenary 1, between Plenary 1 and final Plenary, and post-final Plenary (*§9.A*).
  * **Regular Notice (*§9.D*):** **≥10 days** (electronic) or **≥30 days** (in-person).
  * **Special Meetings (*§17*):** Called by **4 Board members**; **≥72 hours** notice (electronic) or **≥30 days** (in-person).
  * **Urgent Co-President Decisions/Meetings (*§9.E*):** **48 hours' notice**.
  * **Electronic Votes (*§9.C, §17.B*):** Must receive at least a number of votes equal to **quorum** to be valid.
  * **Roll-Call Voting (*§10.B*):** Voting is by roll-call unless unanimous consent exists.
* **Nomination Qualifications (*Art. V, §12*, Dec. 2024 rules):**
  * Present (in person or virtually) at the GM&WC where nominated;
  * Actively served on ≥1 InterPride committee or working group for **at least one (1) full year** prior (defined as **≥70% meeting attendance**);
  * Proven leadership experience (committee co-chair/project lead *or* leadership role in their member Pride/2SLGBTQIA+ org during the prior year);
  * Endorsed by and affiliated with a **Full Member organization in good standing**, which must qualify as a Full Member within the **first 60 days** of the new year or forfeit office (*Art. V, §12.B [restored from Jan. 2024] & §18.G*);
  * May accept nomination to **only one office** at a time (*§12.C*) and must be able to serve the full term (*§12.D*).
* **Election Mechanics (*Art. V, §13*):**
  * Signed or electronic ballot at the **final plenary** of the GM&WC.
  * Unopposed races **must** include an option to **hold the position vacant** (*§13.C*). If "vacant" wins a majority, the seat stays open until Board replacement (*§13.F*).
  * At least **two (2) independent supervising delegates**—neither on the Board nor affiliated with any candidate's member org—appointed before voting starts (*§13.E*).
  * Election requires a **majority of votes cast by those present and voting** (*§13.F*). Ties repeat until broken (*§13.G*).
* **Terms & Transitions (*Art. V, §§14–15*):**
  * **3-year terms** (*§15.D*), staggered across two GMs (*§15.B*).
  * **30-Day Transition Window (*§15.A*):** Incoming terms start and outgoing terms conclude **30 days after the GM&WC closes**.
  * **Term Limit (*§15.C*):** Maximum **two (2) consecutive elected terms in the same office**. (Filling a mid-term vacancy does not count toward this two-term cap under *§14.B*.)
* **Removal & Due Process (*Art. V, §18*):**
  * Applies broadly to Officers, Directors, GAC members, Committee members/Co-Chairs, delegates, contractors, and consultants.
  * **Seven Statutory Grounds (*§18.A–G*):** (A) Financial/theft felony conviction; (B) Other felony/criminal conviction unless overridden by **2/3 Board vote within 30 days** with public notice *(expressly exempts convictions tied to criminalization of LGBTIQ+ status/practices, gender identity/expression, sex work, HIV status, or civil disobedience)*; (C) Conflict of Interest; (D) Malicious intent toward InterPride; (E) Vote of "no confidence" in essential role functions; (F) Violation of Bylaws, Standing Rules, or governing documents; (G) **Loss of endorsement/affiliation, or membership fee lapse past 60 days from Jan. 1**.
  * **Mandatory 4-Step Fair & Reasonable Procedure (*§18.H*):**
    1. Investigation conducted by the **Board in partnership with the GAC**;
    2. Motion to accuse (citing Charges & Specifications) passed by the **Governance Committee**;
    3. Subject heard **orally and in writing within 10 days maximum** after Governance passes its motion;
    4. Recommended discipline executed by **2/3 majority vote of the Board of Directors**, with written notice to the expelled person **within 24 hours**.

### Articles VI–IX — Regions, GAC, GM&WC & Languages
* **Regions (*Art. VI*):** The world is divided into Regions (currently 20 Regions). Regional reorganization requires majority vote of Full Members in affected regions + GAC approval (*§1*, note GAC Rules require 2/3 GAC vote). Regions with ≥3 Full Members adopt regional policies and register them with the Secretary (*§2*). Regions with **<3 Full Members** are administered by the Board through the assigned VPGO, who may appoint Regional Directors by Board majority vote (*§3*).
* **Global Advisory Council (*Art. VII*):** 2 Regional Reps per Region (3 if >50 member orgs) (*§1*). GAC elects **3 GAC Board Representatives** (max 2 terms) (*§3*). GAC can recommend Board vacancy appointees, approve regional boundaries/new regions with the Board, and review annual budget, work plan, narrative, and financial reports (*§4*).
* **General Meeting & World Conference (*Art. VIII*):**
  * **Frequency (*§1*, updated Dec. 2025):** Held **at least once every two (2) years**, never later than **25 months** after adjournment of the prior GM&WC.
  * **Host & Format (*§2*, updated Dec. 2025):** Location and host approved by **vote of the Board**; Board may designate a **fully virtual or hybrid format** if needed.
  * **Spokesperson Credentialing (*§3*):** Each Full Member submits in writing **before or within 24 hours after Plenary 1 opens** the name of **one (1) spokesperson and two (2) alternates**.
  * **Scheduling Guardrail (*§3.E*):** **Plenary 2 must not be scheduled on the first or last day of the Conference.**
* **Languages (*Art. IX*):** Organization languages include **English, French, Portuguese, and Spanish**; singular includes plural and vice versa.

### Articles X–XIII — Finance, Assets, Amendments & Law
* **Two-Stage Bylaw Amendment Process (*Art. XII*):**
  * **Initiation:** Must include an **implementation plan** and be signed by **≥5 representatives from different Full Member organizations**, OR approved by majority vote of the Board or GAC (*§1*).
  * **Adoption:** Must pass **both** (1) an affirmative majority vote at the GM&WC *and* (2) a **two-thirds (2/3) majority vote of the Membership within 60 days** of the first vote (*§2*). Takes effect immediately upon the second vote.

## Part 4: Complete Guide to the Standing Rules (Apr. 6, 2026 Edition — 52 Rules, Titles 01–12)

Unlike Bylaws, Standing Rules may be enacted or amended by a **Board motion** sponsored by at least **two (2) Board members** or a standing committee (*SR 03.06*), or by Membership vote.

### Title 01 — Committees, Workgroups & Taskforces (*SR 01.01–01.12*)
* **Committee Operations (*SR 01.01*, rev. Aug. 2025):**
  * **Standing Committees** are established by Standing Rule (permanent mandate); **Ad Hoc Committees** are established by Resolution (finite task/dissolution criteria, cannot duplicate a standing committee).
  * Every committee/subcommittee needs **≥2 members** and **2 to 4 Co-Chairs** (*§5.A–B*). Co-Chairs must be endorsed by a **Full or Affiliate Member Organization** qualifying within the first **60 days** of the fiscal year (*§5.F*).
  * **Mandatory Cadence:** Monthly records/minutes delivered to the Secretary; **all Co-Chairs must attend a Board meeting at least once every two (2) months** (*§5.D*).
  * **3-Missed-Meetings Rule (*§5.G*, added Aug. 2025):** Committee members or Co-Chairs missing **3 consecutive meetings** may be removed for non-attendance (with exceptions for health/disability if communicating and working productively).
* **Standing Bodies Chartered Under Title 01:**
  * *SR 01.02*: Conference Structure & Integrity Committee (**CSIC**)
  * *SR 01.03*: **Emeritus Council** (Advisory body of former Board members, Regional Reps, Co-Chairs, and Staff; **non-voting Board seat** updated Jan. 2024)
  * *SR 01.04*: **Member Services Committee**
  * *SR 01.05*: **Human Rights & Diversity Affairs Committee (HRDA)**
  * *SR 01.06*: **Finance Committee**
  * *SR 01.07*: **Fund Development Committee**
  * *SR 01.08*: **WorldPride Committee**
  * *SR 01.09*: **Communications & Website Operations Committee**
  * *SR 01.10*: **Scholarship Committee**
  * *SR 01.11*: **Governance Committee** (Board/GAC orientations, mentors, Org Agreements, attendance/compliance tracking, nominee vetting, Bylaws/SR stewardship, hiring/compensation via *Hiring Subcommittee*, leadership training via *Leadership Development Subcommittee*, and committee of inquiry for grievances/arbitration)
  * *SR 01.12*: **InterPride Global Transgender Task Force (IGTTF)** *(added Dec. 2025)* — 15–21 members, **≥2/3 Trans/GNC/Intersex/Gender Diverse/Two-Spirit**, weighted Trans vote on member applications, 3-yr member terms (max 3), 1-yr Chair/Co-Chair terms (max 3), ≥75% meeting attendance, Board members welcome without standard application interview.

### Title 02 — Financial Governance, Reimbursements & Dues (*SR 02.01–02.07*)
* **Financial Reporting & Controls (*SR 02.01*):**
  * **Monthly Package:** Treasurer + Finance Committee present P&L, budget vs. actual, balance sheet, AR/AP, bank reconciliations, and check registers to the Officers by the **20th of each month** for Officer approval by the start of the next month, then distribute to the Board.
  * **Year-End Statements:** Unaudited annual statements presented to the Board **≤30 days after Dec. 31**, approved by start of following month, and posted to Members.
  * **Tax Reporting (IRS Form 990):** Presented to Officers **≥15 days before due date**; Officers approve within **10 days**; distributed to Board and posted to Members.

## Part 6: Global Advisory Council (GAC) & Regional Architecture (*GAC Rules Jul. 17, 2026 ed.*)

* **Structure:** 20 global Regions. Each Region with **3+ member organizations** elects **2 Regional Representatives** (**3** if **>50 member organizations**), max **1 per member org or city**. Regions with `<3` member orgs may have a Board-appointed rep until eligible to elect. No GAC Rep may concurrently serve as a Pride Platform Board Rep or InterPride Officer.
* **Terms & Limits:** **3-year terms** (*GAC Rules §2*, taking effect at the close of that year's GM&WC; staggered initial 2-yr/3-yr split when both seats fill simultaneously), max **2 terms**.
* **Standardized 8-Step Regional Election Process (added Jul. 17, 2026):** Jointly administered by **Governance Co-Chairs** (`governance.chairs@interpride.org`) and **VPMS** (`vpms@interpride.org`):
  1. Notice emailed to `regionXXmembers@interpride.org` (cc VPMS).
  2. Packets (Full Member Endorsement Letter, Letter of Intent, **≤400-word bio**, **3 references**, optional CV) sent to outgoing reps, Governance Co-Chairs, and VPMS.
  3. Clear deadline (>1 week recommended).
  4. **VPMS + Governance** verify candidate and org eligibility.
  5. **Recorded regional Q&A meeting** posted on calendar.
  6. Recording & packets emailed to regional members.
  7. **7-day secret Google Form vote** (with voter verification) built with Governance.
  8. **Governance + VPMS** jointly certify results → notify winner → announce to region → onboard.
* **GAC Board Representatives (3 Voting Board Seats):**
  * Elected by GAC from Regional Reps via **7-day electronic ballot** after an electronic Q&A (400-word bio, endorsement signed by another officer of their Pride org, 3 references), **requiring a 2/3 supermajority vote** and verified by GAC Board, Governance, and **two InterPride Board members**; max **2 consecutive 3-year terms**; priority on hemispheric/geographic diversity and elevating marginalized voices.
  * Lead **monthly GAC Board check-ins** (**inviting Co-Presidents, Governance Chairs, and VPGOs**) and **bi-monthly open general GAC meetings**.
* **Auto-Resignation & Removal:** Missing **>3 consecutive unexcused** local/official GAC meetings triggers automatic resignation (`§2.6.f`); sending regrets and missing >50% across 2 consecutive meetings is grounds for removal (`§3.A.13`). GAC itself may remove a Rep by **2/3 vote**; Board retains parallel removal authority under *Bylaws Art. V, §18*.

### 9.2 Quick Tactical Decision Checklist for a Co-President
Before taking or approving any significant executive action, verify these five checks:
1. **Authority & Dollar Threshold:** Is this budgeted and within Co-President operational/contract authority (*SR 12.01.G*), or does it require Officer approval (`<$500` unbudgeted, logo merch, goal reassignment) or a Board vote (`>$500` unbudgeted, Standing Rule change, policy waiver, litigation/credit)? If taken under *SR 12.01.G*, calendar it immediately for the **next Board meeting report** (*SR 12.01.H*)—and remember the Board must advise the **GAC within 30 days** (*Art. V §6*).
2. **Notice & Quorum Validity:** Did a regular electronic Board meeting get **≥10 days' notice** (or **≥72 hrs** for a 4-member special meeting, or **48 hrs** for an urgent Co-President call)? Outside meetings under *SR 08.06*, did you allow up to 2 weeks of single-thread discussion and keep `"ACTION REQUIRED – VOTE"` open until **quorum** voted?
3. **Confidentiality Tier & Conflict Check:** What Confidentiality Tier applies (*Org Agreement*)? Never leak individual vote counts, confidential grievances, donor names, or Tier 1 deliberations. If *you* or any participant has an actual or perceived conflict (*SR 08.10*) or is the subject of an ethics/grievance filing (*SR 03.04 / 08.04 / 08.11*), step out of intake/decision channels immediately.
4. **Rule Integrity:** Does the action align with higher-ranking Bylaws over an outdated Standing Rule or PDF typo (Part 8)?
5. **7-Year Archival Discipline:** Save all signed contracts, Co-President decisions, motion appendices, and minutes into the appropriate **Google Shared Drive**—never in a personal `"My Drive"`.
