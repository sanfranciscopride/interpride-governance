## Governance Context
Adopted in October 2023 to guide the structural transition of former Regional Representatives into the **Global Advisory Council (GAC)** and to establish how partnered **Pride Platforms** (EPOA, FCP, USAP) interface with the Board and GAC.

{: .important }
> **2026 Update on USAP Pride Platform Contract:** Per the April 23 and May 14, 2026 Governance Committee Minutes, the USAP–InterPride Pride Platform Contract expired/was terminated in 2026. InterPride assured USAP members that their InterPride membership remains valid through the end of 2026, and the Governance Committee + VPGOs are reconfiguring U.S. GAC regional coverage (`USAP Central`, `Eastern`, `Southern`, `Western`) and codifying the procedure for ending/transitioning Platform Board representation under `SR 02.07` and `Bylaws Article V`.

INTERNATIONAL ASSOCIATION OF PRIDE ORGANIZERS O

GLOBAL ADVISORY COUNCIL AND PRIDE PLATFORM TRANSITION PLAN

OCTOBER 2023

GAC AND PRIDE PLATFORM TRANSITION IMPLEMENTATION PLANS

GAC Transition and Implementation In the transition to include Pride Platforms (PP) on the InterPride Board of Directors, as recommended by the Strategic Plan, and approved by vote of the membership, the GAC will be reduced to three (3) seats. These seats should not overlap representation with partnered PP’s already established. The GAC Board should strive to include under- represented regions. To assist with this transition, the current Vice Presidents of Global Outreach (VPGOs) will meet with the current GAC leadership and assist in achieving representation for those regions. This transition should be completed by December 31, 2023 so all the representatives can be onboarded and take their seats when invited to at the January InterPride Board Meeting.


### PURPOSE

To define the manner in which Regions are established, operate, and may dissolve, as well as the manner in which Global Advisory Council (GAC) Representatives are selected and operate. The primary purpose of the Council shall be to equally represent in the engagement and activities to promote Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, and Transgender Pride on an international level; to increase networking and communication among Pride Organizations; and to encourage diverse communities to hold and attend Pride events and to act as a source of education. ELECTION OR APPOINTMENT TO THE GAC BOARD

- **A.** The currently seated GAC Board members will send an announcement out to all general GAC representatives, stating the number of seats vacant, requesting criteria to be submitted by a specified deadline.

- **B.** Criteria as recommended in the Candidate Verification Process (Standing Rule 03.09) will be submitted to their regional GAC representatives, who will consult with Governance to determine eligibility, then forwarded to the GAC Board.

- **C.** In the event of no GAC representatives for that region, they will send their criteria directly to the Governance Committee and the GAC Board.

- **D.** Regions can meet and review potential candidates and can recommend one or all of those indicating interest.

- **E.** The GAC Board will conduct a remote candidate question and answer session for all the GAC representatives.

- **F.** The GAC Board will distribute an electronic ballot with a voting period of not less than seven (7) days. Governance will serve as election observers and verify the results.

- **G.** Candidates receiving super majority votes (two-thirds or 66%) will be seated in order of highest votes attained until all seats are filled.

REMOVAL OR RESIGNATION FROM THE GAC BOARD A GAC Board Representative may be removed at any duly called meeting of the Global Advisory Council, by a two-thirds (2/3) vote of the present and voting Council members. Grounds for removal include, but are not limited to:

- **A.** Conflicts of interest

- **B.** Criminal conduct

- **C.** Repeated unexcused absences from the Council Meetings

- **D.** Violation of InterPride’s Board Member Agreement, Values Statement, or Code of Ethics and Conduct, or other governing documents

- **E.** When found to be acting contrary to the best interest of the Council

GAC AND PRIDE PLATFORM TRANSITION IMPLEMENTATION PLANS

The GAC retains the right to remove a GAC representative in accordance with the Governing documents of the Organization. The Board of InterPride retains the right to remove a GAC representative in accordance with the governing documents of the Organization. A GAC representative may resign from their service by submitting a letter of resignation to any of the other representatives, and then reported to the Governance Committee of the Organization, copying in the Secretary and Co-Presidents, for record keeping.

GAC DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
  - The GAC Representative on the Board will act as the leaders of the Council.
  - In addition to those duties and responsibilities that may, from time to time, be assigned to them by their Region, GAC Representative shall have the following duties and responsibilities: c Advancing the mission and goals of the Organization c Conducting outreach to all Pride organizations in their Region and representing their views to the Board c Disseminating information to and from the Board to Members and interested parties within the Region c Maintaining good relations with past, current, and potential members c Seeking out and recruiting new Member organizations c Assisting the Treasurer in the collection of Membership dues within their Region c Providing the Secretary and the VP of Member Services with contact and other information on all Pride organizations within their Region, for entry into databases c Attending at least two-thirds (⅔) of the scheduled meetings of the Board, and otherwise participating within the organization c Approve regional boundaries or recommend changes as needed c Gather information, produce reports, and present recommendations to the Board c Recommend to Governance individuals for appointment by the Board to vacant positions c Actively participates in at least one (1) committee of the Organization c Creating and editing the GAC Rules and Regulations for the Council, to be approved by a super majority (two-thirds or 66%) of elected Representatives) at any duly called meeting of the Council c Exercising and performing such other duties and responsibilities as may, from time to time, be assigned by the Board, the Bylaws, and any other governing documents of the Organization

The VPGOs and GAC leadership should strive to encourage representation continentally, which would include three (3) seats total, one (1) representative from the following:
  - Asia
  - Oceania
  - Africa
  - Latin America
  - South America

If the transition should leave a region un(der)represented, it is the responsibility of their liaison VPGO to make sure they represent them until such time as a one is elected/appointed, or they organize or join a Pride Platform.

GAC AND PRIDE PLATFORM TRANSITION IMPLEMENTATION PLANS

Pride Platform Transition and Implementation This transition should be completed by December 31, 2023 so the representatives can be onboarded and take their seats by the January InterPride 2024 Board Meeting. To assist with this transition, the VPGOs will work with the Pride Platform leadership.


### PURPOSE

To represent the interests of the Pride Platforms of InterPride (IP) and bring their voices and concerns to the leadership.

PRIDE PLATFORM BOARD MEMBERS DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
  - Liaising with the Pride Platform members with which they are elected to represent.
  - Attending their Pride Platform conference, if one is scheduled, as well as any local Pride events possible.
  - Working to recruit Pride organizations within the Pride Platform to which they are elected.
  - Serving on at least one committee, subcommittee, or workgroup of the InterPride Board.
  - Exercising and performing such other duties and responsibilities as may, from time to time, be assigned by the IP Board, Bylaws, Standing Rules, and the other governing documents of the Organization.
  - Attending InterPride Board of Directors meetings and providing a Platform report.

ELECTION TO THE INTERPRIDE BOARD Initially, the Governance Committee of InterPride, working with the Board or governing body of the Pride Platform, will ensure the establishment of equitable candidate submission notice, process for receipt of candidate submissions, and electronic remote election of candidates. Elections will take place in coordination with and at the same time as Board elections of InterPride. Pride Platforms will be responsible for conducting their own elections within their Platforms. Pride Platforms will reach out to their members in good standing of InterPride, within their Platform to announce the call for candidates. From those candidate submissions received and verified, elections will be held to choose the one representative to serve on the Board of InterPride for a term of three (3) years. If the representative is unable to complete their term there will be a new election. This election will be held within 30 days. In the interim, the board of the Pride Platform, may appoint a representative to fill the vacancy.

REMOVAL OR RESIGNATION Removal for Pride Platform InterPride Board members will follow the same guidelines as those for regular InterPride Board members, and will include but not be limited to violations of our Values Statement, Board agreement, Bylaws, Standing Rules, Code of Ethics and Conduct, and other governing policies or documents of the Organization. This specifically includes any disparagement of the Organization and any of its Board of Directors, members, staff, or contractors; as well as failure to attend three (3) consecutive Board meetings. Either the Pride Platform can take appropriate steps to remove the Pride Platform Board member, or the InterPride Board of Directors can do so.
