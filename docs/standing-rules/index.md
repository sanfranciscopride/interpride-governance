## Constitutional Role & Amendment Authority

The **Standing Rules** constitute the operational code of InterPride. Unlike the Bylaws, Standing Rules may be adopted, amended, or repealed by either a **majority vote of the Board of Directors** or a **majority vote of Full Member Organizations** at a General Meeting & World Conference (GM&WC). In any conflict between a Standing Rule and the Bylaws, the Bylaws control.

## Major Additions in the `2025-12-13` & `2026-04-06` Editions

1. **[`SR 01.12` — InterPride Global Transgender Task Force (IGTTF)](sr-01-12.html) (Added Dec 2025):** Establishes the 15–21 member Global Transgender Task Force to advise leadership, review trans-specific policies, and develop the mandatory Travel Advisory & Safety Plan template under `Resolution 25-01`.
2. **[`SR 02.07` — Membership Fee Schedule & Pride Platform Memberships](sr-02-07.html) (Approved Mar 13, 2026):** Codifies the AGI sliding-scale membership fee schedule (`$50–$1,000` USD), OECD ODA country tiers (`$1/$5/$10/$20` USD), and Pride Platform membership contract rules (`50/50` split, Jan 31 remittance, Dec 31 lapse, 50-Pride minimum, and 1 Board seat per Platform).
3. **[`SR 08.04` — Code of Ethics and Conduct](sr-08-04.html) (Updated Oct 2025):** Extends the Code of Ethics to all official InterPride activities and adds **Section 3 (4-Step Accountability & Progressive Discipline Process)** overseen by the **Governance Committee Co-Chairs**, plus immediate Step 3 suspension authority.
4. **[`SR 08.09` — Risk Management Policy](sr-08-09.html) (Approved May 2025):** Establishes enterprise risk management across 5 risk categories (Governance, Operational, Financial, Reputational, External) with a centralized Risk Register and quarterly Board reporting.
5. **[`SR 08.10` — Conflict of Interest Policy](sr-08-10.html) (Approved May 2025):** Codifies mandatory annual Conflict of Interest disclosures, situational disclosures, recusal from deliberation and voting, and investigation by the Risk & Compliance subcommittee of Governance.
6. **[`SR 08.11` — Whistleblower Reporting and Protection Policy](sr-08-11.html) (Approved May 2025):** Establishes good-faith reporting channels to the **Governance Committee Co-Chairs** (`governance@interpride.org`), strict anti-retaliation protections, and confidential investigation procedures *(Note: contains unreplaced `[PLATFORM NAME]` placeholder in Section 2 flagged for September 2026 redline)*.

## Architecture of the Standing Rules (`Titles 01–12`)

- **Title 01 (`SR 01.01–01.12`):** Committees, Workgroups, & Taskforces (General Operations, Conference Integrity, Emeritus Council, Fund Development, Human Rights, Member Services, Finance, Scholarship, WorldPride, Audit, Governance, IGTTF)
- **Title 02 (`SR 02.01–02.07`):** Finance, Budget, Expense Reimbursement (`>=75%` vote participation), Auditing, Waivers (max 2 consecutive years), Grants, and Membership / Pride Platform Fee Schedule (`SR 02.07`)
- **Title 03 (`SR 03.01–03.09`):** Administration, Minutes, Grievance & Conflict Resolution Process (`SR 03.04`), Sexual Harassment, 7-Year Resolution Expiration (`SR 03.06`), Language, and Candidate Verification (`SR 03.09`)
- **Title 04 (`SR 04.01–04.04`):** General Meeting & World Conference (GM&WC) Bid, Host, 60-Day Record Date (`SR 04.02 Sec 1.A.1`), Regional Conference Loss Reimbursement, and Ombudspersons
- **Title 05 (`SR 05.01–05.02`):** Marketing & InterPride Logo Usage
- **Title 06 (`SR 06.01`):** WorldPride Expressions of Interest, Bidding, Vetting, Licensing, and Post-Event Requirements
- **Title 07 (`SR 07.01`):** International Pride Theme *(Marked `INACTIVE – SEPTEMBER 2021`)*
- **Title 08 (`SR 08.01–08.11`):** Additional Rules & Core Compliance Policies (Code of Ethics `SR 08.04`, Underrepresented Regions `SR 08.05`, Electronic Voting Outside Meetings `SR 08.06`, Strategic Planning `SR 08.08`, Risk Management `SR 08.09`, Conflict of Interest `SR 08.10`, Whistleblower Protection `SR 08.11`)
- **Title 09 (`SR 09.01–09.02`):** Regional Affairs & Standards
- **Title 10 (`SR 10.01`):** Media & Public Relations
- **Title 11 (`SR 11.01`):** Campaigning Policy & Spending Caps
- **Title 12 (`SR 12.01`):** Board Duties & Responsibilities
