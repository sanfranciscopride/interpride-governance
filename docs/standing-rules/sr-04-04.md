## Adopted Text (`04.06.2026` Edition)

### PURPOSE

To provide guidelines and requirements for the selection and roles of Ombudspersons for the InterPride General Meeting & World Conference (GM&WC).


### SECTION 1 – DEFINITION

An official appointed to investigate complaints, barriers to access, safety, and individuals' issues against InterPride, its members, or a hosting organization. A designated neutral who provides conflict resolution and problem-solving services. The typical duties of an ombudsperson are to investigate complaints and attempt to resolve them, through recommendations (binding or not), restorative justice, or mediation. Ombudspersons sometimes also aim to identify systemic issues leading to poor actions or breaches of member and human rights. The Ombudsperson does not play a role in formal processes, nor represent any side in a dispute.


### SECTION 2: OMBUDSPERSON SELECTION REQUIREMENTS


- **A.** The recommended number of Ombudspersons for the GM&WC is three (3), though it can be less or more.

- **B.** They cannot be an active Board member or Committee Co-Chair of InterPride, or a Planning Committee/ Host Organization member.

- **C.** Preference is for individuals who have formal training in conflict resolution and non-violent de-escalation, as well as crisis intervention and trauma-informed counseling.


### SECTION 3: AUTHORITY TO APPOINT


- **A.** The Governance committee will review all applicants for the position of Ombudsperson(s) and select appointee(s).


### SECTION 4: REQUIREMENTS OF THE OMBUDSPERSONS


- **A.** Receive, investigate, and resolve, if possible, all complaints received from participants.

- **B.** Be available all days of the conference and associated events to support all participants of all identities.

- **C.** Refer any participants in crisis to relevant services and/or authorities.

- **D.** In the above situation(s), a report will go to the Governance Co-Chairs within 24 hours.

- **E.** Follow related policies and procedures of InterPride.

- **F.** Provide a report of all complaints received, investigated, resolved, and unresolved to the Governance

- **G.** Co-Chairs Chairs and the Co-Presidents within 7 days of the event or meeting.


### SECTION 5: OMBUDSPERSON SUPPORT


- **A.** The Governance Co-Chairs will act as support during the conference for the Ombudspersons to fulfill their duties.

- **B.** The Governance Committee will follow through with any unresolved matter(s) reported by the Ombudsperson(s).

- **C.** InterPride will cover registration fees for the General Meeting & World Conference.


### SECTION 6: ROLE OF THE OMBUDSPERSON(S)

The following areas of concern fall under the role of the Ombudspersons, and may be investigated by them, though resolution could be assigned to a related department:

- **A.** Gender neutral signage (lack of; responsibility of the Host Committee/Event Coordinator)

- **B.** Gender neutral language (responsibility of all parties)

- **C.** Misogyny, misandry, and misanthropy (address globally by InterPride)

- **D.** Racism, Sexism, Transphobia, Biphobia, Homophobia, Exclusion (address globally by InterPride)

- **E.** Misgendering (addressed globally by InterPride)

- **F.** Conference or Event Content (CSIC, WorldPride, Event Coordinators)

- **G.** Vibe/Conduct (Anger, Disrespect, Disruption, Bullying, Marginalizing, Patronization – address globally by InterPride)

- **H.** Safety, Accessibility, Communication (venue- or event-related)
