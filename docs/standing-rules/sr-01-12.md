## Governance Summary & Commentary
**Added December 2025:** Establishes the **InterPride Global Transgender Task Force (IGTTF)** to advise leadership, review trans-specific policies, and develop the mandatory Travel Advisory & Safety Plan template under `Resolution 25-01`. Comprises 15–21 members (at least 2/3 Trans, GNC, Intersex, Gender Diverse, or Two-Spirit), with 3-year member terms and 1-year Chair/Co-Chair terms.

## Adopted Text (`04.06.2026` Edition)

NECESSITY
  - To bring attention and awareness to the violent attacks, legislative proposals, and general wellbeing of our global transgender community.
  - To provide resources and support to the global transgender community.
  - To examine the atmosphere of the InterPride Board of Directors to ensure that it is a safe space for transgender people to participate.

MISSION & VISION The mission and vision of the InterPride Global Transgender Task Force (IGTTF) is to provide education and advocacy on transgender issues that improve the quality of life for the global transgender community and to celebrate those lives.

COMPOSITION
  - Consists of up to 15 members.
  - Can be increased to up to 21 if more interestinterested is determined.
  - 2/3 of the members should be Transgender, Gender Non-Conforming, Intersex, Gender Diverse, Gender Expansive, or Two-Spirited.
  - Will have a Chair and Vice-Chair.
  - Initially championed by Jordan Braxton, GAC Region 4 Representative and Board of Directors.
  - Task Force Leadership should consist of:
  - Chair
  - Directs Task Force to see it fulfills its responsibilities
  - Ensures the Task Force has specific objectives
  - Monitors committee member assignments
  - Motivates and encourages committee members
  - Presides over meetings to ensure adherence to the major issues and purposes
  - Works with the Vice-Chair to create agendas and meeting note
  - Co-Chair
  - Assists the Chair with duties
  - Presides over meetings in the absence of the Chair
  - Works with the Chair to create agendas and meeting notes
  - Task Force Members
  - Attend 75% of Task Force meetings
  - Carries our individual assignments as assigned.
  - Actively contributes and voices opinions
  - Actively working on a task to be a member of the Task Force.

MEMBER SELECTION
  - Initial Task Force members can join through open call. No selection or voting process.
  - Initial members wanting to be on the Task Force must be members of a Pride Member organization.
  - Initial members must have a Letter of Support from their Pride Member organization.
  - Once Task Force is established an application must be completed to join the Task Force.
  - Application must be accompanied with a Pride Member Organization Letter of Support.
  - Application will be reviewed by the sitting Task Force members.
  - Application moves forwards with a simple majority vote with Trans members of the Task Force having a weighted vote.
  - Applicants must be interviewed by a member of the Task Force, a member of the Human Rights Committee and a member of the InterPride Board of Directors.
  - Applicants must receive a simple majority vote to be accepted to Task Force with Trans members of the Task Force having a weighted vote.
  - Existing InterPride Board members are welcome on the Task Force without the above requirements.

TERM LIMITS
  - Each member of the Task Force will have a 3-year term limit.
  - Each member of the Task Force can serve up to 3 consecutive terms.
  - The Chair and Co-Chair will serve 1-year terms.
  - The Chair and Co-Chair can serve up to 3 consecutive terms.
