## Governance Summary & Commentary
**Approved March 13, 2026:** Codifies InterPride's complete **Membership Fee Schedule** (AGI sliding scale from `$50` to `$1,000` USD; OECD ODA tiers of `$1 / $5 / $10 / $20` USD; community center Pride P&L deduction rules) and **Section 2: Pride Platform Memberships** (`50/50` fee split after transaction fees, Jan 31 / 30-day remittance, Dec 31 lapse, minimum 50 Pride org members, 66% fee-contributing, no for-profit licensing, and 1 elected Board representative per Platform).

## Adopted Text (`04.06.2026` Edition)

### PURPOSE

To establish a policy regarding how membership fees are paid.


### SECTION 1 – MEMBERSHIP PAYMENTS


- **A.** The Organization, as provided for in Article IV, Section 8 of its Bylaws, shall collect an annual membership fee known as fees.

- **B.** InterPride membership is for one calendar year, concluding on December 31st. The cost of membership ibased on each organization’s gross annual income and the following schedule: Full Membership/Associate Membership $0-25,000 $50 $25,001-50,000 $100 $50,001-75,000 $150 $75,001-100,000 $200 $100,001-200,000 $400 $200,001-400,000 $600 $400,001-1,000,000 $800 $1,000,0001+ $1,000 Provisional Members $50 Affiliate Membership $100 for local, or statewide organization/company $200 for national and international organization/company

- **C.** Full Membership/Associate Membership/Provisional Membership for organizations with annual revenues of $0-25,000 from Official Development Assistance (ODA) eligible countries. The list of countries under each category are updated by the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD), www.oecd.org/en.html. Least Developed Countries: $1 Other Low-Income Countries: $5 Lower Middle-Income Countries and Territories: $10 Upper Middle-Income Countries and Territories: $20

- **D.** All Memberships are to be paid through the membership portal on the InterPride website. Member Prides will have the option to pay by credit card, electronic transfers, or check. If paid by check, the check should be received by the Treasurer within 30 days of submission via the membership portal.

- **E.** All memberships are to be paid in US-equivalent dollars (USD).

- **F.** The Treasurer will reconcile the memberships received with a monthly report from the membership portal that is provided by the Vice President, Member Services/Member Services Committee.

- **G.** Pride organizations that are programs of community centers should submit a Profit and Loss Statement, or a similar financial report, for their Pride event revenue independent from their center's compiled revenue. This deducted difference should also include in-kind contributions. The actual center's operating revenue will only be referenced for assessment purposes.


### SECTION 2 – PRIDE PLATFORM MEMBERSHIPS


- **A.** InterPride will split each paid membership fee with the Pride Platforms, on a 50/50 percent basis, for all regions within their Pride Platform. A Pride Platform is recognized when it includes at least two-thirds (2/3) individual paid member Pride organizations, has elected Platform officers, and has bylaws or operating documents that have been filed with the government where it resides.

- **B.** Membership fees which are submitted and paid through the Pride Platform's membership portal, must be sent to InterPride on a regular basis, as per the contract.

- **C.** The Treasurer and the Vice President of Member Services will transfer membership fees within 30 days upon receipt of those received by January 31st, and with the end of the fiscal year on December 31st annually, will declare all memberships as lapsed if not renewed by the payment threshold above.

- **D.** No Pride Platforms will be licebsed or run by a separate for-profit entity.

- **E.** Pride Platform Requirements:
  - **1.** Platforms must maintain a minimum of fifty (50) Pride organization members each year.
  - **2.** 66% of Platform members must contribute to Membership fee payments.
  - **3.** Platform members must be located within the same InterPride region or regions.
  - **4.** Platforms must allow members to decide if they wish to join or not, without blocking them from joining InterPride.
  - **5.** Platforms must agree to the 50/50 percent membership fee rate split (after deduction of all transaction fees) with InterPride.
  - **6.** Platforms will choose an actively engaged representative to serve on the InterPride Board in a leadership role and provide support to the Organization.
  - **7.** InterPride reserves the right to request a new representative if the selected person fails to best represent InterPride ( i.e. Interpride Core Values and Governance).
  - **8.** Platform representatives to InterPride should be selected by the membership in that region through a clear vote, the results of which are shared with InterPride.
  - **9.** Pride Platforms will be responsible for distributing information about InterPride and any changes to its membership fees and processes to its members.
  - **10.** Pride Platform-offered discounts will be deducted from their portion of the Membership fee.
  - **11.** All membership fees must be paid by credit card, electronic transfer, or check and received by the Treasurer within 30 days of submitting via the membership portal.


### SECTION 3 – ENACTMENT AND PRECEDENCE

This standing rule shall take effect immediately upon passage and replace all other standing rules, motions, resolutions, joint agreements and other policies enacted by the Executive Committee, Board of Directors and Membership dealing with the subject matter contained herein.
