BID REQUIREMENTS AND SELECTION PROCESS

### PURPOSE

To establish the procedures for selecting a host city for the General Meeting and World Conference, herein referred to as the GM&WC of InterPride (the Organization).


### SECTION 1 – ORGANIZATIONAL ELIGIBILITY

In order to be eligible as a potential host city of the GM&WC of the Organization, any proposed host city must have at least one airport, within a fifty (50) mile radius, offering direct international flights, and have substantial airline service from at least one (1) major airline. The proposed host city must also be home to at least one (1) InterPride Full Member within a fifty (50) mile radius in good standing.


### SECTION 2 – ORGANIZATIONAL PROCESS TIMELINE

Bids to host a future GM&WC are accepted at the GM&WC two (2) years before the proposed conference. Any eligible member organization intending to make a bid to host a future GM&WC must follow these deadlines in order to help give the Conference Structure and Integrity Committee (CSIC) time to review the bid and to ensure it meets the of InterPride’s standing rules, policies, and procedures.

- **A.** Selection of a host GM&WC will be announced at the GM&WC two (2) years before the proposed conference. Suggestions will be accepted from delegates of Member Organizations per the timeline below.

- **B.** Deadline for suggestions for host cities from delegates of Member Organizations shall be submitted to the Executive Director at the conclusion of the Mid-Year Meeting two and a half (2.5) years before the proposed conference.

- **C.** CSIC, Executive Director, and Co-Presidents review suggested host cities, obtain commitments from host city Pride organizations, request proposals for conference venues, and receive and review proposals from conference venues in suggested host city/cities in June, two (2) years before the proposed conference.

- **D.** The Executive Director and CSIC review the proposals and present their recommendation to the Board of Directors by September, two years prior to the GM&WC.

- **E.** Host City selection announced by CSIC, during third and final plenary of the GM&WC two (2) years before the proposed conference.

- **F.** A Planning Subcommittee will be formed following the GM&WC two (2) years before the proposed conference. The committee will be coordinated by CSIC.


### SECTION 3 – GM&WC LOCATION ANNOUNCEMENT REQUIREMENTS

The presentation of the selected host city by CSIC, InterPride Executive Director and the Co-Presidents should include:

- **A.** Endorsements or letters of support or invitation from:
  - **1.** Local and/or state/provincial, and/or national governments;
  - **2.** Tourism offices and/or convention/visitors’ bureau;
  - **3.** 3. Local and/or state/provincial and/or national peer LGBTI+ organizations.

- **B.** Detailed information from the proposed host hotel and conference venue(s), including room rates, meeting room charges, Food and Beverage options, internet service and parking rates.

- **C.** The conference must be at least four (4) days long, include both days of a given weekend and take place no earlier than 15 September and no later than 15 November during the year for which bids are being accepted.

- **D.** General information concerning the host city including language(s) spoken.

- **E.** Information on the local LGBTQIA+ community infrastructure (including entertainment options) and an outline of proposed social activities during the GM&WC. This should include a map showing the city, points of LGBTI+ interest and the location of the proposed host hotel and conference venue(s).

- **F.** A proposed budget or outline of intended financing for the GM&WC. This must include the proposed registration fee(s) for delegates.

- **G.** A schedule of transportation costs that delegates could incur attending the GM&WC in the proposed host city.

- **H.** All figures presented must be in USD (United States dollars).

- **I.** The official language of InterPride is English. If possible, however, the proposal should also offer French, Spanish, and Portuguese translations.


### SECTION 4 – GEOGRAPHIC LOCATION

CSIC will work to ensure that host cities for the GM&WC represent the global membership of the Organization. Whenever possible, the GM&WC should not be held on the same continent in consecutive years.
