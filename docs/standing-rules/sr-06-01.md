## Governance Summary & Commentary
**Revised October 2025:** Governs WorldPride Expressions of Interest, single and joint applications, the `180/150/120/90/70/30`-day vetting timeline, virtual or in-person presentations, 7-month license contract negotiations, and post-event membership requirements.

{: .warning }
> **Governance Audit Errata (`SR 06.01` Internal Contradictions & Numbering Bugs):**
> 1. **Application Fee (`$500` vs `$5,000`):** `Section 1.A.6` (Expression of Interest) requires a non-refundable fee of **`USD $500.00`** *"which will be added to the application payment total"*, whereas `Section 3.A.9` requires a non-refundable fee of **`USD $5,000.00`**. The text should clarify whether `$500` is the Expression of Interest deposit credited toward the `$5,000` full application fee.
> 2. **Self-Contradictory Abstention Rule (`Section 8.B`):** The second sentence of `Section 8.B` is an unremoved editorial note that directly contradicts the first sentence: *"with abstention votes counting in the total votes cast, placed and being treated as a vote against hosting a WorldPride in that year, no provisional license shall be granted. **(Absentions are a null vote and cannot be counted as a no vote.)**"*
> 3. **Section 4 Lettering & Section 12 Duplicates:** In `Section 4`, items `J, K, L` were accidentally relabeled `3, D, E` before jumping to `M, N, O`. In `Section 12`, `B.1` and `C` are duplicate sentences, and `P.` is an empty bullet.

## Adopted Text (`04.06.2026` Edition)

### PURPOSE

To establish the procedures by which applications for the WorldPride title are handled and the requirements and process for licensing. The WorldPride title is granted by the membership.


### SECTION 1 – EXPRESSION OF INTEREST (AT THE GENERAL MEETING & WORLD CONFERENCE)

To establish and maintain all submissions and standing rules, and to ensure the highest level of support to members.


- **A.** SINGLE APPLICATIONS or in Cooperation with another Organization. In order to be eligible to apply for the WorldPride title, an applicant organization must meet the following requirements:
  - **1.** The applicant organization must have attended two (2) of the previous three (3) InterPride annual General Meeting & World Conferences (GM&WC), at least one (1) in person, immediately prior to the application.
  - **2.** Attendance at a GM&WC requires a minimum of attending all plenaries and voting.
  - **3.** The applicant organization must have attended at least one (1) previous WorldPride.
  - **4.** The applicant organization must have no operating deficit exceeding ten (10) percent of revenues in the three (3) calendar year period immediately prior to their application. No waivers may be granted for this requirement.
  - **5.** The applicant organization must propose to hold the WorldPride event at least three (3) years after their application is considered by the membership.
  - **6.** A non-refundable fee of USD $500.00, submitted to the Treasurer of InterPride, must accompany applications, which will be added to the application payment total.
  - **7.** The Co-Presidents will work with the Committee Co-Chairs to determine the fee..


### SECTION 2 – ESTABLISHMENT OF WORLDPRIDE TITLE

pursuant to Resolution 97-01, the organization established the title “WorldPride”, which it may license from time to time, to a full member organization. The event will be based on a cycle of two (2) calendar years. the title should be granted in the spirit of geographic diversity.


### SECTION 3 – REQUIREMENTS FOR APPLICANT ORGANIZATIONS


- **A.** SINGLE APPLICATIONS In order to be eligible to apply for the WorldPride title, an applicant organization must meet the following requirements:
  - **1.** The applicant organization must be a current, full member organization of InterPride and have been in good standing for at least three (3) consecutive calendar years prior to submitting an application. No waivers may be granted for this requirement.
  - **2.** The applicant organization must have organized at least three Pride Events (physical or hybrid) within the previous three (3) calendar years.
  - **3.** The applicant organization must have attended two (2) of the previous three (3) InterPride annual General Meeting & World Conferences (GM&WC), at least one (1) in person, immediately prior to the application.
  - **4.** Attendance at a GM&WC requires a minimum of attending all plenaries and voting.
  - **5.** The applicant organization must have attended at least one (1) previous WorldPride.
  - **6.** The applicant organization must have no operating deficit exceeding ten (10) percent of revenues in the three (3) calendar year period immediately prior to their application. No waivers may be granted for this requirement.
  - **7.** The applicant organization must propose to hold the WorldPride event at least three (3) years after their application is considered by the membership.
  - **8.** The applicant organization must be present at the GM&WC World Conference at which their application is considered.
  - **9.** A non-refundable fee of a total of USD $5,000.00, submitted to the Treasurer of InterPride, must accompany applications.
  - **10.** The Co-Presidents will work with the Committee Co-Chairs to determine the fee.


- **B.** JOINT APPLICATIONS In order to be eligible to apply for the WorldPride title, an applicant organization must meet the following requirements:
  - **1.** Joint applications by multiple organizations must be submitted by a lead organization, which will fulfill the requirements of Section and be accountable to InterPride for the delivery of the event. All applicants must be Full Members of InterPride.
  - **2.** Joint applications must be for an event within a defined geographical area which would allow for reasonable travel by participants.


### SECTION 4 – APPLICATION CONTENT

An application should be structured with clear headings and a table of contents that show where in the document each requirement has been met. Any application for the WorldPride title must include the following:

- **A.** Assignment of a single point of contact with InterPride and authorization for that person to act on behalf of the organization in all matters related to the application. This is valid for a single or a joint application.

- **B.** Name, full postal address, telephone, and email address, and contact person for the applicant organization.

- **C.** A description of the history and legal status of the applicant organization and its structure.

- **D.** A description of the applicant organization’s involvement with InterPride

- **E.** The proposed date(s) and geographic location(s) of the event(s).

- **F.** Details of the proposed program, including:
  - **1.** an opening ceremony;
  - **2.** a march, parade or similar demonstration;
  - **3.** a human rights conference on various aspects/issues of LGBTQI+ life;
  - **4.** a closing ceremony;
  - **5.** an InterPride Membership Reception, co-designed with InterPride; and
  - **6.** a program including affordable and accessible events.

- **G.** A full description of the political context of the program, its projected impact on a global scale, a mission statement and anticipated measurable outcomes.

- **H.** A description of the special cultural or social reasons for hosting the event.

- **I.** A business plan for the event that includes the following:
  - **1.** a proposed budget;
  - **2.** current organizational structure and a staffing plan/timeline that will be followed in the event that the applicant is selected; and
  - **3.** a list and description of key personnel who will be responsible for the delivery of the event. Identification of the increased cost of the event relative to a typical year.

- **D.** A realistic strategy for funding the increase identified in 3.I

- **E.** An international marketing plan for the event, including:
  - **1.** a timeline;
  - **2.** format(s) of marketing;
  - **3.** projected costs of marketing; and
  - **4.** key points that will be emphasized in marketing efforts.


- **M.** General information about the host city/region/country, including:
  - **1.** international airport location;
  - **2.** transportation to/from the event(s);
  - **3.** relevant health and safety/security issues; and
  - **4.** accommodation availability and cost(s).

- **N.** A brief description of LGBTI+ life in the host city/region/country.

- **O.** A signed intent-to-bid agreement The application must clearly specify the global dimension of the event and evidence the desire to embrace and respect the participation of the international LGBTQI+ community. The application must also fully comply with this standing rule, along with the Bylaws, other standing rules, and other governing documents of InterPride.


### SECTION 5 – SUPPORTING DOCUMENTS

Applications must be accompanied by as many of the following documents as possible. If any of these documents cannot be obtained, a reason must be given as to why the document(s) is unavailable.

- **A.** Letters of support from:
  - **1.** organizations within the city/region/country where the event(s) will take place exhibiting their support;
  - **2.** government officials (mayor, city council, governor, president, prime minister, etc.);
  - **3.** LGBTQI+ businesses in the city/region/country of the event(s);
  - **4.** media;
  - **5.** prospective sponsors;
  - **6.** local tourism authorities;
  - **7.** travel partners (airlines, hotels, travel agents, car rental agencies, etc.) and other event partners; and
  - **8.** human rights organizations.

- **B.** Attested financial documentation for the prior three years that demonstrate financial stability, including, but not limited to, balance sheets and audited financial statements.


### SECTION 6 – APPLICATION SUBMISSION


- **A.** Applications for WorldPride must be submitted no less than three (3) years in advance of when the event is proposed to take place.

- **B.** Draft applications must be received by the WorldPride Committee Co-Chairs for review no later than 180 days prior to the GM&WC in the year it will be considered. This review is to verify only compliance with the minimum requirements, and not the caliber or quantity or quality of the information provided. The Co-Chairs will provide the applicants with a list of deficiencies to be corrected 150 days before the GM/WC.

- **C.** Revised bids must be received by the WorldPride Committee Co-Chairs no later than 120 days before the GM&WC, and distributed to the Committee for final review. This review is both to verify compliance with the minimum requirements and to evaluate the quality of the application. This distribution of the proposal(s) shall be confidential to the committee and not to be made public until a time determined by the Board. This confidentiality does not apply to the applicant, who may publicize their own application as they wish.

- **D.** No later than 90 days before the GM&WC, the WorldPride Committee will vote on whether to recommend the bid be accepted by the InterPride Board. The Committee’s recommendation will be sent, with the bid and an explanation for the recommendation, to the InterPride Board, and the applicant shall be advised of the Committee's recommendations.

- **E.** No later than 70 days before the GM&WC, the Board shall vote on whether to accept the recommendations of the WorldPride Committee.

- **F.** The Board shall send the successful applications to the Membership no later than 30 days before the GM&WC where they shall vote on whether to grant the provisional WorldPride license.

### SECTION 7 – APPLICATION PRESENTATION


- **A.** Each applicant organization shall be provided an opportunity to make a presentation regarding their application for the WorldPride license during the GM & WC. This presentation can be in person, or virtually.

- **B.** Applicants will be allocated up to 30 minutes for presentation including questions and answers.

- **C.** The applicant organization is responsible for coordinating any technical needs they might have for this presentation with the GM&WC’s Host Committee.

- **D.** Presentations shall be given in English. Simultaneous translation shall be made available by the GM&WC host.


### SECTION 8 – APPLICATION SELECTION


- **A.** An application for the WorldPride title shall be provisionally granted to an applicant organization should two- thirds (2/3) of the full member organizations present and voting at the GM&WC at which their application is presented, vote in favor of its acceptance. The vote shall take place using the process outlined in the Bylaws. This vote will take place by ballot during or following a plenary session of any given GM&WC. The choices to be provided shall be: voting in favor of an applicant organization’s bid, voting to not award the license to any applicant organization, and abstention.

- **B.** Should only one application be considered, and the bid not receive a two-thirds (2/3) super majority vote, with abstention votes counting in the total votes cast, placed and being treated as a vote against hosting a WorldPride in that year, no provisional license shall be granted. (Absentions are a null vote and cannot be counted as a no vote.)

- **C.** When there shall be two (2) or more applications and should no option receive a majority vote, additional votes shall be taken with the application with the lowest vote total being removed from each subsequent round of voting until a majority vote for an option is achieved. Abstention votes will count in the total and be treated as a vote against hosting a WorldPride in that year. The application achieving a simple majority vote (50%) shall be awarded the provisional license.


### SECTION 9 – REQUIREMENT SUSPENSION

Organizations from ODA-eligible countries applying to host a WorldPride event may request relief for part of the licensing fee. The organization must demonstrate a compelling reason for this request. The request should be submitted to the WorldPride Committee as part of their bid and may only be granted by the Board.


### SECTION 10 – LICENSURE – GENERAL


- **A.** The provisional license of the WorldPride title is contingent on the host organization (successful applicant) and InterPride entering into a signed licensure agreement containing the terms and conditions upon which the usage of the WorldPride title is authorized by the Organization.

- **B.** Negotiations with the successful applicant will follow the award of the provisional title for a period not to exceed seven (7) months. InterPride will be represented by its legal counsel with the support of a negotiating team of the Co-Presidents and WorldPride Committee Co-Chairs.

- **C.** The negotiating team will negotiate an agreement that is consistent with the application. Any material changes, particularly related to the size, budget, or quality of the event will require approval from the Board.

- **D.** If an agreement is not successfully negotiated within seven (7) months of the award, the award will expire unless an extension is granted by the Board of InterPride. If no extension is granted, the Board will determine whether to grant the license to the second place bidder or not to have a WorldPride event in the proposed year.


### SECTION 11 – WORLDPRIDE HUMAN RIGHTS CONFERENCE

The WorldPride Host organization will collaborate with the InterPride Human Rights and Diversity Committee on the development, production and programming of the WorldPride Human Rights Conference.


### SECTION 12 – LICENSURE – SPECIFICS OF AGREEMENT

The following terms should be incorporated into the licensure agreement:

- **A.** The event must be consistent with the one presented in the application;

- **B.** The event name will include “WorldPride”, the name of the host city, and the year it will be hosted; 1. In the case of joint applications, the name of a shared region or the lead applicant city may be used;

- **C.** In the case of joint applications, the name of a shared region or the lead applicant city may be used;

- **D.** An accessible private viewing area for InterPride members to attend opening and closing ceremonies with a clear unobstructed view of the event(s);

- **E.** Any InterPride contingents in any parade or march, shall be placed near the front of the event(s) in the formation.

- **F.** A program including affordable and accessible events;

- **G.** A program of accountability between the host organization and InterPride;

- **H.** A schedule for reporting: a) of the progress of the event(s) to the WorldPride Committee; b) the financial status of the Host(s) committee.

- **I.** In-person reporting of the progress of the event(s) to the Membership at the World Conferences leading up to the event(s);

- **J.** A requirement for a post-WorldPride presentation at a GM&WC following their event(s), including a detailed written report;

- **K.** A requirement that host organizations maintain full member status with the Organization for a period of two (2) years following the event(s).

- **L.** Payment of a non-refundable licensure fee, with a detailed explanation of any agreed upon installment plan;

- **M.** A percentage of the event(s) revenue will be paid to InterPride;

- **N.** A percentage of any merchandising revenue of the event(s) will be paid to InterPride;

- **O.** An agreement that the licensee will not disparage InterPride before, during or after the event(s).

- **P.** A statement clarifying the length and scope of the license.

- **Q.** The ability for InterPride to withdraw the WorldPride title and revoke licensure should the host organization be found to be in violation of the licensure agreement.
