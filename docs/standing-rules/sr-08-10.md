## Adopted Text (`04.06.2026` Edition)

- **A.** PURPOSE AND SCOPE This policy ensures decisions and actions by InterPride officers, board members, volunteers, and contractors are made in the best interest of the organisation. It applies to all individuals involved in InterPride’s governance and operations.


- **B.** DEFINITION OF CONFLICT OF INTEREST A conflict of interest is a situation or matter in which an officer has an interest that may compromise their obligations to the organisation or allow them to derive personal benefit from decisions made in their official capacity. Conflicts include real, perceived, or potential interests that may influence actions or decisions. Examples include:
  - Using confidential knowledge for personal or professional gain
  - Financial interests in entities doing business with InterPride
  - Involvement in decisions concerning close associates or relatives
  - Accepting gifts that influence decisions

- **C.** DISCLOSURE REQUIREMENTS All Board Members, Officers, Committee Chairs, and contractors must:
  - Complete an annual Conflict of Interest Disclosure Form
  - Declare new conflicts as soon as they arise
  - Disclose at the beginning of any meeting if they are conflicted
  - Submit disclosures for inclusion in the Conflict Register

- **D.** ASSESSMENT AND MANAGEMENT Conflicts will be reviewed by the Risk & Compliance subcommittee of Governance, with input from the Governance Committee and relevant leadership. Actions may include:
  - Recusal from votes or discussions
  - Formal documentation of mitigation actions
  - Escalation to the Co-Presidents if required

- **E.** CONFIDENTIALITY AND PRIVACY All disclosures and conflict management processes are confidential. Information will be shared on a strict need-to-know basis and securely stored per GDPR and U.S. privacy law. Breaches must be reported to the Governance Committee and Co-Presidents immediately.


- **F.** CONSEQUENCES OF NON-COMPLIANCE Failure to disclose or manage conflicts appropriately may lead to consequences as outlined by the Code of Ethics and Conduct (Standing Rule 08.04):
  - Removal from the Board, Committees, or Taskforces
  - Termination of contracts or volunteer roles
  - Reputational and legal consequences for InterPride

- **G.** COMPLIANCE AND REVIEW This policy supersedes all existing policies and or statements pertaining to Conflicts of Interests. This policy will be reviewed annually by the Governance and Risk Committees. All changes require Board approval. Updates must be circulated to all relevant stakeholders within 30 days.


- **H.** TRAINING AND AWARENESS This All incoming Board members, staff, and committee chairs will receive training during onboarding. Refresher sessions will be provided annually during the General Meeting & World Conference. The policy is included in the Board handbook, Code of Ethics & Conduct, and referenced in the GAC and Staff Agreements.
