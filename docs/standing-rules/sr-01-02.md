## Adopted Text (`04.06.2026` Edition)

### PURPOSE

To establish the mandate and membership requirements for the Conference Structure and Integrity Committee (CSIC).


### SECTION 1 – COMMITTEE ESTABLISHMENT

The Board of Directors hereby establishes the Conference Structure and Integrity Committee.


### SECTION 2 – COMMITTEE MANDATE

CSIC is tasked with the following areas of specific responsibility and/or oversight:

- **A.** Leading the planning of the annual General Meeting & World Conference, herein referred to as the GM&WC, to be made up of:
  - **1.** Representative(s) of the Host organization in the selected host city
  - **2.** InterPride Co-President(s)
  - **3.** CSIC members
  - **4.** CSIC co-chairs

- **B.** Overseeing compliance by the Planning Committee of the GM&WC with the rules and procedures established by the Board of Directors for the organization and conduct of the GM&WC.

- **C.** Reviewing and providing recommendations to the Board of Directors regarding the rules and procedures pertaining to the organization and conduct of the GM&WC.


### SECTION 3 – COMMITTEE MEMBERSHIP


- **A.** CSIC’s membership shall be open to all individuals from member organizations.
  - **1.** At least one representative from member organizations selected to host a future GM&WC shall be required to participate in CSIC.
  - **2.** Representatives from prior hosts of the GM&WC are highly encouraged to continue their participation on the committee once their hosting responsibilities are completed. B. TThe Committee

- **B.** The Committee chair (s) shall be subject to approval by a majority vote of the Board of Directors present and voting on such an appointment.

- **C.** The list of the membership for CSIC shall be maintained by the chair(s) of CSIC and transmitted to the Secretary as members join or depart.
