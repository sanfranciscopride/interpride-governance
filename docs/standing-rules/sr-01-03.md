## Adopted Text (`04.06.2026` Edition)

### PURPOSE

The purpose of the Emeritus Council (EC) shall be to provide advice and support to the InterPride Board on matters related to the workings of the Organization and on topics related to the community served by the Organization. In retaining the institutional and community knowledge of the EC members, the hope is that maintaining these assets will be helpful to keeping InterPride successful and sustain its financial stability.


### SECTION 1 – ROLE AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Their main responsibility is to support the Co-Presidents and/or the Board of Directors of InterPride as a whole. As non-voting members, the EC members are welcome to attend Board meetings, including closed sessions. Emeritus Council members serve in an advisory capacity, and shall have a non-voting seat on the Board of Directors of InterPride. This seat will be selected by the Council and their representative will be chosen annually. Emeritus Council members may have special roles during official events or at the General Meeting & World Conference (GM&WC). Emeritus Council members are automatically members of the Alumni Committee and can be considered as historians for InterPride. As decided by the Co-Presidents from time to time, an EC member might be the focus of or contributor for an interview, podcast, marketing or educational purposes. As advisors, Emeritus Council members are expected to:

- **A.** Participate in the two full Emeritus Council meetings each year

- **B.** Participate when requested at special meetings of the InterPride Board

- **C.** Be available individually and/or as a group to members of the Board or to Committee Co-Chairs

- **D.** Respond in a timely manner to all queries from the InterPride Board

- **E.** Participate in group responses from the Emeritus Council to queries from the InterPride Board submitted to the EC or in part thereof

- **F.** Contribute knowledge and know-how in the best interest of InterPride, to ensure its sustainability (operationally and financially)


### SECTION 2 – MEETINGS OF THE EMERITUS COUNCIL

The Emeritus Council shall meet at least twice a year, at dates selected by the Emeritus Council. The Emeritus Council may be called to meet exceptionally, in-person or remotely, by a majority of its members. Remote participation through virtual means shall be permitted with advance notification. Emeritus Council members shall be called to meet via electronic and paper invitations, no later than one month in advance of the date of the meeting (one week in the case of an exceptional meeting). The InterPride Board shall be in charge of securing a venue to hold each Emeritus Council meeting, if in-person, as well as a virtual meeting platform.


### SECTION 3 – COMPOSITION

It is agreed that the Co-Presidents shall initiate the initial creation of the Emeritus Council. Number of Members: No less than seven (7) and no more than twenty-one (21) members. Terms: A three-year term, with initial appointments as follows:
  - Up to seven (7) members shall be brought on-board in 2023
  - Up to seven (7) additional members shall be brought on-board in 2024
  - Up to seven (7) additional members shall be brought on-board in 2025

Designation: The Emeritus Council may appoint, by simple majority, new members at the annual General Meeting & World Conference of InterPride. Partial/Incomplete Terms: Vacancies due to resignation, death, removal, or other causes, shall not be filled until the next cycle of designation.


### SECTION 5 – ELIGIBILITY

The InterPride Board officer’s contributions throughout their tenure will have proven invaluable due to their institutional knowledge and history of the organization. Any past InterPride Officer and/or Co-Chair, who left in good standing, was elected or appointed, and has served for no less than ten (10) years total on the below listed roles is eligible to be a member of the Emeritus Council. The Emeritus Council shall work to ensure diversity of gender, race, sexuality, and location within the Emeritus Council. Consideration for other eligible candidates can be made by a combination of the following; however, must include serving as an officer of the Board for a minimum of 3 years.
  - Board member
  - Past Regional Directors
  - Global Advisory Council
  - Chair or Co-Chair of Committees, Task Forces, or Working Groups Any Board member who has been removed for cause or has resigned pending a removal vote and/or caused detrimental harm to InterPride, shall not be allowed to serve. Exceptions can be made at the discretion of the Emeritus Council.


### SECTION 6 – REMOVAL

Emeritus Council members may be removed by a majority vote of the Emeritus Council for cause, upon proposal from any Emeritus Council member, by a simple majority vote. Examples of justifiable causes include, but are not limited to:
  - Lack of participation in EC activities
  - Undeclared conflicts of interest
  - Public disparagement of the organization
  - Creating more harm than good Once removed, the individual cannot be reconsidered for reappointment.


### SECTION 6 – FINANCIAL COMPENSATION

Members of the Emeritus Council shall not receive financial compensation for their participation or performance of their duties in this role. Members of the Emeritus Council shall not be reimbursed for expenses incurred by their participation in the activities of the Emeritus Council or InterPride, outside of a budget or approval of the Board.
