## Adopted Text (`04.06.2026` Edition)

### PURPOSE

To establish Organization policy regarding the retention of a tax preparer in the Organization’s State of Incorporation.


### SECTION 1 – RETENTION OF AUDITOR IN STATE OF INCORPORATION

The Organization shall retain an auditor in its State of Incorporation to prepare all necessary tax related documents required and necessary to the Organization maintaining its nonprofit status.
