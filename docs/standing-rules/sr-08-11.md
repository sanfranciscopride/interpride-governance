## Adopted Text (`04.06.2026` Edition)

### PURPOSE

This InterPride requires all Board members, officers, contractors, employees, members, and volunteers to uphold the highest ethical and legal standards in their duties. As a Texas-incorporated nonprofit with global operations, InterPride is subject to the Texas Business Organizations Code Chapter 22 governing nonprofit corporations, as well as applicable federal and international laws. This policy ensures compliance with the Sarbanes-Oxley Act (U.S. federal law) and applicable whistleblower protections under Texas Labor Code §554.002.


### SECTION 1 REPORTING RESPONSIBILITY

This Whistleblower Policy encourages all individuals affiliated with InterPride to report misconduct, unethical behaviour, or legal violations affecting the organisation. Texas Business Organizations Code §22.353 mandates that nonprofit organisations implement conflict of interest and ethics policies, and this policy aligns with those requirements. Mandatory Reporting Obligation: All Interpride Board members, officers, contractors, employees, Committee Chairs and Committee members, general members, and volunteers must promptly report any known or suspected ethical breaches, violations of law, or breaches of InterPride’s governing documents. Reports should be submitted through InterPride’s Whistleblower Reporting Channels, as outlined in Section 2.


### SECTION 2 REPORTING PROCEDURE

Reporting Channels
  - Primary Contact: Reports should be submitted via InterPride’s Whistleblower Hotline, a secure and independent reporting channel to ensure impartiality.
  - To ensure a secure, confidential, and accessible reporting process, InterPride has implemented a dedicated whistleblower platform. Reports can be submitted through [PLATFORM NAME], a secure and anonymous reporting tool available 24/7 worldwide. How to Report a Concern:
  - Access the platform via a provided link or QR code posted on the website.
  - Choose to remain anonymous or provide your details.
  - Submit your concern through a secure online form.
  - The Governance Co-Chairs will review and respond as per our investigation procedures All reports are encrypted and managed in accordance with global best practices, ensuring whistleblower protection and compliance with legal frameworks. Escalation Process If the complainant is uncomfortable reporting to the Co-Presidents, they may contact:
  - Governance Co-Chairs or their duly authorised representative.
  - Any external, third-party whistleblower reporting services, as applicable. Written Documentation All complaints must be documented in writing. Anonymous complaints will be accepted if they contain sufficient detail for investigation. Legal Compliance In compliance with Texas Labor Code §554.002, InterPride prohibits interference or intimidation against individuals reporting good-faith concerns.


### SECTION 3 ACCOUNTING AND AUDITING MATTERS

Financial & Auditing Concerns This policy aligns with Sarbanes-Oxley Act 2002, which prohibits retaliation against whistleblowers reporting financial fraud.
  - Any concerns regarding accounting irregularities, financial mismanagement, or fraud must be reported immediately to:
  - InterPride’s Treasurer and Finance Committee Co-Chairs copying in the Co-Presidents
  - External auditors (if applicable)
  - Relevant legal authorities if required by law in consultation with InterPride Legal Counsel.

### SECTION 4 ACTING IN GOOD FAITH

Good-Faith Reporting Standard This policy is governed by Texas Civil Practice & Remedies Code §27.001 (Anti-SLAPP Statute), which protects individuals against retaliatory lawsuits for reporting misconduct:
  - Whistleblowers must have a reasonable belief that the reported conduct constitutes an ethical, legal, or policy violation.
  - False or malicious reports may result in disciplinary action, including membership suspension or removal from leadership roles.


### SECTION 5 NO RETALIATION

It is contrary to the values of InterPride for anyone to retaliate against any Board member, officer, contractor, employee, member, or volunteer who, in good faith, reports an ethics or conduct violation, or a suspected violation of law, such as a complaint of discrimination, or suspected fraud, or suspected violation of any regulation governing the operations of InterPride. Anyone or any group who retaliates against someone who has reported a violation in good faith is subject to discipline up to and including termination of role and/or membership. Zero Tolerance for Retaliation:
  - Retaliation against whistleblowers is strictly prohibited.
  - Violations may result in legal action, removal from the organization, or other disciplinary measures.
  - This protection is in accordance with Texas Labor Code §554.003, which provides remedies for individuals subjected to retaliatory actions.


### SECTION 6 CONFIDENTIALITY

Violations or suspected violations may be submitted on a confidential basis by the complainant. Reports of violations or suspected violations will be kept confidential to the extent possible, consistent with the need to conduct an adequate investigation.


### SECTION 7 HANDLING OF REPORTED VIOLATIONS

The Governance Co-Chairs (or their duly appointed representative) will notify the person who submitted a complaint acknowledging receipt of the reported violation or suspected violation. All reports will be promptly investigated within fourteen (14) days and appropriate corrective action will be taken, if warranted by the investigation.
