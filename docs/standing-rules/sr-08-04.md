# SR 08.04 – CODE OF ETHICS AND CONDUCT

## Governance Summary & Commentary
**Revised March 2024 / Updated October 2025:** Extends the **Code of Ethics and Conduct** to all attendees and participants at any official InterPride activity. Replaces the old 1-sentence violation clause with a **4-Step Progressive Discipline Process** (`Section 3.A`) administered by the **Governance Committee Co-Chairs**, plus immediate Step 3 suspension authority (`Section 3.B`) for illegal, neglectful, or reputation-damaging conduct.

## Adopted Text (`04.06.2026` Edition)

### PURPOSE

InterPride seeks to maintain a professional and ethical environment. It is imperative that all Board Members, Global Advisory Council Members (GACs), Members, Individuals, Staff/Contractors/Consultants, and Officers conduct themselves in a professional manner, strive to avoid conflicts of interest/personal advantage, protect our reputation and integrity, and to operate the organization/their duties in an open and transparent manner, with accountability and integrity. By adhering to a Code of Ethics and Conduct, the Board GACs, Members, Individuals, Staff, and Officers all base their conduct on the core values (Values Statement) and principles of the mission statement of InterPride. Whether through membership, elected or assigned roles, or attendance as a participant of any official InterPride activity (conferences, meetings, events), the Code of Ethics and Conduct, with the Values Statement and other governing documents, policies, and procedures, will apply equally. The Code of Ethics and Conduct includes, but is not limited to, the mission of the organization, its system of governance, compliance with laws and regulations, responsible stewardship of funds and donations, open disclosure of information, regular review of programs for effectiveness, and integrity in both operation and fundraising methods.


### SECTION 1 – CODE OF ETHICS AND CONDUCT EXPECTATIONS

We hold true:
  - Respect for the communities we work with and serve.
  - Integrity in our actions.
  - Responsibility for our decisions and their consequences.

We are committed to:
  - Acting honestly, truthfully, and with integrity in all our transactions and dealings
  - Avoiding conflicts of interest
  - Appropriately handling actual or apparent conflicts of interest
  - Treating our Solidarity and Scholarship grantees fairly
  - Treating every individual with dignity and respect
  - Treating our Board members and Staff with fairness, good faith, and provide conditions that safeguard their rights and welfare
  - Acting responsibly toward the communities in which we work and for the benefit of the communities that we serve
  - Being responsible, transparent, and accountable for all of our actions; and
  - Improving accountability, transparency, ethical conduct, and effectiveness.


### SECTION 2 – CODE OF ETHICS AND CONDUCT GUIDELINES


- **A.** ACTIONS – GACs, Members, Individuals, Staff, and Officers are expected to act in a manner that will reflect a positive image for the Organization, fulfill their duties under the Bylaws, and other policies and procedures that may be adopted, from time to time, by the Membership, the Board of Directors, or the Officers; and work to further the Organization’s mission, vision, and goals.

- **B.** INTENTIONS – No GACs, Members, Individuals, Staff, and Officers shall knowingly or willfully violate the Articles of Incorporation, Bylaws, and other policies and procedures that may be adopted, from time to time, by the Membership, the Board, or the Officers.


- **C.** CONFLICTS OF INTEREST – Any GACs, Members, Officers, Individuals, and Staff/Contractors/Consultants that find themselves a party to a conflict of interest or the appearance thereof, with regards to any matter before the Board or Officers, shall immediately and fully disclose the nature of the conflict of interest or appearance thereof to the appropriate body and recuse themselves from attempting to influence the outcome and/or voting on such a matter. This also applies to all candidates for GAC and Officer positions.

- **D.** INFLUENCE – No GACs, Members, Officers, Individuals, and Staff/Contractors/Consultants shall use their membership, position, title, or association with the Organization to secure special privileges or exemptions for themselves personally with third parties/other organizations.

- **E.** COMPENSATION – No GACs, Members, Officers, Individuals, and Staff/Contractors/Consultants shall directly or indirectly receive or agree to receive any compensation, gift, reward, or gratuity valued at over fifty ($50) USD from any source for any matter connected with or related to their duties. This provision shall not apply in the following circumstances:
  - **1.** Regarding reimbursement or other monies from the Organization related to the exercise of a GAC or Officer’s duties, as defined by the Bylaws and other policies and procedures that may be adopted, from time to time, by the Membership, the Board of Directors, or the Officers; and
  - **2.** Regarding items provided to all attendees of a conference or other event related to the exercise of a GAC or Officer’s duties, as defined by the Bylaws and other policies and procedures that may be adopted, from time to time, by the Membership, the Board of Directors, or the Officers.

- **F.** COMMITMENT – No GACs, Members, Officers, Individuals, and Staff/Contractors/Consultants shall directly or indirectly promise or agree to commit any expense for another member, delegate, scholarship awardee, or a vendor on behalf of the Organization.

- **G.** EXPECTATIONS – GACs, Members, Officers, Individuals, and Staff/Contractors/consultants are expected to uphold the Organization’s Equal Opportunity and Respect policy (Standing Rule 03.03, Section 1) as it applies to the execution of their duties and responsibilities. Furthermore, they are expected to treat all Member representatives, their fellow GACs, Members, Officers, Individuals, and Staff/Contractors/ consultants; and the general public, in a respectful manner at all times; and working to foster an open and welcoming environment during meetings, events, and other functions of the Organization.

- **H.** NON-DISPARAGEMENT – InterPride expects all individuals acting in any official capacity for the Organization, including members of the Board of Directors, Committees, Regional Representatives, Global Advisory Council, volunteers, contractors, and affiliates, to conduct themselves with respect, professionalism, and in alignment with InterPride’s values of integrity and inclusion. No person shall make, publish, or cause to be made or published, any statement, whether oral, written, digital, or otherwise communicated, that could reasonably be interpreted as disparaging, defamatory, or damaging to the reputation, mission, or operations of InterPride, its Board, members, partners, or representatives. Likewise, InterPride shall not issue or authorize any communication that could reasonably be construed to disparage or defame any individual serving, volunteering, or contracting with the Organization. This section does not restrict constructive dialogue, open expression of diverse viewpoints, or the reporting of misconduct, discrimination, harassment, or unlawful acts through appropriate organizational or legal channels. All communications made in connection with InterPride, whether in meetings, correspondence, or public forum, shall reflect courtesy, integrity, and mutual respect consistent with the standards set forth in this Code of Conduct and in the InterPride Values Statement.


### SECTION 3 – VIOLATIONS

In an attempt to correct or deter unacceptable conduct and other verifiable violations of the Code of Ethics and Conduct, Values Statement, and any other agreements or governing documents of the Organization, the Governance Committee will provide the Subject(s) an opportunity to modify their behavior.

- **A.** PROCESS – Progressive notice comprised of the following steps, which we are recommending be followed in the sequence list below, as applicable:
  - **1.** First Violation – A verbal reminder will be given to the Subject(s), if the situation is applicable, (else the Governance Co-Chairs can issue one via email). This can be done by either of the Co-Presidents, Governance Co-Chairs, the person(s) chairing a meeting or producing an event where the incident occurred, or anyone in an InterPride leadership role attendant at the time, making sure to report it to the Governance Co-Chairs within 24 hours. The Governance Committee, in concert with the Leadership Development subcommittee of Governance, will provide the Subject(s) with documentation that outlines unacceptable violations as guidance, and offer supportive education designed to help them understand how to avoid repeating such.
  - **2.** Second Violation – A written warning will be issued, within 24 hours of the incident or subsequent report filed, to the Subject(s) by the Governance Co-Chairs, or a complaint is filed with them and the Co- Presidents*. The warning will contain the reason(s) for this disciplinary action, with notice of possible suspension if the violation(s) continue. *In the case of a violation report about a Co-President, that Co-President should be omitted from receipt of the initial report, which will then be followed up on by Governance.
  - **3.** Third Violation – Suspension or removal of the Subject(s) from participation from the activities directed affected, as determined by the Governance Co-Chairs, and with input from the body directly overseeing that area. This could be:

- **A.** The Board of Directors — Board meetings, Organization representative roles, Organization events (such as the General Meeting & World Conference).

- **B.** Committee Co-Chairs — Board Committees, workgroups, taskforces, and their related meetings or events.

- **C.** Global Advisory Council Board — Their meetings and/or related events. The Subject(s) will be notified of the reason(s)s for suspension or removal and reminded of the potential for termination if there is another violation. They will also be given a 7-day period in which they can appeal the decision; however, the final decision will rest with the Board of Directors, pending the Governance Committee’s recommendation(s).
  - **4.**  Final Violation — Removal of the Subject(s) from their role and termination of any and all participation will be voted on by the Board of Directors. Discussion and vote will be conducted by the Governance Co-Chairs, in an Executive Session meeting of the Board, and all information will be kept confidential. The Subject(s). Removal can include the revocation of their role as a representative for any InterPride member organization, and/or roles within InterPride. If the member organization(s) associated with the Subject(s) are/were party to the violations, their membership could also be suspended or terminated.

- **B.** Additional Consequences — In the event of illegal, neglectful, inappropriate, or any other behavior that may negatively impact the reputation of InterPride, by Member organization representatives, InterPride officers, committee co-chairs, Board members, or other persons designated to represent InterPride, Governance may immediately implement Step 3, Suspension or Removal. Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR) may be appropriate, depending on the recommended actions of the situation (e.g. counseling, demotion, censure, etc.).
