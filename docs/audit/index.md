## Executive Overview

During the ingestion and line-by-line longitudinal comparison of all **182 governing PDFs** (`2022–2026`), we identified **11 specific textual corruptions, unreplaced placeholders, internal contradictions, and cross-document conflicts** in the current `2025–2026` governing documents.

Every single item below is also surfaced inline via a **⚠️ Governance Audit Errata** callout on the exact article or rule page where it occurs, complete with ready-to-adopt redline language following the Governance Committee's **September 30, 2026** review deadline.
