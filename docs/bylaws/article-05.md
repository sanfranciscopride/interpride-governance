## Governance Summary & Commentary
Establishes the **Board of Directors** composition, Officer roles, powers, fiduciary duties, meetings, quorum, nomination qualifications, election procedures, vacancy replacements, terms of office, term limits, and removal/disciplinary procedures.

- **2022 → 2023 → 2024 Amendments:**
  - Split `VP of Diversity, Inclusion, and Accessibility` into **`Up to One (1) VP of Diversity and Inclusion`** and **`Up to One (1) VP of Accessibility`** (Pride Winnipeg Amendment, 2023).
  - Added **`One (1) non-voting Emeritus Council representative`** (`09.29.2023_V1` / `01.20.2024`).
- **2024 → 2025 Amendments (`12.12.2025`):**
  - **Co-President Gender Representation (`Section 1`):** Updated to: *"Three (3) Co-Presidents; one (1) position held by a woman, one (1) position held by a man, and one (1) position held by a non-binary or gender diverse person; two (2) of the three (3) seats must be filled at all times."*
  - **Nominee Qualifications (`Section 12.A`, Amended Dec 6, 2024):** Replaced the old requirement of attending 1 of the 2 prior GM&WCs with:
    1. Must be present at the GM&WC (in-person or virtually);
    2. Must have **actively served on at least one (1) InterPride committee or working group for at least one (1) full year prior** (defined as **$\ge 70\%$ meeting attendance**);
    3. Must have **proven leadership experience** (Co-Chair/Project Lead on a committee/working group OR leadership role in their member Pride/2SLGBTQIA+ organization).
  - **Elections (`Section 13.E`):** Replaced *"Methods & Standards Committee"* with *"the body responsible for conducting the election"*.
  - **Removal (`Section 18.G`):** Added explicit ground for removal: *"Loss of endorsement and/or affiliation, or a lapse in membership fees past 60 days from the start of the year."*

## Adopted Text (`12.12.2025` Edition)

### SECTION 1 COMPOSITION

  - Three (3) Co-Presidents; one (1) position held by a woman, one (1) position held by a man, and one (1) position held by a non-binary or gender diverse person; two (2) of the three (3) seats must be filled at all times.
  - Up to One (1) Vice President of Diversity and Inclusion
  - Up to One (1) Vice President of Accessibility
  - One (1) Vice President of Membership Services
  - Four (4) Vice Presidents of Global Outreach
  - One (1) Secretary
  - One (1) Treasurer
  - One (1) Emeritus Council member (non-voting)
  - One (1) Representative for each Pride Platform with a signed partnership agreement with the Organization
  - Three (3) members from the Global Advisory Council (GAC) – non-Pride Platform regions, as described in Article VI.


### SECTION 2 GENDER PARITY

In seeking to fill these positions that are non-gender specific, the Organization shall strive to achieve the goal of gender parity, including trans and non-binary identities.


### SECTION 3 ORGANIZATIONAL REPRESENTATION

In seeking to fill these positions, the Organization shall strive to achieve a balance of representation from members self-identified as large, medium, and small organizations.


### SECTION 4 RESPONSIBILITIES OF PRIDE PLATFORM BOARD MEMBERS

The Pride Platform Board Members are tasked with:

- **A.** Liaising with the Pride Platform members with which they are elected to represent;

- **B.** Attending their Pride Platform conference, if one is scheduled, as well as any local Pride events possible;

- **C.** Working to recruit Pride organizations within the Pride Platform to which they are elected.;

- **D.** Serving on at least one committee, subcommittee, or workgroup of the InterPride Board;

- **E.** Exercising and performing such other duties and responsibilities as may, from time to time, be assigned by the IP Board, Bylaws, Standing Rules, and the other governing documents of the Organization;

- **F.** Attending InterPride Board of Directors meetings and providing a Platform report.


### SECTION 5 GEOGRAPHIC REPRESENTATION

In seeking to fill these positions, the Organization shall strive to achieve balanced geographic representation.


### SECTION 6 POWERS

The Board shall have authority to take any action necessary to conduct the business of the Organization. The Board shall advise the Global Advisory Council of all actions it takes on InterPride’s behalf within thirty (30) days of the said action being taken.

### SECTION 7 DUTY TO RESPOND

The Board must respond to all recommendations and reports received from the Global Advisory Council within sixty (60) days. Recommendations can be accepted with implementation or noted with no implementation.


### SECTION 8 MULTIPLE POSITIONS ON THE BOARD PROHIBITED

A person shall only serve in one position on the Board at any given time.


### SECTION 9 MEETINGS


- **A.** The Board will meet at least three (3) times during a GM, once prior to the first plenary session, once between the first and final plenary sessions, once after the final plenary session, as well as at any other time deemed necessary.

- **B.** Meetings of the Board may be called by the Presidents of the Organization, by a majority vote of the Global Advisory Council, or by a quorum of the Board.

- **C.** Meetings may take place or actions may be authorized either in person or by electronic means. Votes conducted by electronic means must receive at least a number of votes equal to quorum in order to be valid.

- **D.** A minimum of ten (10) days’ notice shall be required for any meeting conducted by electronic means. A minimum of thirty (30) days’ notice shall be required for any meeting conducted in person.

- **E.** Urgent decisions can be made by the Co-Presidents, and meetings can be called by the Co- Presidents with 48 hours’ notice.


### SECTION 10 VOTING


- **A.** Each member of the Board shall be entitled to a single vote.

- **B.** Voting shall be conducted by roll-call unless unanimous consent shall exist on a given matter of business.


### SECTION 11 REIMBURSEMENT OF EXPENSES

Reasonable expenses for duties fulfilled by a member of the Board shall be reimbursable if approved by a majority vote of the Board.


### SECTION 12 ELECTIONS – NOMINATIONS TO OFFICE


- **A.** Nominees to the Board shall be present at the GM&WC (whether in-person or virtual) at which they are nominated and must also have actively servedon at least one (1) InterPride committee or working group for at least one (1) full year prior to the GM&WC at which the vote will take place, AND has proven leadership experience.
  - **1.** Active service requires attendance at seventy (70) percent of the meetings held by the committee during the year preceding their nomination.
  - **2.** Proven leadership experience includes but is not limited to serving as a co-chair or taking the lead on a project within the committee OR holding a leadership role within their member Pride or 2SLGBTQIA+ organization during the year preceding their nomination.

- **B.** In the event that a vacancy arises requiring a replacement, the Board will appoint a suitable replacement. The appointee must be affiliated with and endorsed by a Full Member Organization in good standing. The appointment will be made through a motion passed by a majority of those present and voting at a duly convened meeting of the Board.

- **C.** Individuals may only accept nomination to one office at one time.

- **D.** Nominees must be able to serve the full term of the office.

### SECTION 13 ELECTIONS – VOTING


- **A.** The election of Officers shall be done by the membership of the organization.

- **B.** Voting shall take place by signed or electronic ballot during the final plenary session of any given General meeting.

- **C.** Should only one person stand for election for any given office, the ballot shall provide an option to hold the position vacant.

- **D.** The Membership shall vote on the Board using the process outlined in the Bylaws, Article VIII, Section 3, Subsection A.

- **E.** Prior to the commencement of voting, the body responsible for conducting the election shall appoint two (2) or more delegates to supervise the election process. These delegates must not be a member of the Board or affiliated with the member organizations from which the nominees are affiliated.

- **F.** The nominee who receives a majority of votes cast by those present and voting shall be elected. In elections where there is only one candidate standing for election and the “vacant” selection shall receive a majority, the position shall be held vacant until such time as the Board shall appoint a replacement per Section 14 of this Article.

- **G.** In the event of a tie, the voting process shall be repeated until the tie is broken.

- **H.** At no time shall more than one (1) person from any given Full Member organization serve on the Board.


### SECTION 14 REPLACEMENT


- **A.** The incoming Officers' terms commence thirty (30) days after the General Meeting & World Conference (GM&WC) at which they were nominated has closed. The outgoing officers' terms conclude 30 days after the GM&WC has closed, following the last plenary.

- **B.** Board members appointed to fill a vacancy will serve until the next GM&WC, then may run for reelection for the remainder of the term being filled. This will not count toward the maximum number of full terms possible specified in Article V, Section 15-C. majority of those present and voting at a joint meeting of the Board and the Global Advisory Council.


### SECTION 15 TERM OF OFFICE


- **A.** The incoming Officers' terms commence thirty (30) days after the General Meeting & World Conference (GM&WC) at which they were elected. The outgoing officers' terms conclude 30 days after GM&WC.

- **B.** The election of officers shall be staggered over two GMs at each GM&WC.

- **C.** No Officer shall serve more than two (2) consecutive elected terms in the same Office on the Board.

- **D.** All Board positions are elected for three (3)- year terms.


### SECTION 16 DUTIES & RESPONSIBILITIES


- **A.** The duties and responsibilities of each officer and director of the Board will be defined in a Standing Rule of the Organization.


### SECTION 17 SPECIAL MEETINGS


- **A.** A special meeting of the Board may be called by four (4) of its members.

- **B.** Special meetings may take place or actions may be authorized either in-person or by electronic means. Votes conducted by electronic means must receive at least a number of votes equal to quorum in order to be valid.

- **C.** A minimum of seventy-two (72) hour hours’ notice shall be required for any special meeting conducted by electronic means. A minimum of thirty (30) days’ notice shall be required for any special meeting conducted in person.

### SECTION 18 REMOVAL OF ELECTED OR APPOINTED DELEGATES AND OFFICERS; COMMITTEE MEMBERS

AND CO-CHAIRS; BOARD, EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE AND GAC MEMBERS; CONTRACTORS AND CONSULTANTS An individual shall be removed from their position when one or more of the following conditions shall have occurred:

- **A.** A conviction for a criminal offense or felony regarding matters of theft and/or financial probity;

- **B.** A conviction for a criminal offense or felony not regarding matters of theft and/or financial probity, provided that the Board shall have the option to override such removal or restriction by a two-thirds majority vote of those present and voting within thirty (30) days of Board notification of said conviction, based on a written note distributed to the membership before the vote and made public after the vote. Current and historical criminal convictions related to the criminalization of LGBTIQ+ status and practices, gender identity and expression, sex work, HIV status, or acts of civil disobedience will be exempted;

- **C.** By the establishment of “conflict of interest,” as described in the governing documents of the organization;

- **D.** By establishment of malicious intent toward the welfare of the organization and its purposes;

- **E.** By establishing a vote of “no confidence” in the Individual’s ability to perform functions essential to their role.

- **F.** Any violation(s) to the Bylaws, Standing Rules, and/or other governing documents of the organization.

- **G.** Loss of endorsement and/or affiliation, or a lapse in membership fees past 60 days from the start of the year.

- **H.** The process shall be considered fair and reasonable when
  - **1.** An investigation is conducted by the Board in partnership with the Global Advisory Council; then
  - **2.** A motion to accuse, citing Charges and Specifications, is approved by the Governance Committee; then
  - **3.** The member is heard, orally and in writing, ten (10) days maximum after the Governance Committee passes its motion; then
  - **4.** The motion to carry out the recommended disciplinary action is approved by a two-thirds majority vote of the Board of Directors; then The member to be expelled is notified in writing 24 hours after the Board vote of the final decision.
