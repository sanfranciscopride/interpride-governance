## Governance Summary & Commentary
Sets forth InterPride's charitable, educational, and human rights purpose: increasing the capacity of LGBTQI+ Pride organizations worldwide, networking, combating bias and bigotry, eliminating accessibility barriers, and licensing the **WorldPride** title.

- **2022 → 2023 Amendment (`09.29.2023`):**
  - Expanded `Section 2` to explicitly include *"combat bias and bigotry in all forms; eliminate barriers and increase accessibility so that all people might fully participate"* and added *"Pride Events"* to protected community entities.

## Adopted Text (`12.12.2025` Edition)

This Corporation is organized exclusively for charitable and educational purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c) (3) of the Internal Revenue Code of the United States of America and subject thereto. The nature of the business to be conducted is to promote Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, Trans, and Intersex Pride on an international level through the following primary purposes:

- **A.** Facilitating networking and the sharing of information related to producing and promoting Pride events and related educational events, including through the organisation of an annual world conference;

- **B.** Empowering and supporting Pride Organizations in their efforts of producing Pride events;

Secondary purposes, where the corporation’s resources so allow, are:

- **C.** Conducting programs of an educational purpose for the intent of helping Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, Trans, and Intersex persons become more comfortable with who they are;

- **D.** Educating the general public towards a better and more accurate understanding of homosexuality, bisexuality, transgender identity, and intersex identity; and

- **E.** Advancing human rights on SOGIESC issues and supporting members’ efforts in doing so.
