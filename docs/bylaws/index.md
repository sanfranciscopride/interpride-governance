## Constitutional Role & Amendment Threshold (`Article XII`)

The **Bylaws of InterPride** are the highest internal governing charter of the organization beneath its Texas Articles of Incorporation and U.S. 501(c)(3) law. In any conflict between the Bylaws and a Standing Rule, Resolution, or Committee Policy, **the Bylaws control**.

### How the Bylaws Are Amended (`Article XII`)
Amending the Bylaws requires a strict **two-stage process**:
1. **Stage 1 — Proposal & GM&WC Vote:** Proposed by $\ge 5$ Full Member representatives (or Board/GAC majority) with an implementation plan, and approved by a **51% simple majority** at an Annual General Meeting & World Conference (GM&WC).
2. **Stage 2 — Membership Ratification Ballot:** Following Stage 1 approval, the proposed amendment is submitted to all Full Member Organizations in good standing via electronic ballot and requires a **two-thirds (2/3, 67%+) supermajority affirmative vote** within 60 days.

## Longitudinal Evolution (`2022` → `2023` → `2024` → `2025`)

- **December 2022 (`2022-12`):** Comprehensive constitutional modernization replacing the legacy regional director model with a restructured Board of Directors (3 Co-Presidents, Secretary, Treasurer, Vice Presidents, 3 GAC Board Representatives, and Partnered Pride Platform Representatives) and creating the **Global Advisory Council (GAC)** under `Article VII`.
- **September 29, 2023 (`2023-09-29`):** Split `VP of Diversity, Inclusion, and Accessibility` into two distinct Vice President seats (`VP of Diversity and Inclusion` and `VP of Accessibility`) and added a non-voting **Emeritus Council** representative (`Article V, Section 1`).
- **January 20, 2024 (`2024-01-20`):** Corrected `Article VII, Section 3` from *"six (6) of its members"* to **"three (3) of its members"** on the Board of Directors, matching `Article V, Section 1`.
- **December 12, 2025 (`2025-12-12` — Current):**
  - **Co-President Gender Representation (`Article V, Section 1`):** Specified one (1) seat held by a woman, one (1) by a man, and one (1) by a non-binary or gender diverse person (with at least 2 of 3 seats filled at all times).
  - **Nominee Qualifications (`Article V, Section 12.A`):** Required nominees to have actively served on at least one InterPride committee or working group for at least 1 full year prior ($\ge 70\%$ attendance) and have proven leadership experience.
  - **GM&WC Frequency & Format (`Article VIII, Sections 1–2`):** Allowed GM&WCs at least once every two years (up to 25 months) and authorized Board-approved virtual or hybrid hosting arrangements.
  - **⚠️ PDF Layout Corruptions Introduced in `12.12.2025`:** During the desktop-publishing layout of `IP_Bylaws_Approved_English_12.12.2025.pdf`, two copy-paste corruptions were accidentally introduced into [`Article II.R` (Quorum)](article-02.html) and [`Article V, Section 12.B` & `Section 14`](article-05.html). ## Complete Index of Articles (`Articles I–XIII`)

| Article | Title | Constitutional Scope |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [**ARTICLE I**](article-01.html) | **Name** | Establishes the legal corporate name of the organization (**InterPride**, also known as **International Association of Pride Organizers**), incorporated in the State of Texas as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. |
| [**ARTICLE II**](article-02.html) | **Definitions** ⚠️ **Errata Flagged** | Defines key constitutional terms used throughout the Bylaws and Standing Rules, including membership categories, Board & GAC structures, voting thresholds, and—added in December 2025—globally inclusive gender definitions (`Woman`, `Man`, `Trans`, `Non-Binary People`, `Gender Diverse`). |
| [**ARTICLE III**](article-03.html) | **Purpose** | Sets forth InterPride's charitable, educational, and human rights purpose: increasing the capacity of LGBTQI+ Pride organizations worldwide, networking, combating bias and bigotry, eliminating accessibility barriers, and licensing the **WorldPride** title. |
| [**ARTICLE IV**](article-04.html) | **Membership** | Governs Membership eligibility, the four membership classes (**Full Member**, **Associate Member**, **Affiliate Member**, **Supportive Member**), application vetting, dues, voting rights (`1 Full Member = 1 Vote`), and termination/suspension of membership. |
| [**ARTICLE V**](article-05.html) | **The Board** ⚠️ **Errata Flagged** | Establishes the **Board of Directors** composition, Officer roles, powers, fiduciary duties, meetings, quorum, nomination qualifications, election procedures, vacancy replacements, terms of office, term limits, and removal/disciplinary procedures. |
| [**ARTICLE VI**](article-06.html) | **Regions** | Governs the geographic **Regions** of InterPride (`Regions 1–20`), regional autonomy, and regional conferences. |
| [**ARTICLE VII**](article-07.html) | **Global Advisory Council** | Establishes the **Global Advisory Council (GAC)**, composed of up to two (2) Regional Representatives per Region (or three (3) for Regions with $>50$ Member Organizations), and its **three (3) elected Board Representatives**. |
| [**ARTICLE VIII**](article-08.html) | **General Meeting And World Conference (Gm&Wc)** | Governs the **General Meeting and World Conference (GM&WC)**, including frequency, host selection, virtual/hybrid authority, plenary structure, notice, and quorum. |
| [**ARTICLE IX**](article-09.html) | **Language** | Designates **English** as the official language of InterPride while committing to translation and interpretation (Spanish, French, Portuguese, International Sign) as resources allow. |
| [**ARTICLE X**](article-10.html) | **Financial Matters** | Governs InterPride's fiscal year (`January 1 – December 31`), contracts, loans, checks/drafts (3 signatories: Co-Presidents + Treasurer), deposits, and financial reporting. |
| [**ARTICLE XI**](article-11.html) | **Dedication Of Assets** | Dedicates InterPride's assets irrevocably to 501(c)(3) charitable/educational purposes, prohibits private inurement or political campaign intervention, and governs asset distribution upon dissolution. |
| [**ARTICLE XII**](article-12.html) | **Amendments** | Establishes the **two-stage Bylaw Amendment process**: proposal by $\ge 5$ Full Member representatives (or Board/GAC majority) with an implementation plan $\rightarrow$ **51% simple majority** at the GM&WC $\rightarrow$ **2/3 (67%+) ratification vote** of the Membership within 60 days. |
| [**ARTICLE XIII**](article-13.html) | **Governing Law And Jurisdiction** | Specifies that the Bylaws are governed by the laws of the **State of Texas** (Texas Business Organizations Code Chapter 22), with severability. |
