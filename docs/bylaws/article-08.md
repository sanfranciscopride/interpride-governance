## Governance Summary & Commentary
Governs the **General Meeting and World Conference (GM&WC)**, including frequency, host selection, virtual/hybrid authority, plenary structure, notice, and quorum.

- **2024 → 2025 Amendments (`12.12.2025`):**
  - **Frequency (`Section 1`):** Changed from an annual requirement to **"at least once every two years"** (no later than 25 months after the previous GM&WC).
  - **Location & Format (`Section 2`):** Changed host approval from Membership vote to **"approved by a vote of the Board"**, and added: *"The Board may also designate a **fully virtual or hybrid format** as the hosting arrangement."*

## Adopted Text (`12.12.2025` Edition)

### SECTION 1 FREQUENCY

The Organization shall hold a Generl Meeting and World Conference (GM&WC) at least once every two years. The GM&WC shall not be held later than twenty–five (25) months following the adjournment of the previous GM&WC.


### SECTION 2 LOCATION

The location and host organization for a future General Meeting & World Conference (GM&WC) must be approved by a vote of the Board. Should an approved location and/or host organization be unable to host a GM&WC, the Board is empowered to take necessary and proper actions to ensure the sustainability of a GM&WC, including, if necessary, the selection of an alternate location and/or host organization; or the designation of a fully virtual or hybrid format as the hosting arrangement.


### SECTION 3 VOTING


- **A.** Each Full Member organization present shall be entitled to cast one (1) vote on any matter of business before the Membership, provided they are in good standing with the Organization.

- **B.** Before or within twenty–four (24) hours after the opening of the first plenary session of the GM, each Full Member organization shall submit, in writing, the name of its spokesperson and two (2) alternate spokespeople.

- **C.** The spokesperson shall cast all votes and shall, together with the alternate spokespeople, be authorized to represent their organization in matters of business before the Membership.

- **D.** In the absence of the spokesperson, an alternate spokesperson may assume the duties of the spokesperson.

- **E.** The second plenary must not be scheduled for the first or last day of the Conference.
