## Governance Summary & Commentary
Specifies that the Bylaws are governed by the laws of the **State of Texas** (Texas Business Organizations Code Chapter 22), with severability.

Note [Resolution 25-03](../resolutions/operational-policy.html) (adopted 2025), which empowers the Board to research and establish a secondary incorporation/registration outside the United States.

## Adopted Text (`12.12.2025` Edition)

### SECTION 1 GOVERNING LAW

These Bylaws shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas in the United States whose courts shall be deemed those of competent jurisdiction.


### SECTION 2 SUBORDINATION

These Bylaws shall be read in conjunction with, shall be subordinate to and, should a conflict exist, give way to the Articles of Incorporation.
