## Governance Summary & Commentary
Designates **English** as the official language of InterPride while committing to translation and interpretation (Spanish, French, Portuguese, International Sign) as resources allow.

Cross-reference with [Standing Rule 03.08 (Language)](../standing-rules/sr-03-08.html) and [Standing Rule 04.02 Sec 11 (GM&WC Language)](../standing-rules/sr-04-02.html).

## Adopted Text (`12.12.2025` Edition)

### SECTION 1 LANGUAGES

The languages of the Organization shall include English, French, Portuguese, and Spanish. The Organization shall endeavor to provide interpretation and translation services to assist Members in participating in its activities.


### SECTION 2 NUMBER

Where the plural or singular is used, it shall, where the context so permits, include the other.
