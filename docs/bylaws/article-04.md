## Governance Summary & Commentary
Governs Membership eligibility, the four membership classes (**Full Member**, **Associate Member**, **Affiliate Member**, **Supportive Member**), application vetting, dues, voting rights (`1 Full Member = 1 Vote`), and termination/suspension of membership.

- **Cross-Reference to `SR 02.07` (Approved March 13, 2026):**
  - While `Article IV` defines the constitutional classes of membership, the specific fee sliding scale (`$50–$1,000` AGI), OECD ODA rates (`$1/$5/$10/$20`), and **Pride Platform Membership** rules (`50/50` split, Jan 31 remittance, Dec 31 lapse, 50-Pride minimum) are codified in [Standing Rule 02.07](../standing-rules/sr-02-07.html).
  - Note that under the **April/May 2026 Governance Committee Minutes**, the **Record Date** for the 2026 GM&WC is **August 30, 2026** (60 days prior to the GM&WC per `SR 04.02 Sec 1.A.1`), and organizations may only qualify for fee waivers for **two (2) consecutive years** (`SR 02.04`).

## Adopted Text (`12.12.2025` Edition)

### SECTION 1 LEVELS OF MEMBERSHIP DEFINED

The organization shall have four levels of membership as herein defined:


- **A.** Full Membership Full Members of the Organization shall, if in good standing, have access to all membership benefits and have full voting privileges on all matters within its purview.


- **B.** Associate Associate Members of the Organization shall have access to limited membership Membership benefits and shall not have voting privileges on any matter being considered by the Membership.


- **C.** Affiliate Affiliate Members of the Organization shall have access to limited membership Membership benefits and shall not have voting privileges on any matter being considered by the Membership.


- **D.** Provisional Provisional Members of the Organization shall have access to all membership Membership benefits and shall not have voting privileges on any matter being considered by the Membership.

Membership Benefits available to the levels of membership listed above and those matters within Full Members’ purview to act upon shall be defined by these Bylaws and other policies and procedures that may be adopted, from time to time, by the Membership, the Board, or the Global Advisory Council.


### SECTION 2 ADMISSION TO FULL MEMBERSHIP

Any Pride organization that has produced a Pride event within a 15-month period prior to a World Conference, upon submitting a membership application and accounting for payment of their annual dues in accordance with Article IV,

### Section 8 of these Bylaws, shall be admitted as a Full Member of the Organization.


### SECTION 3 ADMISSION TO ASSOCIATE MEMBERSHIP

Any Pride organization that has produced a Pride event within a 18-month period prior to a World Conference but does not meet the requirement of being an organization as defined by Article II, Paragraph P, above, upon submitting a membership application and accounting for payment of their annual dues in accordance with Article IV, Section 8 of these Bylaws, shall be admitted as an Associate Member of the Organization.


### SECTION 4 ADMISSION TO AFFILIATE MEMBERSHIP

Pride Networks, upon submitting a membership application and accounting for payment of their annual dues in accordance with Article IV, Section 8 of these Bylaws, shall be admitted as an Affiliate Member of the Organization.

### SECTION 5 ADMISSION TO PROVISIONAL MEMBERSHIP

Any Pride organization that has not produced a Pride event within an 18-month period prior to a World Conference, upon submitting a membership application and accounting for payment of their annual dues in accordance with Article IV,

### Section 8, of these Bylaws shall be admitted as a Provisional Member of the Organization.


### SECTION 6 TERM OF MEMBERSHIP

Membership at all levels shall be for a term of one calendar year (January to December), shall commence upon the acceptance of a membership application and the accounting for payment of a Member’s dues and shall conclude on 31 December of a given calendar year.


### SECTION 7 MEMBERSHIP ADHERENCE TO ORGANIZATION POLICIES AND PURPOSE

No Pride organization or network shall be denied membership at any level, provided that they adhere to the policies and purposes of InterPride. Pride organizations that fail to adhere to said policies and purposes may be denied or removed from membership upon review and a two-thirds (2/3) vote of the Board, that are present and voting, on such a denial or removal. The Board may, at its discretion, choose to delegate this authority to the Global Advisory Council, by the passage of a standing rule to this effect.


### SECTION 8 MEMBERSHIP DUES – ASSIGNMENT OF ASSESSMENT

The Organization shall collect annual membership dues according to a method that shall be detailed in a resolution approved by the Board.


### SECTION 9 WAIVER IN CASES OF EMERGENCIES

The Board, by a two-thirds (2/3) majority vote, will have the authority to waive any sections of this Article in instances of emergencies; including, but not limited to, global pandemics, social unrests, repercussions from natural and human- made disasters, and or travel advisories triggered by geopolitical instability; and/or extraordinary situations beyond the control of InterPride or its members.
