## Governance Summary & Commentary
Defines key constitutional terms used throughout the Bylaws and Standing Rules, including membership categories, Board & GAC structures, voting thresholds, and—added in December 2025—globally inclusive gender definitions (`Woman`, `Man`, `Trans`, `Non-Binary People`, `Gender Diverse`).

- **2024 → 2025 Amendments (`12.12.2025`):**
  - **`F. Board` & `G. Global Advisory Council Board`:** Updated from *"six representatives appointed"* to **"three representatives elected/appointed"** by the Global Advisory Council, aligning Article II with `Article V, Section 1` and `Article VII, Section 3`.
  - **`J. LGBTQI+` & `P. Pride Organization`:** Standardized `LGBTI+` to `LGBTQI+`.
  - **Added `X. Emeritus Council`:** Defines the advisory body of former Board members, Regional Representatives, Committee Co-Chairs, and Staff.
  - **Added `Y. Woman`, `Z. Man`, `AA. Trans`, `BB. Non-Binary People`, `CC. Gender Diverse`:** Codifies self-determination of gender identity and explicitly recognizes global/indigenous gender identities (*fakaleiti, leiti, muxes, chibados, two-spirit, yan daudu, hijra, khawaja sara, waria, māhū, bakla*) for the Co-President gender diversity mandate in `Article V, Section 1`.

{: .warning }
> **Governance Audit Errata (`12.12.2025` PDF Corruption — Article II.R `Quorum`):**
> In the December 12, 2025 PDF layout (`IP_Bylaws_Approved_English_12.12.2025.pdf`, p. 7), the sentence defining `G. Global Advisory Council Board` (*"The three representatives appointed to serve on the Board by the Global Advisory Council from among the Council's elected regional representatives."*) was accidentally pasted onto the end of **`R. Quorum`**.
> - **Canonical / Restored Text of `R. Quorum`:** *"Quorum – the minimum number of voting members who must be present at a properly called meeting in order to conduct business in the name of the group."*

## Adopted Text (`12.12.2025` Edition)

- **A.** Abstention Indications that a person is present but choosing not to cast their vote — as such, they do not meet the requirement of “present and voting” as defined in Item M, below, nor are they included in a vote total.


- **B.** Annual Gross Income (AGI) All income/revenue received by a member organization in a fiscal year (twelve-month accounting period) prior to the deduction of any expenses.


- **C.** General Meeting (GM) The business portions of the World Conference, including all plenary sessions of the Membership and meetings of the Board of Directors, the Global Advisory Council, committees of the Board, and their subcommittees. Old name = AGM.


- **D.** World Conference (WC) A gathering of the Organization’s membership, including educational and networking opportunities and the General Meeting of the Membership.


- **E.** Affiliation A person holds affiliation when they are actively engaged in an ongoing basis with a member Pride and that affiliation is recognized by the member Pride.


- **F.** Board The Board of Directors of InterPride, comprising elected officers (Co-Presidents, Vice Presidents, Secretary, and Treasurer) and three liaisons by the Global Advisory Council. (See Article V – The Board.)


- **G.** Global Advisory Council The three (3) representatives elected to serve on the Board by the Global Advisory Council, according to the rules and (GAC) Board regulations of the Global Advisory Council.


- **H.** Global Advisory Council A body made up of representatives from the global regions that meets regularly and advises the Board on issues, needs, Representatives activities, and recommendations from all regions.


- **I.** In Writing Unless otherwise specified, “in writing” shall refer to communications received either on paper or via electronic means (i.e., email).


- **J.** LGBTQI+ Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, Trans, Queer, and Intersex and other diverse sexual orientations, gender identities and expressions, and sex characteristics.


- **K.** Majority Vote (Simple) A motion requiring a (simple) majority vote would pass with 51% or more voting in favor. Abstention votes adjust the overall majority for motions to be carried.


- **L.** Officer A member of the Board and/or the Executive Committee.


- **M.** Present and Voting Constitutes a person present in person (or electronically, as will be permitted) and casting either a “yes” or “no” vote.


- **N.** Pride Event A parade, march, rally, festival, arts festival, cultural activity or other event/ activity, and is at least partially open to the general public, organized for communities of diverse sexual orientations, gender identities and expressions, and sex characteristics and promoting the visibility and/or validating the existence of those persons and commemorating the Stonewall Riots and/or a similar historic event/annual/periodic event as produced by a Pride Organization, as defined in Paragraph P.


- **O.** Pride Network An organization whose membership shall principally consist of Pride organizations. This organization may act as a Pride Platform in its relationship to InterPride.

- **P.** Pride Organization An organization* that produces Pride events as one of its major purposes or activities; and operates in the manner of a non-governmental organization (NGO); and any profits of the organization must benefit the LGBTQI+ communities. *The organization may be either registered or unregistered with their local authority.


- **Q.** Pride Platform An organization registered in a country within a region that serves as a membership organization for Pride organizations in that region and has a joint membership agreement formed with InterPride.


- **R.** Quorum Fifty-percent (50%) or more of those present and voting. For meetings of the Board, this includes officers. For meetings of the membership, it includes all credentialed members present. For committee meetings, this includes all committee members. The three representatives appointed to serve on the Board by the Global Advisory Council, according to the rules and regulations of the Global Advisory Council.


- **S.** Region A geographic area, as defined by a duly passed resolution of the Board and Global Advisory Council, which is entitled to representation on the Board of Directors.


- **T.** Regional Council A group of representatives from member organizations from the region lead by regional representatives appointed by a region’s membership to the Global Advisory Council. These appointments are conducted within the region itself, under the regional council’s own rules and regulations. The Regional Council has a duty to communicate the region’s challenges, barriers, needs, risks, opportunities, and strengths to the Global Advisory Council.


- **U.** SOGIESC Sexual Orientation, Gender Identity and Expression, and Sex Characteristics


- **V.** Two-Thirds Vote A motion requiring a supermajority vote would pass with 67% or more voting in favor. Abstention votes adjust the overall (Supermajority) majority for motions to be carried.


- **W.** Plenary A meeting or session where all participants at a general meeting are invited to attend. A plenary can be delivered in-person, online, and/or in a format deemed necessary by the Board. Plenaries can be held as a live meeting or for a period of time as an asynchronous session. At least one plenary must be held live during each general meeting.


- **X.** Emeritus Council An advisory group of InterPride past officers and alumni Board members, available to consult with the current InterPride Board, committees, and contracts for advice, historical, and expert information.


- **Y.** Woman A woman is anyone who identifies and lives as a woman, including cisgender, trans, and intersex women, as well as culturally specific identities, including, but not limited to, fakaleiti (Tonga), muxes (Mexico), chibados (Angola), and others. Being a Woman is self-determined by gender identity and lived experience, not by sex assigned at birth, anatomy, or chromosomal characteristics.


- **Z.** Man A man is anyone who identifies and lives as a man, including cisgender, trans, and intersex men, as well as men within culturally specific identities, including, but not limited to, two-spirit (Indigenous North America) and yan daudu (Hausa). Being a Man is self-determined by gender identity and lived experience, not by sex assigned at birth, anatomy, or chromosomal characteristics.


- **AA.** Trans Trans describes people whose gender identity differs from their sex assigned at birth, including trans men, trans women, and some culturally specific identities, including, but not limited to, hijra (South Asia), khawaja sara (Pakistan), waria (Indonesia), and two-spirit (Indigenous North America). Not all gender-diverse people use this term.


- **BB.** Non-Binary People Non-binary people have gender identities that fall outside the traditional categories of man and woman. This includes agender, genderfluid, and genderqueer, as well as culturally rooted identities, including, but not limited to, māhū (Hawai‘i) and bakla (Philippines). Some non-binary people also identify as trans or gender diverse.


- **CC.** Gender Diverse Gender diverse is an umbrella term for individuals whose gender identity or expression falls outside binary norms. This includes trans, non-binary, and gender non-conforming people, as well as culturally specific identities across Africa, Asia, the Americas, Europe, and Oceania.
