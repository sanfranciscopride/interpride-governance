## Governance Summary & Commentary
Establishes the **Global Advisory Council (GAC)**, composed of up to two (2) Regional Representatives per Region (or three (3) for Regions with $>50$ Member Organizations), and its **three (3) elected Board Representatives**.

- **2023 → 2024 Amendment (`01.20.2024`):**
  - Corrected `Section 3 (Representation on the Board)` from *"six (6) of its members"* to **"three (3) of its members"**, aligning Article VII with `Article V, Section 1`.
- **Cross-Reference:** See [GAC Rules & Regulations (`07.17.2026`)](../gac/index.html) for the newly standardized 8-step Regional Representative election process jointly administered by Governance and VPMS.

## Adopted Text (`12.12.2025` Edition)

### SECTION 1 STRUCTURE

The Global Advisory Council will have two representatives appointed by their regional pride platform or the InterPride members of the region they represent. For Regions with more than 50 member organizations, the region may appoint a third representative on the Global Advisory Council.


### SECTION 2 REGIONAL COUNCILS

Each region will have the right to form a council to coordinate activities, outreach, and communications within the region. A council can be a regional Pride platform.


### SECTION 3 GLOBAL ADVISORY COUNCIL REPRESENTATIVES ON THE BOARD

The Global Advisory Council will elect three (3) Representatives to serve on the Board. Representatives can serve on the Board for a maximum of two (2) terms.


### SECTION 4 AUTHORITY AND LIMITATION


- **A.** The Global Advisory Council has the authority to gather information, produce reports, and present recommendations to the Board.

- **B.** The Global Advisory Council has the authority to:
    - **a.** Approve regional boundaries
    - **b.** Approve the creation of a new region in partnership with the Board
    - **c.** Review the organization’s annual budget and work plan
    - **d.** Review the annual narrative and financial reports of the organization
    - **e.** Recommend individuals, for appointment to the Board, to fill vacant positions
