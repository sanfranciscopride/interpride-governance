                    BYLAW AMENDMENT FORM
All Bylaw amendment proposals must be filled out, signed, and uploaded to the following form
link: www.interpride.org/bylawsresolutionsgmwc2023.

The deadline for these proposals is 29 October 2023 at 15:30 GMT. Bylaw Amendments proposed by
caucus meetings, workshops, the Board of Directors, etc., at the General Meeting & World Conference
(GM&WC) must be submitted similarly.

PLEASE NOTE
   ●   All Bylaw amendment proposals must be signed by a credentialed delegate and co-signed by
       four (4) additional credentialed delegates. All five (5) delegates must be from different
       full-member organizations.
   ●   The Governance Committee will review all Bylaw amendment proposals. After determination
       that they are appropriate, the proposal will be presented at a Plenary session.
   ●   The lead delegate submitting the proposal will be asked to give a short presentation to introduce
       the proposal at the first Plenary session at the GM&WC.
   ●   To be adopted, Bylaw amendments must receive a majority affirmative vote (50% plus 1) on a
       first ballot held within seven days of the GM&WC, then must obtain a super-majority (⅔)
       affirmative vote occurring within 60 days of the first vote.
   ●   Late, unreadable, or incomplete forms will not be accepted.

Bylaw Amendment Proposal:

  We must recognize and emphasize the importance of accessibility being at the
  forefront of the organization. The current duties and responsibilities of the Vice
  President of Diversity, Inclusion, and Accessibility are too excessive for a single
  individual to handle.

  To ensure that InterPride is able to maintain accessibility as a core value of the
  organization:

  Let it be it resolved that the board role of Vice President of Diversity, Inclusion, and
  Accessibility be split into two roles, separating Accessibility from Diversity and
  Inclusion creating a new board role of Vice President of Accessibility.

  Amend bylaws Article V, Section 1, to replace ‘Up to one (1) Vice President of
  Diversity, Inclusion, and Accessibility’ with ‘Up to one (1) Vice President of
  Diversity and Inclusion’ thereby removing references to Accessibility from this
  board member role.

  Amend bylaws Article V, Section 1, to add ‘Up to one (1) Vice President of
  Accessibility’, thereby creating a standalone role for Accessibility.

  In addition, amend Standing Rule 12.01 ‘Vice President of Accessibility, Diversity,
  and Inclusion’ section 1 to delete ‘Accessibility’.

  Amend Standing Rule 12.01 to add ‘Vice President of Accessibility’ and
  appropriate duties and responsibilities for the board role.
