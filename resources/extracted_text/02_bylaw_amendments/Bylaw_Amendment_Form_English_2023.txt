                    BYLAW AMENDMENT FORM
All Bylaw amendment proposals must be filled out, signed, and uploaded to the following form
link: www.interpride.org/bylawsresolutionsgmwc2023.

The deadline for these proposals is 29 October 2023 at 15:30 GMT. Bylaw Amendments proposed by
caucus meetings, workshops, the Board of Directors, etc., at the General Meeting & World Conference
(GM&WC) must be submitted similarly.

PLEASE NOTE
   ●   All Bylaw amendment proposals must be signed by a credentialed delegate and co-signed by
       four (4) additional credentialed delegates. All five (5) delegates must be from different
       full-member organizations.
   ●   The Governance Committee will review all Bylaw amendment proposals. After determination
       that they are appropriate, the proposal will be presented at a Plenary session.
   ●   The lead delegate submitting the proposal will be asked to give a short presentation to introduce
       the proposal at the first Plenary session at the GM&WC.
   ●   To be adopted, Bylaw amendments must receive a majority affirmative vote (50% plus 1) on a
       first ballot held within seven days of the GM&WC, then must obtain a super-majority (⅔)
       affirmative vote occurring within 60 days of the first vote.
   ●   Late, unreadable, or incomplete forms will not be accepted.
