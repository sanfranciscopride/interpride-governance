                    BYLAW AMENDMENT FORM
All Bylaw amendment proposals must be filled out, signed, and uploaded to the following form
link: www.interpride.org/bylawsresolutionsgmwc2023.

The deadline for these proposals is 29 October 2023 at 15:30 GMT. Bylaw Amendments proposed by
caucus meetings, workshops, the Board of Directors, etc., at the General Meeting & World Conference
(GM&WC) must be submitted similarly.

PLEASE NOTE
   ●   All Bylaw amendment proposals must be signed by a credentialed delegate and co-signed by
       four (4) additional credentialed delegates. All five (5) delegates must be from different full-
       member organizations.
   ●   The Governance Committee will review all Bylaw amendment proposals. After determination
       that they are appropriate, the proposal will be presented at a Plenary session.
   ●   The lead delegate submitting the proposal will be asked to give a short presentation to introduce
       the proposal at the first Plenary session at the GM&WC.
   ●   To be adopted, Bylaw amendments must receive a majority affirmative vote (50% plus 1) on a
       first ballot held within seven days of the GM&WC, then must obtain a super-majority (⅔)
       affirmative vote occurring within 60 days of the first vote.
   ●   Late, unreadable, or incomplete forms will not be accepted.

Proposed amendment

InterPride’s primary focus must always be first on its role as a network of Pride
organisers. Other purposes are secondary.

We therefore propose to amend Bylaws Article III – Purpose as follows:

 This Corporation is organized exclusively for charitable and educational
 purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code
 of the United States of America and subject thereto.

 The nature of the business to be conducted is to promote Lesbian, Gay,
 Bisexual, Trans, and Intersex Pride on an international level through the following
 primary purposes:

 A. Facilitating networking and the sharing of information related to producing and
 promoting Pride events and related educational events, including through the
 organisation of an annual world conference;

 B. Empowering and supporting Pride Organizations in their efforts of producing
 Pride events;

 Secondary purposes, where the corporation’s resources so allow, are:

 C. Conducting programs of an educational purpose for the intent of helping
 Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, Trans, and Intersex persons become more comfortable
 with who they are;

 D. Educating the general public towards a better and more accurate
 understanding of homosexuality, bisexuality, transgender identity, and intersex
 identity; and

 E. Advancing human rights on SOGIESC issues and supporting members’ efforts
 in doing so.
