                RESOLUTION PROPOSAL FORM
All Resolution Proposals must be filled out, signed, and uploaded to the following form link:
www.interpride.org/bylawsresolutionsgmwc2023.

The deadline for Resolution Proposals is 29 October 2023 at 15:30 GMT. Resolutions are items or
   statements that require the action and/or support of InterPride membership. These include
   boycotts, positions on global issues, the creation of new caucuses, etc. Examples can be found in
   the Resolution Guide on the IP website (https://www.interpride.org/resolutions). All resolution
   proposals must be typewritten or neatly printed.


PLEASE NOTE
   ●   Resolutions, if intended to require action, must contain an implementation plan. The delegation
       submitting the proposal and those who co-sign are jointly responsible for its implementation.
   ●   If needed, resolution proposals submitted without an implementation plan will be returned to the
       person who introduced them to be revised and resubmitted by the date and time specified
       above.
   ●   Late, unreadable, or incomplete forms will not be accepted.
   ●   Resolutions should be brief and clear. The person submitting the proposal should be prepared
       to give a short (2-minute) presentation at a Plenary session to introduce the proposal.
   ●   All Resolution proposals must be signed by a credentialed delegate and co-signed by four (4)
       additional credentialed delegates. All five (5) delegates must be from different delegations and,
       ideally, different regions.

Proposed resolution

Within three months of the closure of the 2023 World Conference, the Board will produce and
send to all members a detailed plan of how the financial losses from the Gala held in April 2023
will be recouped. This plan should be specific and have a definitive timeline. The Board should
report on progress at each World Conference until all funds have been recouped.
