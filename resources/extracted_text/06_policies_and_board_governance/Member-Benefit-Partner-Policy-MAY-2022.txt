Introduction

Thank you for your interest in InterPride’s Member Benefit Partner Program (MBPP).
Submitting an application does not guarantee that you will be accepted as a Member
Benefit Partner (MBP).
Before you apply, please review all the guidelines. It is important to learn about
requirements for the application process. The MBPP application is located and
completed online by clicking here. For more information see the Frequently Asked
Questions (“FAQs”) section.
As a global non-profit organization, we rely on volunteers to conduct the review
process for each MBP application. Since we are dependent upon volunteers, and the
generous support of our partners, we ask for a USD$250 donation to support our
important work advancing the global LGBTQIA+ community when submitting an
application. We appreciate your support in advance. Please make the donation by
clicking here.
Submission of an application and donation does not guarantee acceptance into the
program. Our MBPP is highly competitive and applications are considered based on
the current needs of our members.

About InterPride
Vision
To achieve legal, social, and cultural equity for the SOGIESC (diverse sexual
orientations, gender identities and expressions, and sex characteristics) community
through the Pride movement.

Mission
   ●   To contribute to the visibility and inclusion of SOGIESC communities.
   ●   To connect, grow, and support Pride Platforms and networks globally.
   ●   To assist the Pride movement to design and conduct human rights events that
       deconstruct myths, lies, and prejudices surrounding SOGIESC realities.
   ●   To maximize networking and communication across the global Pride movement,
       offer resources, grow and leverage partnerships.

Values
Trust, Respect, Accountability, Diversity, Multiculturalism, Kindness, Bravery

Role
InterPride is the international organization that advances the Pride movement by
coordinating with global partners that share our values.
We are uniquely suited to carry out our work because InterPride:
   ●   Works with and supports the visibility and development of Pride networks
       around the world, thereby contributing to the presence and influence of Pride
       efforts at regional and local levels.
   ●   Supports the production of Pride events for the global SOGIESC community.
   ●   Manages, supports, and awards WorldPride™, global grant programs, and
       related initiatives.
Founded in 1982, InterPride is a not-for-profit membership-based organization with
more than 400 members from over 60 countries. Sources of income include
membership dues, licensing fees, sponsorships, donations, and merchandise sales.
We partner with other international, regional, and national LGBTQIA+ (Lesbian, Gay,
Bisexual, Transgender, Queer, Intersex, Asexual+) associations and human rights
organizations to facilitate social change for LGBTQIA+ people around the world. Each
year, InterPride, its partners, and its members engage millions of people through
in-person and virtual events, mainstream media, and social media.

About Pride
   ●   InterPride defines Pride as any public or semi-public gathering led by and for
       people from SOGIESC communities that promotes visibility and inclusion.

   ●   Such gatherings take many forms including but not limited to a parade, march,
       rally, festival, as well as events reflecting varying cultural practices such as
       pageants, traditional dance, ceremonies, and story-telling.
   ●   The nature of Pride gatherings is influenced by political, social, and cultural
       contexts including constraints imposed by at-risk environments.
   ●   A Pride organization is one that produces a Pride event. An organization may
       be described as Pride-like where it does not produce a Pride event as
       described above but aspires to do so. The inability to produce a Pride event
       may be due to factors such as the prohibition against public assembly,
       politically hostile environments, lack of funding, or organizational capacity.

Opportunities And Benefits
InterPride MBPP participants are afforded a number of benefits to help elevate their
brand. Some of these benefits include:
   ●   Potential for market penetration and business growth.
   ●   Marketing and communication access to a captive base of target customers of
       Pride organizations around the world. There are an estimated 1,500 to 2,000
       Pride organizations around the globe, and collectively they mobilize millions of
       individuals annually in communities across the globe. This can be achieved by
       listing the partner on our marketing materials, communication channels, live
       stream sessions, and newsletters.
   ●   Endorsement from InterPride, an association with a strong reputation and
       established team of Pride organizers.
   ●   Credibility to the products/services offered and increased trust in the supplier
       via InterPride's endorsement.
   ●   Increased visibility through InterPride's promotions to members, prospective
       members, and the larger business community.
   ●   Logo on our websites (Main, Member, Solo) that reach members throughout the
       year.

Eligibility Criteria
   ●   Organizations that publicly states support for LGBTQIA+ rights with a
       statement or policy on their respective website.
   ●   Opportunities for increased visibility of InterPride and member organizations.
   ●   Prior experience with the LGBTQIA+ community and Pride organizers highly
       preferred.
   ●   Meets the criteria under our Partnership & Sponsorship Ethics Policy ( Appendix
       C) and agrees to the requirements.

MBPP Requirements
Any potential MBP requires a tangible, relevant, and eye-catching value. Proposed
benefits cannot simply be benefits that members are able to access elseware.

                                                                                          5
Otherwise, they're neither meaningful nor effective as a member benefit for
InterPride.
InterPride has developed the following criteria for potential MBPP’s:

Relevance
The products, services, or promotions must be highly relevant to festival and event
production and non-profit organization management. InterPride will only consider
products/services from vendors that demonstrate a proven value with unique benefits
in terms of price, product, or service that members would not easily obtain elsewhere
in the marketplace.

Membership Value
The partnership should be focused on 'exclusive' benefits that are considered highly
valuable by InterPride members and available only through membership with us.

Revenue Potential
Sponsorship, advertising, and revenue-sharing opportunities are welcome and are
expected in any proposal. Revenue-sharing opportunities created through carefully
chosen partnerships can enhance InterPride's ability to generate additional revenue
while providing value for members.

Term of Agreement
MBP terms of agreements range from two (2) to five (5) years. InterPride assess the
MBP’s every twelve (12) months.

MBP Marketing
To ensure the growth and success of the program, InterPride and the partner will
commit to a proposed marketing plan to create awareness of the offering and
partnership. Ongoing marketing of the program/service/product will be established for
the duration of the agreement.
The plan will vary with each partner, but the channels and opportunities available
include:
   ●   Stand-alone email blasts
   ●   Mentions in member newsletters
   ●   Online logo placement
   ●   Social media posts
   ●   Opportunities to share educational content via presentation or in writing
   ●   Sponsorship of conferences
   ●   Virtual events
   ●   Networking events

Reporting
The partner must have the capability to monitor program participation by InterPride
members and to report regularly to InterPride on program activity and growth.

Supplier Exclusivity
There may be instances where InterPride will provide an MBP with supplier exclusivity,
meaning that InterPride will not engage others in the same field (parameters to be
determined in the contract) in similar MBP’s. InterPride will not, however, agree to
exclusivity in terms of general advertising sales, membership, or involvement with
competitors for purposes beyond an MBP.

Global Reach
Services should be provided worldwide, and in multiple languages.

Application Submission
Applications may be submitted using the form by clicking here. If you are unable to
access the form, please contact Member Services at
members.services.chairs@interpride.org for assistance.
The steps from application submission are set out in Appendix A.
In addition to other specified information, the application form requires the applicant
to provide:
   ●   Organization name and address
   ●   Website, and/or social media links
   ●   Primary contact person (Name, Pronouns, Email, Phone)
   ●   Alternate contact person (Name, Pronouns, Email, Phone)
   ●   Year the organization was established
   ●   Description of organization purpose
   ●   Description of organization activities
   ●   Number of organization members
   ●   Inclusion and diversity policy
   ●   Geographic reach of product or service being proposed
   ●   Detailed description of the product or service
   ●   A description of how members will benefit from the product or service
   ●   Terms and conditions for discount and exclusive offerings to members
   ●   Specific product or service being offered to members including pricing and free
       trial length
   ●   Sponsorship or revenue share percentage provided to InterPride with any
       requirements and limitations clearly stated

   ●   Requests related to marketing, promotion, and content sharing
   ●   Proposed time period for partnership (please note, InterPride assess the MBP’s

   ●   Agreement to the Brand Recognition requirements (Appendix B), should the

   ●   Agreement to InterPride’s Partnership & Sponsorship Ethics Policy (Appendix C)

Applications must be fully completed to be considered. Submission of applications will
be acknowledged within 48 hours of receipt.

Application Assessment
While the Committee will make every effort to expedite the assessment of
applications, a period of sixty (60) days from submission should be allowed for
notification of the outcome of the application.

Assessment Criteria
Select members of our leadership team will review proposals. Select members of
InterPride may be consulted at any point during this process to establish the
relevance of the product or service.
If it is determined that InterPride sees value in moving ahead with the MBP, we will
work with the partner to translate the proposal into either an annual or multi-year
agreement. As previously noted, InterPride assesses the MBP’s every twelve (12)
months.
A prospective partner does not become an official MBP until deliverables are agreed
upon and both parties sign a contract.
Some of the factors that are taken into consideration in the application assessment
include, but are not limited to:
   ●   Does the member benefit align with InterPride’s mission and values?
   ●   What benefits will the product or service deliver to Pride organizers?
   ●   Are there competitive discount offerings for InterPride’s members?
   ●   Is there a vital need for the type of product or service offering?
   ●   Is the member benefit well-described?

Notification of Decision
All applicants will be notified in writing of the outcome of their application within
forty-eight (48) hours of the decision being made.
Upon notification to successful applicants and finalization of any outstanding
documents, the process of adding you as a MBP will commence.

Appendix C. Partnership & Sponsorship Ethics Policy
InterPride is the international organization that ties Pride together globally. Members
of our organization are dedicated volunteers who organize and work to put on Pride
events worldwide. We value the active participation of partners and sponsors who
provide essential support, resources, and finances to enable us to deliver our events
and services.

InterPride exists to promote LGBTQIA+ Pride on an international level, increase
networking and communication among Pride Organizations, and encourage diverse
communities to hold and attend Pride events and act as a source of education.
InterPride is a voice for the LGBTQIA+ community around the world. We stand up for
inequality and fight injustice everywhere.
Partners and sponsors do not influence the policy or operation of InterPride. Given
our commitment to transparency, all partners and sponsors are listed in full on our
website.
InterPride will not enter into partnership or sponsorship arrangements with
companies who:
   ●   Manufacture or promote tobacco products.
   ●   Manufacture or sell weapons or arms.
   ●   Promote alcohol or drugs (including marijuana) in a way that does not meet
       standards set by local government in relation to consumption, advertising,
       marketing, and age of sale.
   ●   Manufacture products in countries where laws criminalize LGBTQIA+ individuals
       unless the company has a stated policy and provides support to LGBTQIA+
       advocacy organizations in that country and/or internationally.
   ●   Financially, verbally or in any other way, support regimes or organizations that
       violate human rights.
   ●   Directly or indirectly violate international treaties on Human Rights, including
       the Universal Declaration of Human Rights.
   ●   Directly or indirectly violate recognized international treaties or standards on
       the protection of the environment.
   ●   Engage in unethical profiteering schemes or lending practices that target those
       with a low socioeconomic status.
   ●   This is not an exhaustive list, and an assessment will be made on each
       potential partner or sponsor, including:
          ○   Listed on various LGBTQIA+ Equality Indexes such as that by the Human
              Rights Campaign (HRC), Stonewall, RainbowTick, or others
          ○   Signatory of any national or international LGBTQIA+ business practice
              protocols (i.e., UN STANDARDS OF CONDUCT FOR BUSINESS: Tackling
              Discrimination against Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, Transgender, & Intersex
              People)
          ○   LGBTQIA+ led or have an LGBTQIA+ Employee Resource Group (ERG)


                                                                                          13
          ○   Media analysis of human rights, LGBTQIA+, environmental and other
              coverage (positive, negative, neutral)
          ○   Review of any LGBTQIA+ policies
          ○   Review of any environmental policies (carbon footprint declaration,
              offsetting promises, monitoring, etc.)
          ○   Overall alignment with the mission of InterPride - a good fit with
              InterPride’s image
          ○   Does the partnership or sponsorship conflict with InterPride’s values?
          ○   Does the partnership or sponsorship suit our overall strategy?
          ○   Will this partnership or sponsorship help us realize our short and
              long-term objectives?
This policy will be reviewed annually. For further information on becoming a partner
or sponsor of InterPride, please email info@interpride.org.
By submitting this application, the applicant acknowledges that they have read and
fully understood the Partnership & Sponsorship Ethics Policy and agree to be bound
by them.
