                                                                    STANDING RULES
                                                                   SR 02.07 – PAYMENT OF MEMBERSHIP FEES

PURPOSE
To establish a policy regarding how membership fees are paid.

SECTION 1 – MEMBERSHIP PAYMENTS
   A. The Organization, as provided for in Article IV, Section 8 of its Bylaws, shall collect an annual membership fee
      known as fees.
    B. InterPride membership is for one calendar year, concluding on December 31st. The cost of membership ibased
       on each organization’s gross annual income and the following schedule:
             Full Membership/Associate Membership
                  $0-25,000                 $50
                  $25,001-50,000           $100
                  $50,001-75,000           $150
                  $75,001-100,000         $200
                  $100,001-200,000        $400
                  $200,001-400,000        $600
                  $400,001-1,000,000      $800
                  $1,000,0001+           $1,000
             Provisional Members                $50
             Affiliate Membership             $100 for local, or statewide organization/company
                                              $200 for national and international organization/company
    C. Full Membership/Associate Membership/Provisional Membership for organizations with annual revenues of
       $0-25,000 from Official Development Assistance (ODA) eligible countries. The list of countries under each
       category are updated by the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD.
             Least Developed Countries:                               $1
             Other Low-Income Countries:                              $5
             Lower Middle-Income Countries and Territories:           $10
             Upper Middle-Income Countries and Territories:           $20
    D. All Memberships are to be paid through the membership portal on the InterPride website. Member Prides will have
       the option to pay by credit card, ACH (automatic clearing house), other electronic transfers, or check. If paid by check,
       the check should be received by the Treasurer within 30 days of submitting via the membership portal.
    E. All memberships are to be paid in US-equivalent dollars (USD).
    F.   The Treasurer will reconcile the memberships received with a monthly report from the membership portal that is
         provided by the Member Services Committee.
    G. Pride organizations that are programs of community centers should submit a Profit and Loss Statement,
       or a similar financial report for their Pride event revenue, independent from their center's compiled revenue. This
       deducted difference should also include in-kind contributions. The actual center's operating revenue will only be
       referenced for assessment purposes.

     SECTION 2 – PRIDE PLATFORM MEMBERSHIPS
        A. InterPride will split each paid membership fee with Pride Platforms on a 50/50 basis for all regions with such
           an established organization. A Pride Platform is recognizedwhen it includes at least 10 different paid member
           Prides, has elected officers, and has bylaws or operatingdocuments that have been filed with the government
           where it resides.
         B. Membership fees are to be submitted and paid the Pride Platform's membership portal, and sent to InterPride
            on a regular basis, prior to December 31st of each year.
         C. The Treasurer and the Vice President of Member Services will, upon failure of recent of fees by January 31st,
            will declare all memberships as lapsed if not renewed.
         D. Each Pride Platform agrees that it will not license a separate regional Pride celebration (EuroPride, FiertéCanada
            Pride, USA Prides conference, etc.) to one of its members in the same year that InterPride’s WorldPride is held in
            that region.

     SECTION 3 – ENACTMENT AND PRECEDENCE
     This standing rule shall take effect immediately upon passage and replace all other standing rules, motions, resolutions,
     joint agreements and other policies enacted by the Executive Committee, Board of Directors and Membership dealing
     with the subject matter contained herein.
