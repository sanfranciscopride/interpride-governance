1. All information at InterPride is confidential to the organization unless it is
explicitly stated to be able to be shared with partners, members, or the public.
2. The matrix describes the levels of confidentiality and the risks of data breaches if
confidentiality is broken. A breach of confidentiality is damaging to the
organization regardless of the level of restriction.
3. If you are not sure if you can share information, please contact the governance
committee chairs and they will connect you with the appropriate person to
confirm if information can be released or not.
