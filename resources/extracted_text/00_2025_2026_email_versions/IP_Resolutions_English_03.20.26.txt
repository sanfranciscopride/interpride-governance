                                                                                                  RESOLUTION 85-08
WHEREAS, Lesbian and Gay Pride Celebrations were initiated in this country by the Stonewall Riots of 1969, and;
Whereas, Lesbian and Gay Pride Celebrations have established an eighteen year history, and;
Whereas, no official archive for the collection and keeping of all slogans, products, historical material and other relevant
information exists at this time, and;
Whereas, the Gay and Lesbian archives of San Diego, a facility proposed for the storing of such information is being
assembled in San Diego, California;
Be it therefore resolved that the International Association of Lesbian/Gay Pride Coordinators officially recognize and
support (philosophically) the creation of this organization.

                                                                                                   RESOLUTION 85-11
BE IT RESOLVED that the Lesbian/Gay Pride Coordinators Conference asserts that excessive charges for police, city
services, and/or insurance requirements is a direct violation of our First Amendment rights of free speech and assembly.


                                                                                                   RESOLUTION 85-12
BE IT RESOLVED that the Lesbian/Gay Pride Coordinators Conference supports the fights against discrimination in
housing, employment, the military, immigration, and marriage based on sexual affectional preference, race, sex, religion,
age, physical handicap or AIDS-related condition.

                                                                                                   RESOLUTION 86-12
BE IT RESOLVED that the smaller, less-organized organizations require and appreciate the assistance and support of the
larger, better-organized cities. We suggest a plan of support which includes making resources of people and supplies
available to us to further to cause of national and international efforts to move “Forward Together.”


                                                                                                   RESOLUTION 89-12
BE IT RESOLVED that the IAL/GPC affirms and supports the individual right of choice in regards to abortion.


                                                                                                   RESOLUTION 07-01
WHEREAS, the Employment Non- Discrimination Act or ENDA, introduced as H.R.2015 in the 110th United States
Congress on April 24, 2007, is a proposed U.S. federal law that would prohibit discrimination against employees on the
basis of sexual orientation or gender identity. The bill provides employment protections similar to those of the Civil Rights
Act of 1964 (also known as "Title VII"), but specifically directed to gay, lesbian, bisexual, and transgender employees.
The bill is different from Title VII in that it contains exemptions for religious organizations and specific provisions about
employer dress codes.
Whereas, on September 27, 2007, a revised version of the ENDA was presented to the House by Rep. Barney Frank. The
bill was numbered H.R.03685. The revised version of the bill no longer includes language regarding protections for
transgendered people.
Thereby the September version of ENDA has been protested by the majority of the LGBT rights organizations. Whereas,
on Monday, October 1, 2007, after a tremendous outcry from individuals and organizations about this sudden change
of the bill, the House leadership announced that the committee vote that was scheduled for that week was postponed.
Since then over 195 organizations have taken action to coordinate lobbying strategies, grassroots mobilizations efforts,
online activism, media and legal activities.                                                                                    7
                                                                             RESOLUTIONS
                                                                                             RESOLUTION 07-01 (CONT.)
    Therefore, InterPride joins the global community in its unified protest of this discriminatory version of the bill.
    Now be it resolved that InterPride demands that the United States Congress withdraw the current discriminatory bill
    and put forward the original inclusive bill that represents true progress in workplace protection based not just on sexual
    orientation but also on gender identity.
    Implementation:
    This resolution, once approved, will be distributed through a press release to include quotes from relevant member
    organizations and InterPride leadership and the resolution itself to media and other organizations to assist in ensuring that
    equality is inclusive. InterPride will distribute the release; additionally, member organizations are called upon to distribute
    the press release within their local communities.

                                                                                                  RESOLUTION 83-01
WHEREAS, California Magazine in their cover story “Whitewash” ran a picture of San Francisco’s Lesbian/Gay Freedom
Day Parade with the caption, “How many will take home the disease?” and;
Whereas, the First Amendment right of Lesbians and Gay males to gather for social and/or political purposes has been
recently contested on the basis of decrying such gatherings as a health hazard and;
Whereas, the Coalition for Human Rights in San Francisco has unanimously called for a boycott of California magazine
until such time as they allow equal space for a reply to their article that, through lies and distortions, condemns the Gay
community as complacent in the spread of AIDS and;
Whereas, we see such attempts to use this medical crisis to curtail our rights as a direct attack on our organizing for
National Pride Week;
Be it resolved that delegates to the National Gay Pride Coordinators Conference join in the boycott of California Magazine.


                                                                                                  RESOLUTION 86-05
WHEREAS, economic pressure has traditionally been used to further causes of those discriminated against; and;
Whereas, the religious right has used economic pressure on the Southland Corporation and 7-11 Stores to censor adult
magazines (Playboy, Playgirl, and Penthouse) from their stores and;
Whereas, most Gay men and Lesbians are equally concerned about the issues of sexual exploitation in the adult literature
industry, but feel the blackmail tactics of the Federation of Decency and other religious rightist groups is dangerous; and;
Whereas, an economic counter-pressure demonstration is the only effective way to express our concerns;
Be it therefore resolved that during Lesbian/Gay Pride Week Celebrations, people be discouraged from shopping at
7-11 stores.


                                                                                                  RESOLUTION 96-02
BE IT HEREBY RESOLVED, that the IALGPC rescind its support and endorsement of the boycott against the Adolph Coors
Brewing Company and its products as a unified organization, thereby adopting a neutral stance and allowing its member
organizations to determine their own positions.

                                                                                                  RESOLUTION 23-01
IN ORDER TO ENSURE that the communication of InterPride, the International Association of Pride Organizations focuses
on, prioritizes, reflects, and honors, first and foremost, the realities, needs, and expectations of its current 350+ Pride
member organizations from 72+ countries;
Whereas, repetitive communications issues, as well as recurring and incomprehensible actions, including suppression of
membership queries, blocking of Pride delegates on the social media outlets of InterPride, posting incorrect membership
information despite corrections, dismissing proposals and contributions from member organizations, lack of social media
representation of member organizations based outside of the US, all of which have further polarized dynamics among
Pride organizations, undermined their friendships and the support they offer each other (in particular Resolutions 86-
12 and 91-06), as well as created unnecessary controversy and drama, and undermined and hindered the trust of the
membership in InterPride;
Whereas, in order to prevent these issues from happening again, plus to improve a respectable and respectful
international organization worthy of being elected by Pride Organizations across the world as the international space
for us to meet, network, share knowledge, and grow our impact; an organization that licenses the massive event
“WorldPride,” and that has been recently, in June 2023, granted Consultative Status to the United Nations Economic and
Social Council (ECOSOC);
And in order to provide the Communications Committee with the necessary operational independence through
streamlining the design, development, and distribution of communications materials that build and leverage strategic,
focused, sensible, and realistic communication, and in particular effective and transparent communication shared with
the membership, and focusing on the membership and on our needs, and elevating our voices;
Be it resolved, that InterPride officially acknowledges these issues, and that the membership asserts its participation in
supporting the efforts for a healthy and empowering communication, as outlined in past resolutions, and beyond.

Implementation: This resolution, once approved, constitutes the communications guidelines of InterPride and the
guidelines of the communications committee. Passing the resolution requires the support of InterPride’s membership in its
immediate implementation, as outlined in the annexed document.


                                                                                                  RESOLUTION 24-01
The People Living with HIV Caucus request that InterPride implements the Undetectable Equals Untransmittable (U=U)
Global Campaign. InterPride is a current signatory.

The Caucus requests that InterPride create materials that member organizations can use in local, regional and global
campaigns to promote awareness of People Living with HIV and the U=U Global Campaign.

Implementation Plan: The Communications Committee and Board must create text, graphics, and other materials for the
InterPride supported campaign that all member organizations can use license free.

Undetectable = Untransmittable,” anti-stigma education, and broader sexual health information, remain essential tools
in reducing HIV transmission, combating misinformation, and supporting the dignity and rights of people living with HIV,
and;
Whereas, InterPride operates as a nonprofit corporation incorporated under the laws of the State of Texas, and as an
organization recognized as tax-exempt under section 501(c)(3) of the United States Internal Revenue Code, and must

law, U.S. federal law, and 501(c)(3) requirements (including prohibitions on partisan political activity and restrictions on
lobbying);
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT, that the following Operative Clauses be enacted:
    1. Explicit Inclusion of HIV & Sexual Health Projects in the Solidarity Fund Resolved, that InterPride shall direct the
    Board of Directors, or the body responsible for the Solidarity Fund (or any successor grants program), to explicitly
    prioritize at least one grant per funding cycle, where financially and legally feasible, that supports projects related to
    HIV and/or broader sexual health, including but not limited to:
        • public education and communication campaigns,
        • anti-stigma and U=U messaging,
        • community-led monitoring or access-to-care initiatives, and
        • initiatives centering on people living with HIV;
        • And that such prioritization shall be implemented in a manner consistent with InterPride’s 501(c)(3) status,
              its Texas nonprofit obligations, and its bylaws, values, and Code of Ethics & Conduct.

                                                                                                    RESOLUTION 25-01
WHEREAS, InterPride is committed to advancing global 2SLGBTQIA+ human rights and to ensuring that all Pride events
and gatherings under its umbrella are inclusive, safe, and accessible for every member of our community, and;
Trans, nonbinary, gender nonconforming, and intersex individuals continue to face disproportionate levels of violence,
discrimination, and state-sanctioned hostility in many parts of the world, particularly when traveling across borders, and;
InterPride’s events, including WorldPride and InterPride General Meeting & World Conference (GM&WC), draw trans,
nonbinary, and gender nonconforming participants, leaders, and community members from around the world, many of
whom may face increased risks due to gender presentation, documentation, or local laws, and;
Ensuring informed participation requires that all attendees, particularly trans, nonbinary, gender nonconforming, and
intersex participants, have access to accurate travel advisories and comprehensive safety information before committing
to travel, and;
InterPride has a responsibility to model best practices in harm reduction, accessibility, and community care when
approving host bids for and planning international gatherings;
THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED THAT, All future WorldPride bids must include a Travel Advisory and Safety Plan
specifically addressing the safety and inclusion of trans, nonbinary, gender nonconforming, and intersex participants, as
a mandatory component of the bid submission. The Conference Committee, in collaboration with the host organization,
must create a Travel Advisory and Safety Plan specifically addressing the safety and inclusion of trans, nonbinary, gender
nonconforming, and intersex attendees.

Implementation: The Travel Advisory and Safety Plan must include, at minimum:
    • A summary of relevant local laws and border policies affecting trans, nonbinary, gender nonconforming, and
       intersex people, including during travel;
    • An assessment of healthcare access, including availability of gender-affirming care and emergency medical support;
    • A plan for gender-inclusive facilities and accommodations, including bathrooms and housing;
    • Identification of local 2SLGBTQIA+ organizations and resources available for support; A protocol for responding
       to safety concerns, incidents, or harassment involving trans, nonbinary, and gender nonconforming attendees.
The InterPride Global Trans Task Force (IGTTF): in consultation with WorldPride committee leads, shall develop a template
and review criteria for this requirement, to be incorporated into the official Host Bidding Guidelines by the next WorldPride
bid cycle. The IGTTF will work with the Local Host and Conference Committee to complete a Travel Advisory and Safety
Plan, to be implemented by the 2027 GM&WC. InterPride will provide support and mentorship to WorldPride bidding
organizations to ensure they can meaningfully fulfill this requirement, particularly for bids from regions with developing
or emergent Pride movements, and any currently selected host locations.
For WorldPride: This requirement shall take effect beginning with the next call for WorldPride bids, and shall be reviewed
for effectiveness after its first implementation cycle.
For the InterPride GM&WC: This requirement shall take effect beginning with the opening of registration for the 2027
conference, and shall be reviewed for effectiveness after its first implementation cycle.

Rationale: This resolution strengthens InterPride’s global commitment to trans, nonbinary, gender nonconforming, and
intersex inclusion, ensuring that Pride spaces remain aligned with principles of safety, transparency, and harm reduction.
By making safety planning a bid requirement, InterPride demonstrates leadership in protecting community members
whose safety may be most at risk when traveling internationally.
