Requirements Single Applications
  ●   Must be a current, Full Member Organization of InterPride and have been in good
      standing for at least 3 consecutive calendar years prior to submitting an
      application. No waivers may be granted for this requirement.
  ●   Must have organized at least three 3 Pride Events (physical, virtual or hybrid) within
      the previous four 3 calendar years
  ●   Must have attended 2 of the previous 3 InterPride’s General Meeting and World
      Conferences, at least one (1) in person, immediately prior to the application.
      Attendance at a GM&WC requires a minimum of attending all plenaries and voting.
  ●   The applicant organization must have attended at least one (1) previous
      WorldPride

                                                                                               04 5
Requirements Single Applications
  ●   The applicant organization must have no operating deficit exceeding ten (10) percent of
      revenues in the three (3) calendar year period immediately prior to their application. No
      waivers may be granted for this requirement.
  ●   The applicant organization must propose to hold the WorldPride event at least three (3)
      years after their application is considered by the membership.
       ○   The WorldPride committee voted on taking the bids for 2028 in 2024.
  ●   The applicant organization must be present at the GM&WC at which their application is
      considered.
  ●   A non-refundable fee of USD $500, submitted to the Treasurer of InterPride, must
      accompany applications.


                                                                                                  04 6
Requirements Joint Applications
  ●   Joint applications by multiple organizations
      must be submitted by a lead organization,
      which will fulfill the requirements of Section 2
      of the Standing rule and be accountable to
      InterPride for the delivery of the event. All
      applicants must be Full Members of
      InterPride.
  ●   Joint applications must be for an event within
      a defined geographical area which would
      allow for reasonable travel by participants.

  ●   Each candidate is expected to submit a

      the membership of no more than USD

  ●   Must include any expenditures that benefited
      other members, such as registration fees or
      travel costs




                                                        05 8
Campaigning Policy
  ●   Candidates are permitted to hand out gifts or
      financially support members provided these
      are disclosed publicly
  ●   May not give members or representatives
      anything that has financial value in direct
      exchange for votes
  ●   Board members of InterPride are not allowed
      to accept anything of monetary value from a
      bidding organization.
