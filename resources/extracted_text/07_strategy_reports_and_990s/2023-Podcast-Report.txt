Introduction
We are in our 2nd year of InterPod, The Global Voices of Pride Podcast, Powered by
InterPride, and have made incredible progress as a credible resource to bring our global
LGBTQIA+ community together but as a Webby-nominated podcast for our public service
and outreach. This year, we not only produced over 15 original episodes but also launched
“The Elephant Series” in response to the experiences that came out of the 2022 World
Conference in Guadalajara. Folks felt it was dire and necessary to have a platform for
authentic conversations around racism. In response to the request, the series was
produced as part of the Racism & Oppression Awareness Program (ROAP) and Richard
Brethour-Bell serves as host. We were able to tape the episodes at Sydney WorldPride,
where the conversations included Black and Indigenous speakers from around the world.

This year, the podcast also served as the central engine in bringing in some new members
to the InterPride family from Asia, which we will detail in this report. InterPod catalyzed
conversations centered around democracy, LGBTQIA+ rights, Gender Equity, racism,
xenophobia, and other issues. This advantage generated interest in bringing InterPride’s
impact and resources to regions where the organization has an opportunity to expand. For
example, Bangkok Pride has become a serious new member to try to bring InterPride
activities to the area while promoting new members and the chance to bring WorldPride to
the region.

Finally, the biggest impact and highlight for 2023 and InterPod this year has been increased
membership voices to the platform. We made an effort to engage our membership so that
they felt they had a voice year-round and not just during the World Conference. We’ve
produced episodes from members of Oxford Pride, Bangkok Pride, SF Pride (produced a
series on what’s going on in the USA and anti-lgbtqia+ bills- all programs during their
inaugural Human Rights Summit), Jersey Pride, Uganda, EPOA, Hong Kong, Mexico,
Argentina, Canada, and more.

Membership Engagement

The production of InterPod has allowed more member voices to be heard this year. We
launched “The Elephant in the Room Series” under the ROAP program with the host VP
Richard Brethour-Bell. We also produced a short series touching on the anti-LGBTQIA+ bills
being proposed and passed in the USA with member SF Pride who produced their
inaugural Human Rights Summit to address the issues. In addition, we continued to cover
central Europe in war-torn regions such as Ukraine and had a deep discussion with
Uganda, which led to some financial support once the anti-LGBTQIA+ bill passed in the
country. These are examples of the over 15+ episodes produced. The reach of these
episodes is far more impactful than the outreach or # of listeners, as we have been told
we’ve saved some lives. Some other episodes we’ve done this year that affected the lives of
many: Trans in Pakistan, Update on Afghanistan and LGBTQIA+, life after marriage equality

Results
Our total outreach numbers and listenership are in the thousands, if not millions if we
count the earned media we’ve received since producing InterPod. Here are the most
impactful results stemming from InterPod:

   1. Membership increase.
   2. Being considered for a major grant from a large corporate funder.
   3. Saving lives and finding resources for our members and members at large.

Limited Data Tracking Sources
Data privacy restrictions can prevent us from obtaining the most accurate data and
analytics in certain countries. We are distributed on 21 different platforms as of October 3,
2023. To provide a solid baseline of our listenership, since we use the Buzzsprout podcast
player embed feature per episode on our website, the current traffic to this page most
accurately reflects our reach.

   ●   From November 2022 to October 3, 2023, the number of users who visited our
       podcast landing page totaled 26,853.

   ●   The event count, also known as clicks on an embedded podcast player on the
       website, totaled 98,632.

Conclusion
It has been a pleasure to produce InterPod. I did not realize the impact until I was writing
this report. There is still much work to do, so I would be most grateful to continue the work.
Thank you for your support of InterPod.
