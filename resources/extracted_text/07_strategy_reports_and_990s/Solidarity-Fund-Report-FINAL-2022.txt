     from individuals. Every dollar is doled out in response to genuine
     requirements.

SOLIDARITY FUND OVERVIEW
InterPride is committed to supporting the evolving needs of our LGBTQIA+ community. The
Solidarity Fund provides grants to LGBTQIA+ Pride or Pride-like organizations around the
world for community-based Pride events, with a focus on Global South and Eastern Europe,
where public LGBTQIA+ activities often face opposition or hostility. Grants are not awarded
to individuals.


Grant Amount
Under the Solidarity Fund, InterPride awards grants up to a maximum award of USD $2,000.
Note that the maximum grant is rarely awarded to enable the total amount of funding available
to be distributed as widely as possible.

FUTURE RECOMMENDATIONS

The following are three key recommendations for the future development
of the Solidarity Fund from the 2022 Co-Chairs.

Recommendation One
Increase the size of the Solidarity Fund’s donor pool by drafting grant applications and
submitting them to a wider variety of worldwide foundations, businesses, governments, and
individual contributors who are not members of InterPride.


Recommendation Two
Develop closer ties between organizations that have received funding from the Solidarity
Fund and those that are members of InterPride. This might include forming relationships
between Prides worldwide, participating in regional Pride organizations, or introducing new
webinars and conference calls for Prides still blossoming as cultural events for the LGBTQIA+
community.

Recommendation Three
Investigate the idea of integrating, aligning, or combining the Solidarity Fund with the
Scholarship Fund that InterPride maintains so that developing Prides supported by Solidarity
might be given a chance to participate in the annual global conference.
