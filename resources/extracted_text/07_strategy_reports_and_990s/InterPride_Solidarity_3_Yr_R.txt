Funding Criteria & Processes
From 2015 to 2017 the Solidarity Fund used the following primary
granting criteria and priorities:

   •   Projects in the global south and east (outside the
       USA/Canada, western Europe and Australia/NZ) would be
       a priority for the fund.
   •   Projects must support specific local events and activities    f
       that support LGBTI communities, including local Pride
       events.
   •   Projects must be time-limited, such as political marches,
       parades, community gatherings, film festivals, special
       events, and celebrations.
   •   Project funding is allowed to cover costs such as staff time,
       advertising, space or equipment rentals, registration fees,
       purchase of materials.

What we will not fund:

   •   Individuals not connected to an organization
   •   Ongoing operating costs for organizations
   •   Ongoing program costs
   •   Capacity building or training
   •   Paying off organizational debt

These criteria were developed by the leadership of the Solidarity
Fund Committee and reviewed by members of our Advisory
Committee. Criteria were publicized on InterPride’s website

Recommendations for the Future

The following are three key recommendations
for the future development of the Solidarity Fund
from the 2015-2017 Co-Chairs:

                      Recommendation 1: Expand the funding base of the
                      Solidarity Fund through the development of funding
                      proposals to a diverse range of international
                      foundations, corporations, governments and
                      individual donors outside of the InterPride
                      membership.




                      Recommendation 2: Enhance the connections
                      between Solidarity Fund grantees with InterPride
                      membership. This could involve creating partnerships
                      between Prides globally, engagement in regional
                      Pride associations, or new webinars and conference
                      calls for emerging Prides and other LGBTI cultural
                      events.


                      Recommendation 3: Explore the possibility of linking,
                      aligning or merging the Solidarity Fund with
                      InterPride’s Scholarship Fund, so that emerging Prides
                      funded through Solidarity have the opportunity to
                      attend the annual global conference.
