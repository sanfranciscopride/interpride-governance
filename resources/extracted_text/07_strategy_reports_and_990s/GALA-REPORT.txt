                                                               Gala Successes

                                                               Our in-person audience of 350 guests included brands, donors, organizations
                                                               and entertainment industry leaders. A majority of them had not previously
                                                               heard of InterPride or our work with member organizations, WorldPride, AGM,
                                                               podcast or global funding.

                                                               We honored visionaries whose work has shown up in accountability, activism,
                                                               gender equality, and other aspects of our mission. Through theirs and our
                                                               stories, and the solidly curated script, the professional production, we were able
                                                               to engage more supporters. They saw a professional organization whose work
                                                               has made a global impact for 40 years.

                                                               We far exceed advertising and marketing goals to elevate InterPride’s brand.
                                                               Because the evening was impeccably produced by JJLA, the experience itself
                                                               left guests with a positive vibe about InterPride. Lucky Break PR elevated the
                                                               press and media. Click this Gala Coverage Book link for detailed impressions
                                                               and coverage.

                                                               We now have access to a wider list of contacts, sponsors, brands and individual
                                                               donors, as well as videos and marketing tools for future outreach.
