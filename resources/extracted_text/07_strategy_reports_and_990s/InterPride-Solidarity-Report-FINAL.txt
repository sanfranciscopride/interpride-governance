    Funding Criteria & Process 2015
    This year the Solidarity Fund developed the following granting
    criteria:

       •   Projects must take place in the 2015 calendar year.
       •   Projects in the global south and east (outside the
           USA/Canada, western Europe and Australia/NZ) would be a
           priority for the fund.                                            f
       •   Projects must support specific local events and activities that
           support LGBTI communities, including local Pride events.
       •   Projects must be time-limited, such as political marches,
           parades, community gatherings, film festivals, special events,
           and celebrations.
       •   Project funding is allowed to cover costs such as staff time,
           advertising, space or equipment rentals, registration fees,
           purchase of materials.

    What we will not fund:

       •   Individuals not connected to an organization
       •   Ongoing operating costs for organizations
       •   Ongoing program costs
       •   Capacity building or training
       •   Paying off organizational debt

    These criteria were developed by the leadership of the Solidarity
    Fund Committee and reviewed by members of our Advisory
    Committee. Criteria were publicized on InterPride’s website
    throughout the application period.

Grantees for 2015

The following are the successful grantees for 2015




                Biliitis Resource Centre (Sofia, Bulgaria)

                $1500 to cover expenses of Sofia Pride Art Week in June 2015,
                including a photo exhibit called “All Love is Equal” and a video
                /photo exhibition on the history of the global LGBT rights
                movement.




                Hong Kong Pride (Hong Kong, China)

                $1000 to cover expenses of the Hong Kong Parade in November
                2015, including parade promotional expenses and production of
                the parade including stage and sound systems.




                Women’s Initiatives (Tirupati, India)

                $1250 to support three public events involving song, drama,
                and speeches to create visibility for LGBTI communities in
                three southern states: Andhra Pradesh, Kerala and Tamil Nadu.




                Nazariya Foundation (New Delhi, India)

                $1250 to support a two-day cultural event as a prelude to Delhi
                Queer Pride in November 2015. Funds will be used for
                performers to explore nuances of caste, religion, gender,
                ethnicity and sexuality.




                Ishtar MSM (Nairobi, Kenya)

                $1000 to support costs of a flash mob/community event for the
                members of the Gay and Lesbian Coalition of Kenya. The event
                will bring visibility to the coalition members at a major
                shopping mall in the central business district of Nairobi.

Recommendations for 2016

The following are recommendations for the future
development of the fund based on our evaluation of
2015 process and grants.

                Recommendation 1: For 2016, begin the grant cycle much
                earlier in the year. Many organizations were not able to apply
                in 2015 as their events took place in May or June. Ideally, the
                grant application should open in January, with a February
                deadline, March review and April approval.




                Recommendation 2: Improve the application form with clear
                criteria on evaluation linked to each question. Try and ensure
                that organizations keep their responses simple and clarify the
                activities of the event from the general activities of the
                organization.




                Recommendation 3: Explore the idea of inviting some past
                grantee representatives onto the review committee. Look to
                funder best practices around how to engage with activists in
                grantmaking review.




                Recommendation 4: Provide a stronger orientation to both
                advisory members and volunteer review committee members.
                Clearly explain the criteria and how to evaluate each
                application.




                Recommendation 5: Hold back a small amount of funding for a
                discretionary pot – maybe 10% of the total granting amount –
                for use past the deadline. Be transparent and clear on the
                website about how this will be decided and how to apply.
