# Texas Business Organizations Code (BOC) Legal Reference

This directory contains clean, searchable reference copies of the State of Texas statutes governing **InterPride** as a domestic nonprofit corporation incorporated in Texas.

## Statutes in This Collection

1. [**Chapter 22: Nonprofit Corporations**](texas_boc_chapter_22_nonprofit_corporations.md)
   - **Form and Bylaws:** Formation, purposes, powers, and adoption/amendment of bylaws (§§ 22.051–22.109).
   - **Members & Governance:** Classes of members, meetings, voting rights, proxies, and delegate governance (§§ 22.151–22.164).
   - **Board of Directors:** Authority, composition, terms, committees, and removal of directors (§§ 22.201–22.217).
   - **Fiduciary Standards (§ 22.221):** The core statutory duty requiring directors to discharge duties in *good faith, with ordinary care, and in a manner reasonably believed to be in the best interest of the corporation*. (Explicitly incorporated into InterPride's annual *Organization Agreement*).
   - **Officers:** Powers, election, and authority (§§ 22.225–22.235).
   - **Books and Records (§§ 22.351–22.354):** Financial record-keeping, seven-year audit retention mandates, and member public inspection rights.

2. [**Chapter 8: Indemnification and Insurance**](texas_boc_chapter_8_indemnification.md)
   - Mandatory and permissive indemnification of directors, officers, employees, and committee agents against legal liabilities and expenses.
   - Standards of conduct required for advancement of defense expenses.

3. [**Chapter 6: Meetings and Voting for Domestic Entities**](texas_boc_chapter_6_meetings_and_voting.md)
   - Procedural rules for notices, waiver of notice, quorum calculations, remote/electronic communication meetings, and action by written consent without a meeting.
