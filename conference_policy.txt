1.2 SHARED PRINCIPLES
InterPride and the Local Host agree to collaborate in accordance with the following principles:
* Equity: Decision-making and responsibility are shared to the greatest extent possible.
* Transparency: Both parties will maintain open communication and access to all relevant information.
* Accountability: Each party commits to fulfilling its responsibilities and reporting progress to the other.
* Community Focus: The GM&WC will center LGBTQIA+ community priorities, accessibility, and inclusion.
* Safety and Care: The planning and implementation of all activities will prioritize the well-being of participants, staff, and volunteers.
* Sustainability: Each party commits to act in the best interests of all Organizations involved.
* Legal Compliance & Ethics: Each Party will conduct all planning, fundraising, and event delivery activities in compliance with applicable laws and regulations of the relevant jurisdictions, including, but not limited to, anti-corruption, anti-money laundering, sanctions, and data protection laws. The Parties agree to apply InterPride’s ethical partnership guidelines and Local Host cultural and political sensitivity guidance in sponsorship, programming, and communications decisions.

   * Point of contact for local arrangements
   * Authority to coordinate local volunteers
   * Responsible for local stakeholder relationships
   * Reports directly to Conference Committee Chairs, including active participation in bi-weekly (every two weeks) Conference Committee meetings and monthly JPC meetings


3.2 Communication Protocol:
* Bi-weekly progress meetings via video conference, to occur during regularly scheduled Conference Committee meetings
   * Required attendance and participation from: 1) Conference Committee, 2) lead coordinator(s) and/or designated representative(s) from Local Host 
* Bi-weekly written updates during and/or in conjunction with regularly scheduled Conference Committee meetings, including:
   * Progress against timeline (both Parties)
   * Budget updates (Conference Committee)
   * Risk register review (Conference Committee)
   * Outstanding decisions needed (both Parties)
* All formal communications must be documented in writing and stored in the shared drive and/or project management system. Database and/or project management system will be managed by InterPride.
   * All regular status updates are to be shared with both organizations' leadership
* Response required within 24 hours for urgent matters


3.3 Meetings
3.3.1 Conference Committee Meetings
   * Meeting cadence: Bi-weekly (every two weeks).
   * Purpose: Lead the planning and execution of GM&WC, ensure compliance with InterPride governing documents
   * Required attendance: All key personnel identified above or their proxies. Absences are to be communicated to Conference Committee Chairs prior to meetings. 
      * Note: Conference Committee meetings that primarily focus on 2027 are not required for Local Host representatives.
3.3.2 JPC Meetings
   * Meeting cadence: Once per month (at minimum)
   * Purpose: Oversee the planning and execution of the GM&WC
   * Required attendance: All Parties identified in Section 4 below or their proxies. Absences are to be communicated to the JPC chairs prior to meetings.
3.3.3 Leadership Coordination Meetings (ad hoc)
   * Meeting cadence: In addition to regular Conference Committee and JPC meetings, the Parties agree that ad hoc leadership coordination meetings may be convened as needed.
   * Purpose: Resolving time-sensitive issues, financial decisions, or safety concerns
   * Required attendance: Co-Presidents, Treasurer, Conference Committee Chairs, and the lead coordinator(s) from each Local Host Organization


4. Governance and Decision-Making


The planning and execution of the Event shall be done in accordance with InterPride’s GM&WC Policies and Procedures in effect on the Effective Date, a copy of which is annexed hereto. 


InterPride retains final decision-making authority over: (i) organizational governance and GM&WC business agenda; (ii) InterPride brand usage and ethical sponsorship guidelines; and (iii) any decision that would exceed the overall budget cap approved by the InterPride Board. 


Local Host retains final decision-making authority over: (i) local stakeholder relationships and (ii) local cultural elements, subject to budget and safety review.


Local Host is solely responsible for ensuring compliance with the legal requirements of Thailand and  local jurisdiction(s) for events, sponsorships, and public activities.


A Joint Planning Committee (JPC) will be established to oversee the planning and execution of the GM&WC. The JPC will be the decision-making body for all GM&WC financial, programmatic, and operational matters, including approval of budget adjustment recommendations.
* The JPC will be co-chaired by one representative from InterPride and one representative from the Local Host.
* Local Host members of the JPC will work in collaboration with the Conference Committee. At least one Local Host member of the JPC will be assigned to each of the InterPride Conference Committee Work Groups: 
   * Accessibility, Safety, & Wellness Work Group
   * Conference Communications Work Group:
   * Conference Hotel Work Group
   * Conference Technology & Logistics Work Group
   * Receptions, Gala, & Local Culture Work Group
   * Scholarship & Visa Process Work Group
   * Sponsorship Work Group
   * Workshops & Caucuses Work Group
* In addition to the above Work Groups, the JPC will convene an Executive Work Group with responsibility for monitoring and reviewing the finances of the GM&WC, and making recommendations to the Co-Presidents on financial decisions. The Executive Work Group will consist of key personnel, including but not limited to:
   * InterPride Co-President(s)
   * InterPride Treasurer
   * Conference Committee Chairs
   * One (1) designated lead from each Local Host organization
* Decisions will be made by consensus where possible. If consensus cannot be reached, decisions may be made by simple majority vote, with each party holding equal representation.
   * Notwithstanding the foregoing, InterPride retains final decision-making authority over: (i) organizational governance and GM&WC business agenda; (ii) InterPride brand usage and ethical sponsorship guidelines; and (iii) any decision that would exceed the overall budget cap approved by the InterPride Board. Local Host Organizations retain final decision-making authority over: (i) local stakeholder relationships; (ii) compliance with Thai legal requirements for events, sponsorships, and public activities; and (iii) local cultural elements, subject to budget and safety review.
* The JPC will meet monthly at minimum, though frequency can be adjusted based on mutual agreement. These meetings may be scheduled as add-ons to Conference Committee meetings.

   * Crisis communication protocol for various scenarios

      * Communication procedures
      * Alternative scheduling options
