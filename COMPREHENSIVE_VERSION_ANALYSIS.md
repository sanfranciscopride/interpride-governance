# Comprehensive Multi-Version Governance Analysis & Audit (2022 – 2026)

> **Prepared for:** Chris Robert (`chris.robert@interpride.org`), Co-Chair, InterPride Governance Committee
> **Date:** October 18, 2026
> **Scope:** Complete longitudinal analysis across all downloaded editions of InterPride's Bylaws, Standing Rules, Resolutions, Global Advisory Council (GAC) Rules & Regulations, Policies, and Agreements from **2022 through August 2026**.

---

## Executive Summary

We have ingested **182 governing, policy, financial, and administrative documents** across two primary sources:
1. **Public Website Archive (`interpride.org/resources/` & WordPress Media Library):** 140 PDFs spanning `2008–2024` (with governing documents frozen at late 2023 / Jan 2024).
2. **Governance Email & Internal Archive (`chris.robert@interpride.org` via Gmail):** 42 files spanning `2025–2026`, including the December 2025 Bylaws, April 2026 Standing Rules, March 2026 Resolutions, July 2026 GAC Rules & Regulations, 2026 Organization Agreement & Contact/Storage Directory, and April–August 2026 Governance Committee Minutes.

### Why a Canonical "Just the Docs" Digital Site is Urgently Needed
Our line-by-line diff across all versions (`2022` → `2023` → `2024` → `2025` → `2026`) uncovered **critical copy-paste corruptions, accidentally deleted sections, unreplaced template placeholders, and cross-document contradictions** introduced during manual PDF layout edits (especially in the `12.12.2025` Bylaws and `04.06.2026` Standing Rules).

Furthermore, the **Public Website (`interpride.org/resources/`) is 2+ years behind** the actual governing documents in force, still serving the `January 20, 2024` Bylaws, `October 11, 2023` Standing Rules, `December 2023` Resolutions, and `January 31, 2024` GAC Rules.

## Part 2: Critical Governance Audit — Corruptions, Bugs & Contradictions in Current (2025–2026) Documents

Before building the canonical digital edition, the Governance Committee should be aware of **11 specific textual defects and cross-document conflicts** we discovered in the current `2025–2026` PDFs:

### 1. CRITICAL: Copy-Paste Text Corruption in `Bylaws (12.12.2025)` Article V, Sections 12.B & 14
In [IP_Bylaws_Approved_English_12.12.2025.txt](file:///Users/chris/Developer/interpride/governance/resources/extracted_text/00_2025_2026_email_versions/IP_Bylaws_Approved_English_12.12.2025.txt#L283-L343), when the December 2024 and December 2025 amendments were typeset into the PDF:
- **`Article V, Section 12.B` (Affiliation, Endorsement & 60-Day Dues Requirement) was accidentally overwritten** with the vacancy replacement text that belongs in `Section 14`!
  - *What `Sec 12.B` said in `01.20.2024`:* *"To be nominated for an Officer position, an individual must be affiliated with and endorsed by a Full Member organization at the time of nomination... Their endorsing organization must qualify to be a Full Member within the first sixty (60) days of the new year or the Officer shall forfeit their position."*
  - *What `Sec 12.B` says in `12.12.2025`:* *"When a seat becomes vacant, there must be a call out to the membership for nominees from Full Member Organizations... Any replacements between the GM&WC are ratified in a joint majority vote of the Board and Global Advisory Council."*
- **`Article V, Section 14.A` (Vacancies) was accidentally overwritten** with a duplicate copy of `Section 15.A` (Term of Office start/end dates), and **`Section 14.B` starts mid-sentence** as a dangling fragment:
  - *What `Sec 14.A–B` says in `12.12.2025`:*
    > `A.` The term of office for each Officer elected at a General Meeting and World Conference (GM&WC) shall begin thirty (30) days after the conclusion of that GM&WC...
    > `B.` majority of those present and voting at a joint meeting of the Board and the Global Advisory Council.
  - *Result:* Both `Section 12.B` (endorsement/affiliation requirement for nominees) and `Section 14.A–B` (how vacancies occur and how Co-President vs. non-Co-President vacancies are filled) were mangled in the `12.12.2025` PDF layout!

### A. Bylaws Evolution (`Dec 2022` → `Sept 2023` → `Jan 2024` → `Dec 12, 2025`)

1. **`2022-12` → `2023-09-29` (2023 San Diego GM&WC Amendments):**
   - **Article I, Sec 2 (Purpose):** Added explicit language empowering InterPride to *"combat bias and bigotry in all forms; eliminate barriers and increase accessibility so that all people might fully participate"* and added *"Pride Events"* to the list of protected groups/events ([Purpose Amendment PDF](file:///Users/chris/Developer/interpride/governance/resources/02_bylaw_amendments/2023_11_Purpose_Bylaws_Amendment_2023-English.pdf)).
   - **Article V, Sec 1 (Board of Directors):**
     - Split `Vice President of Diversity, Inclusion, and Accessibility` into two separate Officer roles: `Up to One (1) Vice President of Diversity and Inclusion` and `Up to One (1) Vice President of Accessibility` ([Pride Winnipeg Amendment PDF](file:///Users/chris/Developer/interpride/governance/resources/02_bylaw_amendments/2023_11_Pride-Winnipeg-Bylaw-amendment_2023-English.pdf)). *(Note: The old combined title was accidentally left in the list until Dec 2025!)*
     - Added *"One (1) Emeritus Council representative"* to the Board ([Emeritus Council Amendment PDF](file:///Users/chris/Developer/interpride/governance/resources/02_bylaw_amendments/2023_11_Emeritus-Council_Bylaws-Amendment_2023-English.pdf)).

2. **`2023-09-29` → `2023-09-29_V1` → `2024-01-20` (Jan 2024 Corrections):**
   - **`2023-09-29_V1`:** Clarified in `Article V, Sec 1` that the Emeritus Council seat is **`One (1) non-voting Emeritus Council representative`**.
   - **`2024-01-20`:** Fixed `Article VII, Sec 3 (Representation on the Board)` where the 2022/2023 text still said the GAC elects *"six (6) of its members"* to the Board, updating it to **`three (3) of its members`** to match `Article V, Sec 1`.

3. **`2024-01-20` → `2025-12-12` (Dec 2024 Medellín & Dec 2025 Amendments):**
   - **Article II (Definitions):**
     - `F (Board)` & `G (GAC Board)`: Changed from *"six representatives appointed"* to *"three representatives elected/appointed"* by the GAC.
     - `J` & `P`: Standardized `LGBTI+` to `LGBTQI+`.
     - Added **`X. Emeritus Council`**, **`Y. Woman`**, **`Z. Man`**, **`AA. Trans`**, **`BB. Non-Binary People`**, and **`CC. Gender Diverse`** with globally inclusive cultural gender terms (*fakaleiti, leiti, muxes, chibados, two-spirit, yan daudu, hijra, khawaja sara, waria, māhū, bakla*).
   - **Article V, Sec 1 (Board Composition):**
     - **Co-Presidents:** Changed from *"no more than one (1) of whom may be a cisgender man; and one of whom must be a cisgender woman, trans, non-binary or gender non-conforming"* to:
       > *"Three (3) Co-Presidents; one (1) position held by a woman, one (1) position held by a man, and one (1) position held by a non-binary or gender diverse person; two (2) of the three (3) seats must be filled at all times."*
     - Removed the duplicate `One (1) Vice President of Diversity, Inclusion, and Accessibility` line.
   - **Article V, Sec 12 (Nominations — Amended Dec 6, 2024):**
     - Replaced the requirement of attending 1 of the 2 prior GM&WCs with:
       1. Must be present at the GM&WC (in-person or virtually);
       2. Must have **actively served on at least one (1) InterPride committee or working group for at least one (1) full year prior** (defined as **$\ge 70\%$ meeting attendance**);
       3. Must have **proven leadership experience** (Co-Chair/Project Lead on a committee/working group OR leadership role in their member Pride/2SLGBTQIA+ organization).
   - **Article V, Sec 13.E (Elections):** Replaced *"Methods & Standards Committee"* with *"the body responsible for conducting the election"*.
   - **Article V, Sec 18.G (Removal):** Added explicit ground for removal: *"Loss of endorsement and/or affiliation, or a lapse in membership fees past 60 days from the start of the year."*
   - **Article VIII, Sec 1 & 2 (GM&WC Frequency & Location):**
     - Frequency changed from annual to **"at least once every two years"** (no later than 25 months after the previous GM&WC).
     - Host location changed from Membership approval to **"approved by a vote of the Board"**, and empowered the Board to designate a **"fully virtual or hybrid format as the hosting arrangement"**.
