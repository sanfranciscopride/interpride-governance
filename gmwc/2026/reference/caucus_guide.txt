About InterPride Caucuses
Caucuses provide an opportunity for InterPride members and conference attendees to network and collaborate based on shared identities or interests. The Conference Committee has identified a host/facilitator for each caucus or networking group below. 


Some caucuses are open to allies, while others are closed spaces. This is noted in the list below.


Access and Support
All registration links are here: GM&WC 2025 Caucuses & Regional Meetings LINKS 
Facilitators will be added as alternative hosts. Tech/accessibility host(s) will be present at all caucuses (with consideration for closed/open spaces). 


Your Role as Facilitator
* Your role is not to make a presentation or create an agenda
* Your role is to:
   * Allow the conversation to be shaped by the attendees of the caucus
   * Redirect if the conversation is veering far off-course or becoming offensive
   * For closed caucuses: ensure the space is held only by those who should be there. Ask folks to self-select out and leave if they are not a member of the caucus.
   * Provide a short report-out at Plenary 2 (if desired)
* You may need or want to: 
   * Ask all attendees to introduce themselves (typically, this includes name, pronouns, Pride organization, and an intro relevant to the caucus - eg how you identify)
   * Prepare some open-ended questions to start the conversation or keep the conversation going 
   * Ensure tasks are delegated, if the caucus has action items they would like to move forward (eg proposing bylaw amendment or resolution, creating or adding to a group chat on Signal, setting up recurring meetings throughout the year, etc)
      * Governance Chairs can provide technical support re: proposing a resolution or amendment: email governance.chairs@interpride.org) 


After The Caucus:
* Coordinate with in-room tech or accessibility host to add the total # of attendees to the FIRST column of this spreadsheet: GM&WC 2025 Volunteer - Zoom Tech and Accessibility Shifts 
* Email Conference Chairs and Co-Presidents to inform them of whether or not you/your caucus will present a report-out at Plenary 2 (csic.chairs@interpride.org, copresidents@interpride.org). Report-outs are not required, but are an option for all caucuses.
   * IF YES: Prepare a brief (1-2 minute) report-out at Plenary 2
