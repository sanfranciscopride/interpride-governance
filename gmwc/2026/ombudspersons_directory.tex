    \vspace{1.5mm}
    \noindent{\normalsize\bfseries\color{primary} Mandate of the Conference Ombudspersons (SR 04.04)}\\[1mm]
    \footnotesize
    In accordance with \textbf{InterPride Standing Rules (SR 04.04)}, the Ombudspersons are designated neutrals appointed by the Governance Committee to provide independent, confidential, and informal conflict resolution, problem-solving, and support services to all conference delegates, presenters, volunteers, and attendees.
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Independent \& Impartial:} Ombudspersons do not represent any side in a dispute, nor are they active Board members or Host Committee leaders.
        \item \textbf{Confidentiality Guaranteed:} Inquiries and discussions remain privileged and safe. Personal details are never disclosed without affirmative consent.
        \item \textbf{Areas of Support:} Investigating participant complaints, accessibility barriers, safety concerns, conduct or interpersonal friction, misgendering, discrimination, and event accessibility.
    \end{itemize}
