### Mandate and Purpose of Conference Ombudspersons
In accordance with **InterPride Standing Rules ([SR 04.04](file:///Users/chris/Developer/interpride/governance/docs/standing-rules/sr-04-04.md))**, Ombudspersons are designated neutrals appointed by the Governance Committee to provide independent, confidential, and informal conflict resolution, problem-solving, and support services to all conference delegates, presenters, volunteers, and attendees.

* **Independent & Impartial:** Ombudspersons do not represent any side in a dispute, nor are they active Board members or Host Committee leaders.
* **Strict Confidentiality:** Conversations, feedback, and inquiries remain strictly confidential. No identifying information is shared without affirmative consent.
* **Areas of Support:** Investigating complaints, barriers to accessibility, attendee safety concerns, interpersonal friction or conduct issues, misgendering, discrimination, and event accessibility barriers.

### How to Access an Ombudsperson During GM&WC 2026

1. **In-Person / Direct Messaging:** Approach any Ombudsperson at the venue during conference sessions, or contact them directly via email or phone/WhatsApp.
2. **Confidential Online Submission Form:** Access the official conference feedback form: ***Form to share questions, comments, issues & feedback with Ombudspersons*** (available on Sched and in the *Important Links & Information* delegate packet).
3. **Governance Committee Escalation:** Under Standing Rule SR 04.04 Section 5, Governance Committee Co-Chairs provide operational support to the Ombudspersons. Any matters requiring systemic follow-up can be escalated to [governance.chairs@interpride.org](mailto:governance.chairs@interpride.org).
