## 1. Executive Summary & Context

A complaint has been formally received from an InterPride member regarding the interpersonal conduct, approach, and tone of an InterPride representative (**Andrew**) toward Board members during the General Meeting & World Conference. 

The reported behavior has been characterized as unprofessional, disrespectful, and inconsistent with the standards expected of individuals representing InterPride in public and conference environments, raising concerns that this conduct harms InterPride’s institutional reputation and the welcoming environment owed to attending delegates.

**Governance Determination & Immediate Framework:**  
1. **Applicability:** Under [Standing Rule SR 08.04](file:///Users/chris/Developer/interpride/governance/docs/standing-rules/sr-08-04.md) (*Code of Ethics and Conduct*), all InterPride representatives—including Board members, Committee/Working Group leads, contractors/staff, and general conference participants—are strictly bound by interpersonal conduct standards, professionalism, and mutual respect.
2. **Jurisdiction & Progressive Process:** InterPride does not permit summary or informal retaliation; rather, [SR 08.04 Section 3](file:///Users/chris/Developer/interpride/governance/docs/standing-rules/sr-08-04.md#L45) mandates a structured **4-Step Progressive Discipline Process** administered by the Governance Committee Co-Chairs.
3. **Immediate On-Site Step (Step 1 Verbal Guidance):** To de-escalate tension on the ground in Phuket without disrupting proceedings, a confidential, direct verbal reminder must be delivered to the individual by leadership (Governance Co-Chairs or a Co-President), coupled with clear boundary expectations for the remainder of the conference.

## 2. Controlling Governing Authorities & Exact Citations

### A. Professionalism & Respect Standards
* **Citation:** *InterPride Standing Rules*, **SR 08.04** (*Code of Ethics and Conduct*), Purpose & Section 1:
  > *"InterPride seeks to maintain a professional and ethical environment. It is imperative that all Board Members, Global Advisory Council Members (GACs), Members, Individuals, Staff/Contractors/Consultants, and Officers conduct themselves in a professional manner... protect our reputation and integrity, and to operate the organization/their duties in an open and transparent manner, with accountability and integrity."*  
  > *"Whether through membership, elected or assigned roles, or attendance as a participant of any official InterPride activity (conferences, meetings, events), the Code of Ethics and Conduct, with the Values Statement and other governing documents, policies, and procedures, will apply equally."*

### B. Progressive Discipline Framework
* **Citation:** *InterPride Standing Rules*, **SR 08.04**, Section 3.A:
  > *"PROCESS – Progressive notice comprised of the following steps...*  
  > *1. **First Violation – A verbal reminder** will be given to the Subject(s), if the situation is applicable (else the Governance Co-Chairs can issue one via email). This can be done by either of the Co-Presidents, Governance Co-Chairs, the person(s) chairing a meeting or producing an event where the incident occurred, or anyone in an InterPride leadership role attendant at the time, making sure to report it to the Governance Co-Chairs within 24 hours...*  
  > *2. **Second Violation – A written warning** will be issued, within 24 hours of the incident or subsequent report filed, to the Subject(s) by the Governance Co-Chairs, or a complaint is filed with them and the Co-Presidents... with notice of possible suspension if the violation(s) continue.*  
  > *3. **Third Violation – Suspension or removal** of the Subject(s) from participation from the activities directly affected, as determined by the Governance Co-Chairs, and with input from the body directly overseeing that area."*

### C. Informal Resolution via Ombudspersons
* **Citation:** *InterPride Standing Rules*, **SR 04.04** (*Ombudspersons*), Section 6.G:
  > *"The following areas of concern fall under the role of the Ombudspersons, and may be investigated by them... Vibe/Conduct (Anger, Disrespect, Disruption, Bullying, Marginalizing, Patronization – address globally by InterPride)."*

## 3. Operational Action Plan (Conference Ground Protocol)

To resolve this issue constructively and protect the conference atmosphere while respecting due process:

### Step 1: Formal Intake & Documentation (Immediate)
* The complainant should be thanked for bringing the concern forward and advised that the matter is being addressed through InterPride's standard governance protocols under SR 08.04.
* Specific factual details (time, location, witnesses, statements made) should be logged in writing with the Governance Committee Co-Chairs.

### Step 2: Leadership De-escalation & Verbal Reminder (Within 12 Hours)
* A private, informal conversation should occur between Andrew and either:
  * A Governance Committee Co-Chair (Chris Robert or Morne Claassen-Smit), or
  * An InterPride Co-President.
* **Talking Points for the Conversation:**
  1. Note that feedback has been received regarding recent interpersonal interactions with Board members and delegates.
  2. Remind Andrew that amidst conference pressures, all representatives must maintain a calm, professional, and respectful tone in public and working areas.
  3. Reiterate that visible friction undermines member confidence and organizational unity during the GM&WC.
  4. Offer an opportunity for him to voice any frustrations privately so they can be addressed constructively through proper leadership channels rather than on the floor.

### Step 3: Progressive Escalation Path (Only if Conduct Persists)
* If unprofessional conduct continues after the verbal reminder, Governance Co-Chairs will immediately issue a formal **Written Warning (Step 2)** pursuant to SR 08.04 Section 3.A.2.
* If severe disruption or insubordination occurs that compromises conference operations, leadership holds authority under SR 08.04 Section 3.B / Section 3.A.3 to temporarily restrict event participation or backstage access.
