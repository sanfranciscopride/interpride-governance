## 1. Executive Summary & Narrative Overview

A complaint was received concerning a conference workshop session where the presentation was delivered in a language other than English, leading an English-speaking delegate to feel excluded and walk out of the room.

**Official Governance Finding:**  
1. **Multilingual Delivery is Permitted and Protected:** While InterPride’s official administrative language is English ([SR 03.08 § 1](file:///Users/chris/Developer/interpride/governance/docs/standing-rules/sr-03-08.md)), InterPride is an international, multilingual organization representing 70+ countries. Under [SR 04.02 § 11](file:///Users/chris/Developer/interpride/governance/docs/standing-rules/sr-04-02.md), [SR 03.08 § 2](file:///Users/chris/Developer/interpride/governance/docs/standing-rules/sr-03-08.md), and the *InterPride Anti-Racism Statement*, presenters are **not** restricted to speaking only English; rather, the organization is committed to linguistic accessibility and de-centering monolingual dominance.
2. **Real-Time Interpretation Infrastructure is Deployed:** To guarantee universal comprehension across linguistic barriers, InterPride provided real-time AI audio interpretation and live captioning for all sessions via **Wordly.ai** (including dedicated links for every workshop and caucus room).
3. **Procedural Breakdown & Remedy:** The issue arose not from an impermissible presentation language, but from a failure of on-site communication/facilitation to ensure the delegate had access to the live Wordly.ai interpretation stream. The remedy is technical and reconciliatory, not restrictive of presenters' native language expression.

## 3. Operational Infrastructure in Place: Wordly.ai

For the 2026 GM&WC in Phuket, InterPride specifically contracted and launched real-time, bidirectional interpretation through **Wordly.ai** across plenary and breakout tracks:
* Documented in: [`Wordly.Ai Access Links for Friday`](file:///Users/chris/Developer/interpride/governance/gmwc/2026/reference/wordly_links.txt)
* Covers all breakout rooms, caucuses, and plenary halls with mobile-accessible QR codes and direct links.
* Any attendee can listen in English (or read real-time captions) via their own mobile device or laptop in any room, regardless of the spoken language of the speaker.

## 4. Action Plan & Operational Directives

1. **Reconciliation & Outreach to the Affected Delegate:**
   * An Ombudsperson or Conference Co-Chair should connect directly with the delegate who left the session.
   * Apologize that they experienced friction or confusion regarding translation access.
   * Verify that they have the active **Wordly.ai** links ([`wordly_links.txt`](file:///Users/chris/Developer/interpride/governance/gmwc/2026/reference/wordly_links.txt)) and assist them in setting up live audio translation / text captions on their phone.

2. **Facilitator Protocol for All Breakout Rooms & Caucuses:**
   * At the beginning of each session, facilitators must verbally announce and display:
     > *"Presentations in this session may be in English, Thai, Spanish, French, or other languages. Real-time translation into English and over 30 languages is live right now via Wordly.ai. Please scan the QR code by the door or use the link in the conference app to listen or read live captions."*
   * In-room tech and accessibility volunteers must assist delegates needing help launching the translation stream.

3. **Protection of Presenters:**
   * Confirm to presenters and hosts that they remain fully entitled and supported to deliver presentations in their primary language.
