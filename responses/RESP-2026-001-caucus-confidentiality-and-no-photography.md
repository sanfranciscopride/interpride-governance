## 1. Executive Overview / Narrative Summary

During General Meeting & World Conference (GM&WC) sessions—specifically during identity-based and thematic member caucuses—questions have arisen regarding the confidentiality of caucus proceedings and the taking of unauthorized photographs, video, or digital recordings.

**Official Determination:**  
Under InterPride's governing framework, identity-based closed caucuses are strictly confidential deliberative spaces. **Unauthorized photography, videography, audio recording, and livestreaming within any closed caucus are strictly prohibited.** 

Because InterPride’s membership includes delegates and grassroots activists from sovereign nations and regions where LGBTQIA+ identities and Pride advocacy are criminalized, non-consensual photography or breach of caucus room confidentiality creates immediate, severe personal safety and legal liabilities. Caucus facilitators hold plenary-backed authority to eject non-members, command the immediate cessation of any recording, and escalate non-compliance to the Conference Ombudspersons and the Board of Directors for disciplinary action under the Code of Ethics and Conduct.

## 3. Legal & Procedural Analysis

1. **Safety and Criminalization Risks in International Assemblies:**  
   Unlike regional pride conferences held in jurisdictions with full civil rights protections, the InterPride GM&WC brings together delegates from across 70+ nations, including regions where anti-sodomy statutes, "anti-propaganda" laws, and violent state or non-state persecution are actively enforced. Capturing identifiable photographic or video likenesses within closed caucuses (e.g., Trans, BIPOC, Small Prides, Kink/Fetish) presents an existential safety and asylum risk to delegates upon their return to home countries.

2. **Chatham House Standard:**  
   By established conference practice and the provisions of the *Caucus Facilitation Guide*, discussions within closed caucuses are privileged under the Chatham House Rule: attendees are free to bring general issues, motions, and collective report-outs to Plenary 2, but neither the identity nor the affiliation of the speakers or attendees may be disclosed outside the room without explicit affirmative consent.

3. **Facilitator Primacy & Enforcement Authority:**  
   Facilitators do not simply moderate discussions; they are officers of the space charged by the Conference Committee to protect the caucus perimeter. If an attendee refuses to delete an unconsented photograph or refuses to leave a closed space, the facilitator is empowered to pause proceedings and summon Conference Security or the Ombudsperson.

---

## 4. Directives & Action Plan

1. **Immediate Facilitator Action:**
   * At the commencement of every caucus session, the facilitator must read the room boundary rule:
     > *"Welcome to the [Name] Caucus. This is a closed, confidential space for individuals who self-identify as part of this community. No photography, videography, or audio recording is permitted at any time. If you do not identify with this caucus, or if you cannot abide by these confidentiality rules, please self-select out now."*
2. **Immediate Handling of an Unauthorized Photograph/Recording:**
   * Facilitators must immediately halt discussion.
   * Require the individual who took the photograph or recording to delete the media in the presence of the room or tech/accessibility volunteer.
   * If non-consensual recording was intentional or resisted, the individual must be instructed to leave the caucus immediately.
3. **Escalation Path:**
   * **Level 1 (In-Room):** Facilitator / Volunteer Door Monitor instructs removal.
   * **Level 2 (Conference Security / Ombudsperson):** Facilitator contacts the **Accessibility, Safety, & Wellness Work Group** and Conference Ombudspersons for formal Code of Conduct escalation.
   * **Level 3 (Board Sanction):** Revocation of delegate credentials for severe or repeated violations pursuant to SR 08.04.
