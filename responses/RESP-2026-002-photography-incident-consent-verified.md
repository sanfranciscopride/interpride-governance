## 1. Executive Summary & Case Disposition

A matter was referred to the Governance Committee regarding photography occurring during a conference caucus / workshop space. 

**Official Finding & Disposition:**  
Following direct verification by both the **In-Room Facilitator** and the **Conference Committee Co-Chairs**, it has been confirmed that:
1. The photographer captured images **solely of individuals who provided prior, explicit affirmative consent**.
2. No non-consenting delegates, background participants, or protected attendees were captured in photo or video media.
3. No breach of closed-space confidentiality or organizational data protection occurred.

**Determination:**  
The photographer’s conduct was **fully compliant** with InterPride’s Code of Ethics, participant consent requirements, and conference safety standards. **The matter is officially closed without disciplinary or corrective action.**

---

## 2. Governing Authorities & Citations

### A. Consensual Media & Individual Autonomy
* **Citation:** *InterPride Standing Rules*, **SR 08.04** (*Code of Ethics and Conduct*), Section 1 (*Core Principles*):
  * Prohibits non-consensual conduct, harassment, and unauthorized disclosure of personal identity. Where affirmative consent is actively sought and granted by consenting adults, no violation exists.
* **Citation:** *2026 GM&WC Policy & Procedures*, § 1.2 (*Safety and Care*):
  * Requires that activities prioritize participant safety. Where participants voluntarily choose to be photographed with informed consent, their autonomy is respected while surrounding space privacy is preserved.

### B. Verification Authority of Facilitator & Conference Leadership
* **Citation:** *Caucus Facilitation Guide (GM&WC)*, § *Your Role as Facilitator*, line 23:
  * Facilitators are the designated on-site authorities responsible for space management and room integrity. Confirmation by the Facilitator and Conference Committee Chairs serves as conclusive evidentiary verification.
* **Citation:** *InterPride Matrix of Confidentiality (April 2024)*, Item 3:
  * Confirms the Governance Committee's role in verifying compliance with organizational confidentiality parameters.

## 3. Operational Guidance & Closing Directives

To maintain absolute comfort and transparency across all conference spaces following this inquiry:

1. **Facilitator Floor Clarification (Optional / If Needed):**  
   If attendees in the session experienced concern or confusion regarding the camera presence, the facilitator may make a brief standard announcement:
   > *"The Conference Committee and Facilitator have reviewed the photography inquiry and confirmed that only consenting individuals were photographed, and all caucus privacy rules remain strictly honored. No unauthorized or non-consensual photos were taken."*

2. **Media Handling Protocol:**  
   The photographer and communications team should ensure that before any images from the conference are published to official channels (e.g., Sched, social media, or website screens pursuant to GM&WC Notice #4), media passes a secondary check ensuring no non-consenting background delegates are visible.

3. **Record Disposition:**  
   This memorandum officially documents the verification and fully resolves the inquiry in the governance record.
