# INTERPRIDE BYLAW AMENDMENT PROPOSAL

**Document Type:** Formal Bylaw Amendment Proposal  
**Subject:** Board of Directors — Term of Office Length (Transition from Three to Four Years)  
**Target Governing Provision:** [InterPride Bylaws, Article V, Section 15(D)](file:///Users/chris/Developer/interpride/governance/docs/bylaws/article-05.md)  
**Proposed By:** The Board of Directors of InterPride  
**Lead Sponsor:** JayJay Potter-Peachey, Vice President of Member Services  
**Supporting Documentation:** Letter of Support from External Sponsor (Pride 365)  
**Submission Deadline:** October 30, 2026, 17:00 ICT (Phuket) / 10:00 GMT  

---

## 1. Title of the Amendment

**Bylaw Amendment 2026-01: Amendment to Article V, Section 15 to Establish Four-Year Terms of Office for Board Members**

---

## 2. Preamble & Justification (WHEREAS Clauses)

**WHEREAS**, the global Pride movement requires robust, sustainable organizational continuity, institutional memory, and strategic alignment to navigate complex international advocacy, human rights challenges, and worldwide conference cycles; and

**WHEREAS**, InterPride's current three (3)-year term of office for Board members results in frequent turnover and shortened strategic execution windows, creating governance instability and disproportionate onboarding overhead for newly seated directors; and

**WHEREAS**, major external partners, philanthropic bodies, and international corporate sponsors (including attested corporate and civil society partners such as Pride 365) establish multi-year funding agreements contingent upon consistent relationship-building, stable institutional leadership, and leadership longevity; and

**WHEREAS**, extending the length of service for full Board members from three (3) years to four (4) years directly addresses continuity gaps, enhances strategic follow-through on multi-year initiatives (such as WorldPride and global grant allocations), and bolsters external sponsorship retention; and

**WHEREAS**, [Article XII, Section 1 of the InterPride Bylaws](file:///Users/chris/Developer/interpride/governance/docs/bylaws/article-12.md) explicitly authorizes the Board of Directors, upon a majority vote of those members present and voting, to propose amendments to the Bylaws directly to the Membership;

---

## 3. Operative Language (BE IT RESOLVED)

**THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT**, the Membership of InterPride amends **Article V, Section 15, Subsection D** of the InterPride Bylaws by striking the word "three (3)" and replacing it with the word "four (4)", to read as follows:

> **ARTICLE V – BOARD OF DIRECTORS**  
> **SECTION 15 – TERM OF OFFICE**  
> 
> *A. The incoming Officers' terms commence thirty (30) days after the General Meeting & World Conference (GM&WC) at which they were elected. The outgoing officers' terms conclude 30 days after GM&WC.*  
> *B. The election of officers shall be staggered over two GMs at each GM&WC.*  
> *C. No Officer shall serve more than two (2) consecutive elected terms in the same Office on the Board.*  
> **D. All Board positions are elected for four (4)-year terms.**

---

## 4. Implementation Plan & Effective Date

Pursuant to [Article XII, Section 2 of the InterPride Bylaws](file:///Users/chris/Developer/interpride/governance/docs/bylaws/article-12.md), all proposed Bylaw amendments must include an associated Implementation Plan:

1. **Effective Date:**
   * This amendment shall take effect beginning with the Board of Directors elections conducted at the **2027 General Meeting & World Conference (GM&WC)**.
2. **Treatment of Incumbent Board Members:**
   * Directors and Officers elected prior to the 2027 GM&WC (including those elected or seated in 2024, 2025, and 2026) shall serve out the remainder of their existing three (3)-year terms.
   * Beginning with the 2027 GM&WC elections, all newly elected Board positions shall be elected to four (4)-year terms.
3. **Staggering Schedule Adjustment:**
   * The Governance Committee, in consultation with the Verification Team and the Board of Directors, shall update the election cycle rotation schedule prior to the 2027 call for nominations to ensure even staggering of executive officer seats across odd- and even-year election cycles.
4. **Member Voting Thresholds:**
   * In accordance with Article XII, Section 2, adoption requires:
     1. An affirmative majority vote of Full Member Organizations present and voting at the 2026 GM&WC Plenary; and
     2. A two-thirds (2/3) supermajority affirmative vote of the Membership via electronic ballot conducted within sixty (60) days following the GM&WC.
