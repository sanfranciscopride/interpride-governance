## 1. Executive Summary & Affirmative Confirmation

A formal inquiry/comment was submitted to conference leadership:
> *"Can the conference leadership team confirm that neither world pride bidding cities recieved any special treatment as both are involved with the conference with one in a leadership role and the other the conference host city"*

### **Official Confirmation & Finding:**
The Conference Leadership Team and the Governance Committee **officially and categorically confirm that neither WorldPride bidding organization has received, nor will receive, any special treatment, procedural advantage, or preferential status.**

Both candidate cities (**Bangkok Pride** and **Pride Barcelona**):
1. Have been subjected to identical vetting standards, timelines, and technical application evaluations by the independent WorldPride Committee pursuant to [SR 06.01](file:///Users/chris/Developer/interpride/governance/docs/standing-rules/sr-06-01.md).
2. Are allocated **exactly identical presentation time, Q&A slots, and stage resources** during [Plenary 2](file:///Users/chris/Developer/interpride/governance/plenary2_agenda.txt) (strictly 20 minutes of presentation + 10 minutes of Q&A each).
3. Operate under strict **Conflict of Interest & Recusal firewalls** ([SR 08.10](file:///Users/chris/Developer/interpride/governance/docs/standing-rules/sr-08-10.md)). No individual affiliated with either bid has any role in administering, tallying, or overseeing the member vote.
4. **Active Operational Firewall Enacted:** Conference Committee leadership proactively directed conflicted conference committee members affiliated with bidding cities (including Andoni Ibáñez and Ann) to completely step aside from conference operations, backstage access, and technical controls yesterday and today, ensuring zero access to conference modifications or unfair advantages.
5. The final selection of the WorldPride title is decided **exclusively by secret electronic ballot of the general membership**, completely insulated from conference organizers or board leadership.

## 2. Governing Authorities & Citations

### A. Equal Application Presentation Standards
* **Citation:** *InterPride Standing Rules*, **SR 06.01** (*WorldPride Applications and Licensure*), Section 7.A–D:
  > *"A. Each applicant organization shall be provided an opportunity to make a presentation regarding their application for the WorldPride license during the GM & WC. This presentation can be in person, or virtually.*  
  > *B. Applicants will be allocated up to 30 minutes for presentation including questions and answers.*  
  > *C. The applicant organization is responsible for coordinating any technical needs they might have for this presentation with the GM&WC’s Host Committee.*  
  > *D. Presentations shall be given in English. Simultaneous translation shall be made available by the GM&WC host."*
* **Application to Plenary 2 Agenda:**  
  As codified in the adopted [Plenary 2 Agenda](file:///Users/chris/Developer/interpride/governance/plenary2_agenda.txt), Items 7 and 8 provide mathematically identical time allocations:
  * **Bangkok Pride:** 20 minutes presentation + 10 minutes Q&A (Total: 30 minutes)
  * **Pride Barcelona:** 20 minutes presentation + 10 minutes Q&A (Total: 30 minutes)
  * Both presentations are facilitated independently by WorldPride Committee Co-Chairs (Gert Claassen-Smit and Steve Taylor), who are not affiliated with either candidate city.

### B. Factual Recusal & Operational Firewall Enacted by Conference Committee
* **Direct Factual Confirmation:**  
  The Conference Committee Lead has directly confirmed that an operational firewall was proactively established prior to the plenary sessions:
  - Key conference committee members affiliated with the WorldPride bids (specifically **Andoni Ibáñez** and **Ann** with Pride Barcelona) were **explicitly instructed to step away from all conference operations, back-of-house logistics, and committee access** for yesterday and today.
  - The non-conflicted Conference Committee co-chairs assumed sole operational command over conference proceedings, ensuring bid personnel have **zero access to conference modifications, technical advantages, or scheduling changes**, guaranteeing absolute parity in treatment and time.

### C. Conflict of Interest & Recusal Standards
* **Citation:** *InterPride Standing Rules*, **SR 08.10** (*Conflict of Interest Policy*), Section B & Section D:
  > *"A conflict of interest is a situation or matter in which an officer has an interest that may compromise their obligations to the organisation or allow them to derive personal benefit from decisions made in their official capacity... Actions may include: Recusal from votes or discussions; Formal documentation of mitigation actions..."*
* **Structural Safeguards:**
  * **Local Host (Bangkok / Thailand Host entities):** While local Thai organizers are supporting the logistical delivery of the GM&WC in Phuket, host responsibilities are governed by the tripartite MOU and [SR 04.02](file:///Users/chris/Developer/interpride/governance/docs/standing-rules/sr-04-02.md). Host personnel have zero jurisdiction over the WorldPride bid evaluation or plenary floor procedures.
  * **Barcelona Affiliations:** Any InterPride officer, committee member, or delegate affiliated with Pride Barcelona is strictly recused under SR 08.10 from any administrative handling of the WorldPride election process.

### D. Sovereign Democratic Prerogative of the Membership
* **Citation:** *InterPride Standing Rules*, **SR 06.01**, Purpose & Section 8.A:
  > *"The WorldPride title is granted by the membership.*  
  > *An application for the WorldPride title shall be provisionally granted to an applicant organization should two-thirds (2/3) of the full member organizations present and voting at the GM&WC at which their application is presented, vote in favor of its acceptance."*
* **Election Integrity:** Neither conference chairs nor the Board of Directors can award, influence, or veto a WorldPride bid. Voting takes place via independent, secure electronic voting (ElectionRunner) managed by the independent Verification Team and Secretary.
